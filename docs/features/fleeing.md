Monsters fleeing off the map edge: the `flee` step, the GM's choice at the edge (Extend map or Let it get away), escaped tokens and Bring back.

## Decisions
- **Only monsters flee in a plan (0.18.0):** a party member the GM plays, or an NPC on the party's side, is refused a flee step (`checkPlan`'s `party`, checked before the route, so the refusal names this reason and not the distance: seen in the 0.18.0 live test); hiding it and "XP not earned" are for monsters. Its retreat is the GM's call.
Designed with Rhea one question at a time on 2026-11-08; she chose the recommended option every time. Ten decisions:
1. **Who decides at the edge: the GM, every time.** The turn stops (on Auto too, the same way it stops for an ability's card) and the Monster Turns card shows **Extend map** and **Let it get away**.
2. **What counts as reaching the edge: a plan step `flee`, planned by the thinker.** It is checked like a Stride: the monster must be able to reach a square on the outermost ring of the map with the actions it has left. Once it stands there the turn stops and the buttons appear. Ending an ordinary Stride on the ring triggers nothing.
3. **Let it get away:** the monster leaves the fight (its combatant is removed), its token stays on the scene, **hidden and flagged `escaped`**, with HP and conditions kept. Nothing is deleted.
4. **Threat and XP:** the encounter's threat stays as built. The escape is noted on the card and in the history window ("escaped: 1 Dire Wolf (30 XP not earned)"). Awarding XP for it is the GM's call at the end.
5. **After Extend map:** the monster keeps its turn. Once the Survey Sheet's redraw has settled it gets a fresh packet and the thinker plans the remaining actions (keep running or turn and fight). Nothing is forced.
6. **Which side grows:** one **Extend map** button per side the monster stands on, labelled with the side; at a corner two buttons. If Extend isn't possible the button is greyed out with the reason, and **Let it get away** stays available.
7. **When the thinker plans to flee:** its judgment (badly hurt, outnumbered, its leader down), with a `why` like any step, so the reason shows in the history. A monster with the **Fleeing** condition must spend its actions fleeing, as the rules say; the prompt (version 13) says so.
8. **Bringing one back:** an **Escaped** section in the Monster Turns window with a **Bring back** button per monster: unhide the token, add it to the combat, roll initiative as for any monster joining a fight. It lands where its token is now (the GM can move it first). The history keeps the escape line and adds a "brought back" line.
9. **Too far from the edge to arrive this turn:** the thinker runs toward the nearest edge with ordinary Strides (already legal) and plans `flee` once the edge is within what its remaining actions can reach. A Fleeing monster must move away from the source; checked the same way.
10. **What players see:** the token has already stood on the edge square; when the GM presses Let it get away the module hides it, so the table watches it run off and vanish. **No chat message** (her standing rule: no whispered or extra chat cards). The card and history carry the record.
11. **End Encounter leaves escaped tokens hidden (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked whether escaped tokens should be unhidden when the encounter ends, she answered "stay hidden". No change: they keep their hidden state and flag.
12. **A whole band running: the first prompt offers both (Rhea, 2026-11-11, R3-13).** When a member of a band reaches the edge while others of its band are still in the fight, the card has **Let this one go** and **Let the whole band go**; after the second, the rest leave without stopping as each reaches the edge; after the first, the next member asks again. A monster fleeing alone (in no band, or the last of its band) asks as before with **Let it get away** (decision 1 stands for single monsters). She chose this over a prompt for every monster and over one silent choice for the band. Settled by Angel: bringing one of them back takes the band's leave away, so the others ask again.

Build choices (Angel, 2026-11-08, within the design; Rhea can change them):
- **The outermost ring is read from the scene's current bounds** (`scene.dimensions` ÷ grid size), never a stored size, so an extended map has new edges at once.
- **A `flee` must be the plan's last step**, like Seek: at the edge the GM decides, and if the map grows the thinker is asked again anyway. A flee with actions left after it leaves them for that re-ask.
- **Extend grows the map by 10 squares**, the Survey Sheet's own default, and the button is greyed when that would pass the Survey Sheet's 120-square limit on that side, when the Survey Sheet is off, or when the scene isn't an encounter map.
- **The GM's press asks the runner** (the flag `extend: side`), as Do it does: the extension and the fresh packet need the runner's page on the fight scene. Any GM may press Let it get away and Bring back, which only change documents.
- **Bring back's initiative** is `combat.rollInitiative` on the new combatant, which PF2e rolls with the creature's own initiative statistic.
- **XP not earned** is PF2e's own `calculateXP` for one creature of the monster's level against the party's level (the Build window's suggested level) and the party size setting.

## How it works
- **A whole band running (0.26.0):** `bandStillFighting(combat, tokenId)` counts the other members of the monster's band still in the tracker and not defeated or dead; with at least one, the card's button reads **Let this one go** and a second, **Let the whole band go**, calls `letBandGo`: it writes `bandGo.<band key>` on the Combat's turns flag and lets this one go. In `runTurn`, a flee that reaches its square saves `fled` as before and then `leaveWithBand` lets the monster go at once when its band has that leave (on the runner's page, which is a GM's), so the turn doesn't stop, on Auto or not; its note says it got away with its band. `bringBack` of a member takes the leave away. The leave is on the Combat, so it ends with the fight. When an extension shifts the squares' numbers, `afterExtend` also moves the bands' rally spots (`docs/features/bands.md`).
- **A runner reload during an extension (0.25.0):** the request keeps the map's size from before (`extendFrom`). After the reload a map that grew is treated as extended (the thinker is asked again, squares shifted as usual); one that didn't goes back to `fled` with the buttons and a note that the page reloaded and the map didn't grow. The Survey Sheet's job runs on the runner's page and dies with it, so offering Extend again there can't grow the map twice. Before 0.25.0 the request was closed as extended either way (seen live in the 0.25.0 close-out: the card said the map was extended when it wasn't, and with no actions left the monster stood on the old edge).
- `turn.mjs`: `flee` is in `COSTS`, `MOVES` and `WORD`; `edgeSides(square, size, cols, rows)` says which edges a creature's space touches. In `checkPlan` a flee goes through the move branch (prone, Speed, route and cost like a Stride), then needs `ctx.edgeAt(to, size)` to return at least one side, and sets `fled` so a later step is refused. The resolved step carries `sides`. `describeStep` says so on the card; `doneStep` gives the thinker "reached the map's edge".
- `fight.mjs`: `gather` passes `edgeAt` from `mapSquares(scene)`. In `runTurn`, after `runStride` moved it: short of its square → the usual "stopped" re-plan (also for a flee, though it is the last step); at its square → the request is saved `state: "fled"` with `fled: { sides, at }` and the loop ends. `endAfter` isn't reached, so Auto never ends a fled turn. The stall check treats `fled` as waiting on a person and `extending` as machine work.
- `letGo`: writes the escape record (`escaped` on the Combat flag, by request id) first (keyed by the request, so a monster that escapes twice keeps both records, and a second GM's press finds it and stops), then saves `escaped: { name, xp, at }` and a note on the request (the card's leftover note shows it once the turn has passed), hides the token with `flags.<id>.escaped`, then deletes its combatant: core's own `Combatant._preDeleteOperation` hands the turn to the next survivor with turn events, and since 0.24.0 the runner's `onTurnHandedOn` notices that turn (core sends no `updateCombat` with `turn` for it; before 0.24.0 a ticked monster next in the order was never asked: found by the 0.24.0 close-out pass, seen twice).
- `extendMap`: writes `extend: side` on the request (the card then reads "Asking <runner> to extend the map…", with Let it get away still offered); the runner's `act` sees `fled` with `extend` and runs `runExtend`: `state: "extending"` (busy, no buttons), the Survey Sheet's `extendEncounter(scene, side, 10)` within 180 s, then waits for this page's canvas to be back on the fight scene with the token drawn, settles, and `afterExtend` saves `extended: side`, shifts every remembered square (the `memory` flag) by the Survey Sheet's returned pixel shift (a north or west extension moves the content), clears the fleeing token's movement history if the shift was recorded as movement, and calls `replan` with the change `{ what: "extended", side }`, not counted against the re-plan limit; the request stays `extending` until `replan` closes it, so a Let it get away can't slip in between. The old request ends "done"; the new one carries the done steps (the flee as "reached the map's edge") and the actions left. The change's words tell the thinker that square numbers shifted. A Survey Sheet timeout isn't offered as a retry (its drawing goes on after the timeout and a second press would grow the map twice): it carries on as if it finished. A runner reload mid-extend: `onRunnerReady` runs `afterExtend` with no shift (whether or not the map grew, asking again is right; remembered squares may then be off by the strip).
- `escapedList` / `bringBack`: escaped tokens are those on the fight's scene with an `escaped` flag naming this combat and no combatant. Bring back unhides, removes the flag, creates the combatant with `sceneId`, rolls its initiative with `skipDialog: true` (PF2e posts that roll to chat, as for any monster joining) and writes `returned` on the latest open escape record for that token.
- Elsewhere: `leaveWaiting` closes a `fled` request whose turn moved on without a press ("The turn moved on while it stood at the map's edge."); `runStride` doesn't treat an escaped token as an unknown creature on the route; `buildPacket` leaves hidden allies (escaped or GM-hidden) out of the packet; the stall check gives `extending` 240 s.
- `ui/turns.mjs`: state words for `fled` and `extending`; in `fled` the buttons Extend map <side> (one per side, disabled with the reason as its title, the reason also written on the card) and Let it get away, with Play it myself still there; an **Escaped** section below the card with Bring back per token. Presses are noted for the history.
- `history.mjs`: a request with `escaped` gets the line "Escaped: <name> got away (<xp> XP not earned)." (its note is not repeated); `extended` a line that the map was extended; `fled` "At the map's edge, waiting for you." A returned monster gets a "brought back into the fight" line in the round it returned (`t.escaped[tokenId].returned`), in the window and the journal.
- `brain.mjs` / `prompt.mjs`: `flee` is a known step kind; the prompt explains the step, when to use it, the GM's choice, the re-ask after Extend, running toward the edge first, and the fleeing condition.

## Traps
- **Test trap: the dungeon test scene's middle has no reachable edge** (0.20.1 close-out): from the Sod Hound's square no Stride then flee reaches the outer ring, so test flees on an outdoor map.
- **A hidden token is still a creature to the move code (review, 2026-11-08):** `runStride` cut every later Stride short at an escaped token's square (it was "someone it didn't know was there"), and `buildPacket` listed it among the allies with its square and health. Both now skip escaped and hidden allies; `knownTo` already did.
- **Don't pass through a button state while finishing work:** going back to `fled` for a moment before `replan` closed the request let a Let it get away orphan a fresh request for a monster that had left the fight. The request stays `extending` until `replan` closes it.
- **Don't advance the turn before deleting the current combatant (live, 2026-11-08):** `nextTurn()` then `deleteEmbeddedDocuments` skipped the next creature's turn (round 1 → 2 with Test Hero's turn lost). Core moves the turn on by itself when the current combatant is deleted; the module just deletes.
- **Rolling initiative by script opens PF2e's roll dialog (live, 2026-11-08):** `rollInitiative` hung on the AI page waiting for it. Pass `skipDialog: true`; it goes through to the statistic roll.
- **A timeout on another module's job doesn't stop the job:** offering Extend again after a Survey Sheet timeout would have grown the map twice.

## Open items
- "Let the whole band go" makes nobody run: each member still flees by its own mind, and one that fights on stays. Nothing tells the thinker the band was let go.
- After a reload that didn't grow the map and a second Extend that did, the first note ("...the map didn't grow...") stays on the finished request and can still be read in the history (seen live on 0.25.0).
- A Fleeing monster with no reachable edge: the prompt tells the thinker to Stride away from the source; the checker doesn't enforce "away from the source".
- The thinker gets no hint that the edge is in reach beyond the map's size and its square; it works it out.
- Extend's effect on other hidden tokens: the Survey Sheet shifts every token, hidden ones too; not yet watched live.
- Each escaped token's flag names its combat, so a later fight doesn't list it (the window shows no fight after End Encounter, so Bring back is unreachable then).
- Players' reactions as it flees are asked like any Stride's (0.20.0, `docs/features/reactions.md`).
- A Stride's diagonal count for the rest of the turn is lost when an extension's shift was recorded as movement (the history is cleared): a 5 ft difference at most.
