Hide, Sneak and Seek: the module presses Visioner's buttons on PF2e's secret card; what the monster knows of its own stealth; when hiding ends.

## Decisions
- **Hide, Sneak, Seek (2026-11-07):** **the module presses Visioner's own buttons** on PF2e's secret card ("if it works, then I don't need to press them"), so Visioner decides; the GM's card only if they aren't there. **The monster knows (B):** what it tried, the light where it stands, its own concealment, who it has standard cover from; never whether the secret roll worked. **Hiding ends (A):** observed just before any action but Hide, Sneak or Step, and right after an attack (off-guard kept for that attack, Visioner's Attack Consequences pressed). **Seek's size, Angel's default** (Rhea had no preference): a 15 ft burst at a corner within 30 ft, or a 30 ft cone; only creatures inside are checked; always the plan's last step, asked again after (not counted as a re-plan). A Sneak moves at most half Speed and needs no proof it started hidden (the monster can't know).
**Design-era decisions (from the 2026-11-05/06 plan; the lines above are the skill's summary of the same choices):**
- **Hide, Sneak, Seek for monsters (built 0.8.0 and 0.8.1 and tested live, 2026-11-07; history in `claude/lythara-encounter-builder.md`, how it works in the repo's DEVELOPING.md; decided by Rhea, 2026-11-07: "if it works, then I don't need to press them"):** each uses PF2e's own action, which posts its secret card with Visioner's buttons; **the module presses Visioner's own buttons on the runner's page** (Apply Changes; Start Sneak before a Sneak's move; Attack Consequences when a hidden monster Strikes), so Visioner alone decides who it's hidden from, prerequisites, cover bonus and off-guard, and the GM can still Revert from the card. **If Visioner's buttons aren't there** (Visioner off, or an update changed its card), the turn stops at Next step and the card asks the GM to do it (the earlier first version, kept as the fallback). Read in Visioner 8.14.17's code (`chat/ui/event-binder.js`, `apply-service.js`): the card's `data-action` buttons `apply-now-hide`, `apply-now-seek`, `start-sneak`, `apply-now-sneak`, `apply-now-consequences` run the whole flow. Proposed steps: `{ do: "hide" }` (needs cover or concealment from someone who sees it), `{ do: "sneak", to }` (half Speed, Stride's route and checks, must start hidden from or undetected by someone), `{ do: "seek", at }` 15 ft burst or `{ do: "seek", toward }` 30 ft cone (area aiming reused); Seek always ends the plan and asks again. **Seek's size (Angel's default, 2026-11-07; Rhea had no preference, "not familiar with those rules"):** a 30 ft cone from the monster or a 15 ft burst centred within 30 ft, the older printed rule; the remaster leaves it to the GM, about 30 ft or less ([Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2301)). Only creatures inside the area are checked, whatever Visioner's own range setting. Easy to change if it plays badly. **Ending hiding (Rhea chose A, 2026-11-07):** a hidden or undetected monster stays so only while it Hides, Sneaks or Steps (rules: Hide, Sneak). Just before any other action, the module makes it observed by everyone it was hidden from or undetected by, and says so on the card; an attack keeps its off-guard for that attack and reveals it after (Visioner's Attack Consequences where its card has it). The GM can hide it again in Visioner's manager to allow a quiet action. Rejected: B, leave it to the GM with a note. **What the monster knows of its own stealth (Rhea chose B, 2026-11-07):** the packet says what it did ("you Hid last action", "you're Sneaking") and, for each enemy it perceives, whether it has cover or concealment from them; **never whether the secret roll worked** (not who it's hidden from), closest to the secret-roll rule and "acts only on what it perceives". Rejected: A, the result (who it's hidden from), and C, nothing extra. **Probed live 2026-11-07 on the Bridge's page (Claude Test Scene, a test wall with `coverOverride` standard, all undone):** Hide: PF2e's `hide` action's card (whispered to GMs) carries Visioner's `apply-now-hide`; a click on the chat log's copy made the Barrow Wight hidden from Test Hero only (the one it had cover from), no dialog, the button became Revert. Seek: the card shows `apply-now-seek` only once its message has Visioner's flag `seekTemplate` `{ center, radiusFeet, templateType, hasTargets: true }`; with a 15 ft circle on Test Hero, Test Hero went hidden to observed and Valeros outside stayed hidden, so **the module limits Seek by writing Visioner's own area flag** (Visioner tests each token's centre, a circle by straight distance; a cone is `templateType: "cone"` with `geometry { direction, angle }`, not yet tried). Sneak: `start-sneak` then `apply-now-sneak` work from code. **Visioner bug found:** with its automatic system off, Start Sneak records every creature's start as hidden, because it calls `TokenDocument#canObserve`, which Foundry 14 doesn't have (`typeof` undefined), so a Sneak can make the sneaker hidden from or undetected by creatures that saw it (Valeros went observed to hidden). It hits players' Sneaks too, in both worlds. The module corrects the card's `sneakStartStates` from Visioner's own stored states before the move.

## How it works
- **Burrow (0.16.0):** a Burrow isn't Hide, Sneak or Step, so a hidden monster comes out of hiding just before it (`actWords` says "burrowing"); below ground it is hidden from everyone anyway.
- **Climb and Release come out of hiding like any other action (0.15.0):** `actWords` says "its Climb" or "letting go of what it holds". Release is free and doesn't trigger reactions, but Hide's rule ends hiding on any action other than Hide, Sneak or Step.
- **Interact comes out of hiding like any other action (0.12.0);** a Delay is no action at all and keeps it hidden (`KEEPS_HIDDEN`); `actWords` says "its Interact" on the card.
- **Hide, Sneak and Seek (Rhea, 2026-11-07).** Steps `{ do: "hide" }`, `{ do: "sneak", to }`, `{ do: "seek", at }` (a 15-ft burst at a grid corner within 30 ft) or `{ do: "seek", toward }` (a 30-ft cone; sizes are Angel's default from the older printed rule, `SEEK`). Rules: Hide https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=62, Sneak ID 63, Seek ID 2301.
  - **Visioner decides, the module presses its buttons.** PF2e's own action (`game.pf2e.actions.get(kind).use`) posts the secret card (whispered to GMs, context type `skill-check`); Visioner adds its panel on GM pages, and `runStealth` presses its buttons in the runner's chat log (`apply-now-hide`, `apply-now-seek`, `start-sneak` then `apply-now-sneak`), as a GM's click would: Visioner works out each creature's prerequisite, the cover bonus and the off-guard, and the button turns into Revert. `applyOnCard` reads the answer from **the pressed button itself**: Visioner sets its `processing` class synchronously on the click (so a press can't run twice) and takes it off when done, turning only that button into Revert (`updateButtonToRevert`); still Apply means it found nothing to change ("No changes to apply"). Never read the card again for it: any redraw of the message (the module's own Seek flag, Start Sneak's writes, Toolbelt) gives a fresh panel that always shows Apply, because Visioner's panel builders never draw Revert (found by the review, 2026-11-07). For the same reason a change to the card is followed by `redrawn` (wait for the chat log's new copy) before a button is looked for, and after a reload the record's `check` usually can't tell and asks the GM. **Pressing (`press`, `freshButton`), found live 2026-11-07:** Visioner draws its panel with the card and binds its jQuery click handler a moment later, with what the page's canvas shows then. A press before the handler is bound does nothing (Start Sneak pressed right after the roll), and a panel drawn while the runner viewed another scene (after a reload, before `ensureScene`) finds no token and answers "No changes to apply" for a Hide that should have worked. So every press first draws the card again (`ui.chat.updateMessage`, with the fight's map open), waits for the panel's click handler (`jQuery._data(panel, "events").click`), and counts only if Visioner took it (the button is `processing` straight after the click); one it didn't take changed nothing and is tried again. Live: Hide applied as "none" after a reload until the card was redrawn; a Seek's card is a `perception-check`, Hide's and Sneak's `skill-check`, all whispered to the GMs (players see "privately rolled some dice"). Visioner's Hide also hides the monster from its own allies when its "ignore allies" setting is off, so `comeOut` reveals it to every creature, allies included. If the buttons aren't there (Visioner off, or an update changed its markup) the step goes to the GM (`gm`, the turn waits at Next step with a note saying what to press).
  - **A Seek's area:** Visioner checks every creature on the map unless the card names an area, so the module writes Visioner's own `flags.pf2e-visioner.seekTemplate` on the card before pressing Apply (`seekArea`: a circle at the grid corner, or a cone from the monster's centre toward the square's centre, 90°, with `hasTargets: true`, without which Visioner shows no Apply button on a card that has the flag). Visioner tests each token's centre, a circle by straight-line distance and a cone by angle from the monster's centre, so the area is an approximation of the rules': a square a burst's 5-10 count leaves out can be in, and a Large creature with only part of its space inside is out. Tested live 2026-11-07: a creature in the burst was found, one outside stayed hidden. **A Seek is always the plan's last step** (`checkPlan`), and the runner re-plans after it with the change `sought`, so the thinker plans the rest with what it found (or the turn ends if no actions are left). That re-ask doesn't count toward `MAX_REPLANS` (`replan(..., { counted: false })`), and nothing is saved between the Seek's record and the re-ask, so a reload there reaches the re-ask again.
  - **A Sneak's start:** Visioner's Start Sneak records each creature's start as `observer.document.canObserve(...) ? "observed" : "hidden"` when its automatic system is off, and Foundry 14 has no `canObserve`, so every start read "hidden" and a Sneak could make the monster hidden from creatures that saw it (Valeros went observed to hidden, 2026-11-07). `fixSneakStart` puts Visioner's own stored `getVisibility(observer, monster)` back into the card's `sneakStartStates` and `startStates` before the move, after waiting for all three of Start Sneak's writes (`sneakStartStates`, `sneakStartPosition`, then `startStates`, which its results window reads). No record counts as observed, which is what the GM's records say, even when the module's own perception would call the monster unseen by that creature. The move itself is `runStride` on the same log entry (`started`, `moved`), at most half Speed (`sneakSpeed`, Visioner's own rounding).
  - **The record (`nextStealth`):** `rollStarted`, `card`, then for a Sneak `startStarted`, `startDone` and the move, then `applyStarted`, `applied` (`"applied"` or `"none"`); `gm` hands it over. A reload after Apply was pressed reads the card (`check`): Revert there means done, otherwise the GM looks. Nothing is pressed twice.
  - **What the monster knows (Rhea chose B):** `you.stealth` says what it last tried and that it hasn't come out of hiding since (`turns.stealth`, kept by the module), the light where it stands, its own concealment and who it has standard cover from; never whether the secret roll worked. `doneStep` says "hid (whether it worked is secret)". `checkPlan` refuses a Hide with nothing to hide behind as far as the monster knows (standard cover from a creature it sees, Taking Cover, concealed, or dim light or darkness; after a move only a wall or trunk next to it or the light there); Visioner checks each creature when it applies. A Sneak needs no proof it started hidden, because the monster can't know: Visioner rolls only against those it was hidden from.
  - **Coming out of hiding (Rhea chose A):** `revealsBefore`: anything but Hide, Sneak, Step or an attack makes the monster observed, just before it, by every non-allied creature that has it hidden, undetected or unnoticed in Visioner (`comeOut`, through Visioner's public `setVisibility`), with a note on the card; `revealsAfter`: an attack does so after (its off-guard came from Visioner during the roll), pressing Visioner's own `apply-now-consequences` on the attack card first where it is there. With Visioner's automatic system on, the card notes it and Visioner decides. `turns.stealth` is forgotten then.
  - **No target in the check:** `stopAiming` before PF2e's action, which would otherwise take a target left from an earlier step into the check's roll options.
  - **The thinker's memory of trying:** `turns.stealth` is cleared by any revealing action (`comeOut` runs for every such step and clears it first), whether or not anyone had the monster hidden; clearing it only when someone did would tell the thinker a failed Hide (found by the review). The packet words it as "has only Hidden, Sneaked or Stepped since", never "still hidden".

- **0.28.0:** an `order` step keeps a hidden creature hidden (`KEEPS_HIDDEN`): speaking is no action, so it isn't the "anything else" that makes it observed. **0.29.0:** a `say` step the same, except a yield: giving itself up brings it out of hiding first (`revealsBefore`).

## Traps
- **Visioner's Start Sneak records every start as hidden with its automatic system off** (Foundry 14 has no `TokenDocument#canObserve`): `fixSneakStart` puts its stored states back on the card after all three of its writes. Players' Sneaks have the same bug; Rhea was told.
- **Visioner's Seek checks the whole map** unless the card carries its `seekTemplate` flag, which needs `hasTargets: true` for the Apply button to show. It tests token centres, so Seek's area is an approximation of the rules' squares.
- **Visioner's Hide can hide a monster from its own allies** ("ignore allies" off), so coming out of hiding covers everyone, allies included.
- **The packet's memory of trying to hide must be forgotten at every revealing action,** whether or not anyone had the monster hidden; forgetting it only when someone did would tell the thinker a failed Hide (found by the review).
- **A Sneak is a move too:** dispatch stealth steps before `isMove` in `runTurn` (0.8.0's first build ran Sneaks as plain half-speed Strides; a unit test now guards the order).
