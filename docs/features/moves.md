Stride, Step, Crawl and Fly: routes, costs by Foundry's own measurement and the rules for height, what bars moving, falling, `move.mjs`.

## Decisions
- **Other speeds by the rules (Rhea, 2026-11-08):** asked which speeds matter on her maps she chose all four (fly, swim, climb, burrow); asked how much of swimming to build she said "Movement could be movement. Take the land type into account and the type of movement the monster has." So each monster moves the way its speeds and the ground allow, as the rules say, and Angel chooses. Asked which first, knowing no current map has deep water (narrow creeks are shallow throughout and wading already works), she chose **flying first**, noting that ponds and lakes have deep water too. Then swimming, climbing, burrowing, each its own release.
- **Fly (0.13.0), Angel's reading of the rules (Rules ID 2344):** a `fly` step at the fly Speed; ground terrain and deep water don't slow it; trunks, rocks, walls and enemies' spaces still block it and it can't end in anyone's space (the conservative reading: trees are taller than a flier goes over, Foundry's walls stop a move at any height, and hovering over a creature is left out of the first version). Height: rising is difficult terrain (10 ft for each 5 ft up, and a square crossed while rising is the same step), straight down costs half, height lost while crossing squares costs nothing more (`heightCost`). In the air (up high with a fly Speed; a creature without one that the GM put up high stands on something) it can't Stride, Step, Sneak, Crawl or flee. A ticked monster with a fly Speed in the air at the end of a turn the module ran to the end itself, with no Fly that round (a Fly begun counts, a hover counts), falls: Arrest a Fall (its reaction, the better of Acrobatics and Reflex, DC 15, rolled publicly), else half the distance as bludgeoning damage, applied whatever the monster-damage setting says because it is the monster's own, and prone. Left alone: a turn the GM played, stopped, held or ended by hand (she may have flown it herself), a Delay (its turn hasn't ended), and the GM stepping the tracker backwards. A dead flier's body drops to the ground with no roll. Rising or dropping on its own square leaves its space, so it ends Take Cover like a move; a hover doesn't. A creature off the 5-ft steps (a GM-set 7 ft) can still hover at that height. Area spells count height (`docs/features/areas.md`). The independent review of 0.13.0 found the Delay, reload, GM-turn, dead-flier, ledge, odd-height, area, backwards and public-roll cases; each was verified in the code and fixed.
- **`flee` is a move (0.11.0):** checked and run exactly like a Stride (prone, Speed, route, Foundry's cost), plus it must end on the outermost ring of squares and be the plan's last step; `routeWords` knows the word. The rest of it is `docs/features/fleeing.md`.

## How it works
- **Strides (0.4.6).** A plan step `{ do: "stride", to: [x, y] }`, squares being map squares from the top-left (a creature's square is its top-left one). `checkPlan` replays the plan from where the monster stands: a Stride moves where every later step is measured from, and costs an action.
  - **Only creatures it knows of shape the route it's told about:** allies the GM hasn't hidden, and those it perceives well enough to know their square (observed, concealed, hidden). 0.4.6 counted every creature, so a refusal ("another creature is at…", a longer route, "no way") could give away an invisible PC or a GM-hidden token (found by an independent review). On the move itself, `cutShort` ends it before the space of a creature it didn't know of, as the rules say for moving into an undetected creature's space; with steps left the thinker is asked again with "It was stopped at [x, y], short of where it was going.", never why. The GM's card names the square.
  - **The route:** Foundry has no pathfinding (`Token#findMovementPath` only adds terrain waypoints and stops at walls), so `findRoute` searches the squares: Dijkstra over square and whether an odd number of diagonals has been taken, never through an enemy's space (an ally's is fine), never ending on anyone, never into a square `readMap` calls blocked (a trunk in its middle), never into deep water, and walls by core's own movement test between where the creature's centre would be (`mover`). Difficult squares add 5 ft and greater 10, which is what Foundry charged. **The diagonal count runs across the whole turn** (Rules ID 444, https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=444), and Foundry agrees: it keeps the token's `movementHistory` for the turn (empty again when the turn changes) and charges a new move as the rest of that path. 0.4.6 measured each Stride on its own and said 10 ft for a move Foundry charged 15, after an earlier Stride with an odd number of diagonals. So `mover` measures the history plus this plan's earlier Strides plus the new route, less the same without it, and `findRoute` starts with the diagonal count they leave (`odd`). A history segment can span several squares (a hand-dragged move), so `diagonalsIn` counts min(|dx|, |dy|) per segment.
  - **The cost that counts is Foundry's:** `measureMovementPath` on `createTerrainMovementPath` of the route, with `TerrainData.getMovementCostFunction`. On 5 routes over difficult terrain that equalled what a real `move` charged in `moveToken` (`passed.cost`), and on a real map it equalled `findRoute`'s count for all 223 squares a Medium creature could reach. For a Large creature Foundry charged 5 ft less on 28 of 219 (it seems to test terrain at the token's centre, not each square), so the route may not be the very cheapest by Foundry's count, but the figure compared with Speed is always Foundry's.
  - **Deep water:** Survey Sheet's greater-difficult regions are named "Deep water"; a Stride can't enter those squares (swimming is its own action). Other greater difficult terrain just costs 10 ft more.
  - **Moves are worked out only for a plan with a Stride or Step** (`gather`'s `moves`), and a failure there refuses the Stride in words, so it can't stop a plan of Strikes. A square past where the search looks (four times Speed and 60 ft) is "far beyond its Speed", not "no way".
  - **Carrying it out (`runStride`):** `TokenDocument#move` with every square of the route as a waypoint and the token's own `movementAction` (walk in a fight). It waits for the `moveToken` hook, which comes before the document's position changes, then for the position and the animation, because cover and sight are read from where the token is drawn. `log` gets `started`, then `moved` with where it ended and the cost charged. A move begun before a reload is never sent again: if the monster is where it was going it counts as done, otherwise the GM looks. Scripted moves work while the game is paused (tested).
  - **The route is checked again just before the move:** the look before each action runs `checkPlan` on the rest of the plan, and a Stride then follows the route that check found (saved over the step, with its cost), not the one found when the plan came in. The ground can change without the monster noticing: a wall, or a creature it doesn't perceive stepping onto the old route.
  - **Its own Stride isn't news:** afterwards `seen.me.square` is set (only when the request has a baseline: one from before 0.4.0 or with no packet has none) to where it stands, so the look before the next action doesn't re-plan because it moved. Everything else still counts, including someone now seen differently from the new square (a fresh think, with the right flat check), an HP change from a reaction, and stopping short (the rest of the plan is checked from where it really is).
- **Fly (0.13.0).** `{ do: "fly", to: [x, y], height }` (height in feet above the ground at the end; left out, the height it has). `checkPlan` keeps the monster's height through the plan (`hereElev`), so a Strike after a Fly is measured from the new height. The ground route is `findRoute` over `flyWorld` (every square plain, deep water allowed, solid squares, walls and creatures as for a Stride) priced by Foundry with the `fly` action at the token's height; `heightCost` adds the height by the rules; the total is compared with the fly Speed. Its own square: a change of height there, or with the same height a hover (0 ft), which still counts as having Flown. `runStride` sends the route with `flyWaypoints` (5 ft of height a square as it goes, the rest at the last square, matching the price), action `fly`, each waypoint with its elevation, and waits for the height as well as the square; a reload mid-Fly counts as arrived only at the square and the height (`nextMove` with height). A Fly cut short by a creature it didn't know of ends at the height it had reached there. `moved.cost` is Foundry's charge, which counts height as a plain third axis, so it can differ from the rules price the plan was checked with. The packet gives `you.height` and `height` on anything in the air it perceives (`heightUp`); `fly` is in the step kinds and the prompt (version 15). Falling (`fallIfAloft`) runs on the runner when the turn moves on, from `Combat#previous` read before anything awaits; whether it falls is the pure `shouldFall` (with `movedOn` for the direction), unit-tested case by case. The request gets `fell` and a note saying it is falling first, so a second turn change can't make it fall twice and a reload part-way leaves the GM a note, then the note says what happened. A Fly cut short ends at the height `heightWhenCut` gives. A Stride after a landing goes as a walk (`moveAction`).
- **Steps (0.5.0).** `{ do: "step", to: [x, y] }`: one square, 5 ft (Rules: https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2304). It goes through everything a Stride does (`mover` with `{ step: true }`, `runStride`, the look before it, `cutShort`, the re-plan when stopped short), with `findStep` in place of `findRoute`: the square must be next to it, free, not solid, not deep water, and **no square it enters may be difficult** (a Large creature's old squares don't count); a wall between the centres refuses it. It needs a land Speed of at least 10 ft. **A diagonal Step after an odd number of diagonals this turn is refused:** the 5-10 count runs across all movement in the turn (Rules ID 444), Foundry would charge 10 ft for it, and a Step moves only 5 ft; `checkPlan` compares Foundry's figure with 5. A Step triggers no reactions, so when reactions are built it gets no gates.
- **Crawl (0.5.1).** `{ do: "crawl", to: [x, y] }`: Player Core's Crawl, only while prone, Speed 10+, one square, staying prone. It goes through the Step's path (`findStep`, `runStride`), moving and priced with Foundry's `crawl` action because a prone token can't walk (PF2e's `canSelect`), while a Stride or Step never goes as a crawl (`moveAction`); the check measures it with the terrain cost alone, which is the rules distance (5 ft a square, 10 for difficult terrain or a second diagonal), so anything over 5 ft is refused, difficult terrain included. **Foundry's own crawl action doubles what it books** (core's `costMultiplier: 2`), so a real Crawl's `moved.cost` reads 10 ft for one square; that is Foundry's bookkeeping, not the rules, and nothing else depends on it (a Stride after a Crawl was priced and charged correctly, tested 2026-11-07). Unlike a Step it can trigger reactions when they're built.
**Design-era text (from the plan; where it and the lines above disagree, the lines above, written from the code, win):**
**Move.** Split at reaction gates, then `tokenDoc.move(waypoints)` with the action on each waypoint. In combat PF2e picks `walk` for a creature with a land speed and `crawl` if prone; outside combat it picks `travel` [CODE]. Walk, travel and crawl all cost 30 ft over the test squares when measured [LIVE]. A scripted move stops at walls and its promise resolves early [DOC], so wait for the `moveToken` hook and reread the position. **[UNVERIFIED]** that a real scripted move charges what we measured, and whether GM scripted moves work while paused.
- **Skipped in the review because it would create or change something; all step 2 unless noted:** start-of-turn settling time. **Checked 2026-11-07:** a real move's charged cost equals Foundry's measurement (5 routes, and every Stride since), and GM scripted moves work while paused.

## Traps
- **Foundry prices height as a plain third axis** (14.370, PF2e 8.7.0, measured 2026-11-08 with `measureMovementPath` and the fly action: 10 ft straight up cost 10, 2 squares across while rising 10 cost 15, 20 ft straight down cost 20). PF2e's flying rules make rising double and dropping straight down half, so the module prices height itself (`heightCost`) and keeps Foundry's figure only for the ground route.
- **`Combat#previous` is the turn that just ended,** but it changes again at the next turn: read it synchronously in the update handler, before anything awaits.
- **Delay moves the turn on before its request says so:** `runDelay` records `ended` and calls `nextTurn` while the request is still running; `delayed` is saved after. Anything reacting to the turn change (the fall) must read the Delay step's log, not `delayed` (found by the 0.13.0 review).
- **Foundry has no pathfinding.** `Token#findMovementPath` only adds terrain waypoints and constrains at walls, so `findRoute` searches the squares and Foundry prices the result.
- **The diagonal count runs across the whole turn** (Rules ID 444), and Foundry charges a move as the rest of the token's `movementHistory` for the turn (cleared at the next turn). Measure history + earlier Strides + the new route, less the same without it. 0.4.6 measured each Stride alone: 10 ft planned, 15 charged.
- **Foundry's price for a Large token differs from a square-by-square count** (5 ft less on 28 of 219 squares: it seems to test terrain at the centre). Medium agreed on all 223. Always compare Foundry's number with Speed.
- **`moveToken` comes before the document's x/y change.** Wait for the position, then the animation, before anything reads where the token is drawn (cover, sight).
- **An update's options object can't be reused** across updates that carry `movement` options: core defines a property on it and the second update throws "Cannot redefine property: <tokenId>". Build a fresh one each time.

## Open items
- A move from an off-grid start begins its route at the rounded square while Foundry moves the token from where it is (one Step tested fine, charged 5 ft); a Tiny monster's own turn; elevation with an off-grid position: not tested live.
- A Stride stopped short by Foundry itself (not by an unseen creature): not reproduced.
- Swim, climb and burrow: next, in Rhea's order (swimming has its deep water in rivers, wide creeks, ponds and lakes).
- Flying over creatures or ending above one, and a flier fleeing off the map edge in the air: not built (it must land first to flee).
- A flier stunned or otherwise unable to act falls at the end of its turn with no Arrest a Fall; one killed in the air drops when its own turn passes, so a creature whose turn is skipped once dead stays up until the GM moves it.
- Arrest a Fall doesn't know whether the reaction was already used this round (reactions aren't tracked yet).
- An off-grid flier changing height on its own square is sent to the grid square, a small free shift.
- `moveAction` asks for `walk` for a Stride after a Fly; live, the token's own action already read `walk` after a Fly (2026-11-08), so this is only a guard.
- Deep water only by unit test.
