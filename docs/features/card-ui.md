The Monster Turns window and plan card (`ui/turns.mjs`), and driving chat cards: finding buttons, pressing them, waiting for cards and prompts.

## Decisions
- **The group tick (0.22.0; Rhea, 2026-11-09: "All, plus one per kind").** Asked how one tick should set Angel and Auto for several monsters at once, she chose a top row for every monster plus a row per kind, with each monster keeping its own ticks. A party member the GM plays is in no group, because her rule for it is that its ticks start unticked and are hers to set one by one (0.18.0). By the same reasoning nothing on the party's side or owned by a player is in a group: an NPC ally, a summon or a companion built as an NPC keeps only its own row's ticks (the review's finding; Rhea, 2026-11-10: "Keep it that way"). A kind's row shows only when there are two or more of that kind and more than one kind in the fight, and the top row only with two or more monsters, so no row repeats another.
**Design-era decisions (from the 2026-11-05/06 plan; the lines above are the skill's summary of the same choices):**
- **Rhea stays in charge:** an "Angel runs this" checkbox per monster; a private plan card with Do it / Change / Play it myself; optional auto-act per monster. If the thinking route is unavailable, she plays the turn herself, so a fight never gets stuck.
Its own GM-only window, **Monster Turns**, listing NPC combatants: "Angel runs this", auto-act, model (stored now, used at step 5), and a light showing whether a thinker is listening. **PF2e HUD's tracker is not modified,** because its markup can change with any update and mods surviving updates is Rhea's main worry. No whispered chat cards: the panel holds the plan, and the public chat shows only the real roll cards.
- **Plan steps labelled by what they cost (0.29.1, R-1; Rhea, 2026-11-12: "Right now it's listed in steps, 1, 2, 3, 4. I'd like it to show what it's doing with each action. Free: / Action 1: / Action 2: / Action 3:").** She had read a free order as one of the boss's actions. Each step on the card and in the Change panel starts with its cost: **Free**, **Action n**, or **Actions n–m** for a two- or three-action activity (Angel's proposal for those, not yet answered either way), counted on from what the turn already spent, so a re-plan carries on the count; a monster's own reaction reads **Reaction**; a step that does nothing and a Delay (no action at all) have no label (`stepLabels` in turn.mjs; the Change panel labels the plan's own steps). The Change panel's heading says "from the start" or "from here on" in place of a step number.

## How it works
- **Bands on the card (0.26.0):** a held band line is named with the held mind lines (`mindHeld` keys starting `band.`, in the words of `bands.mjs` `HELD_LABEL`). At the map's edge a monster with others of its band still in the fight gets two buttons, Let this one go and Let the whole band go (`docs/features/fleeing.md`, decision 12); one press disables every edge button on that card. A card's buttons wrap onto a second row when they don't fit (four at the edge in a narrow window squeezed their words before).
- **The Remembers fold-outs (0.25.0):** one on the plan card for the monster in hand and one below it for every other creature that keeps a notebook (`_nbHtml`; what they show is `docs/features/memory.md`). Their buttons carry `data-nbdo`, not `data-do`: the card's own listener takes every `data-do` inside a request's card and would have treated a cross-out as Play it myself. Open fold-outs (`_nbOpen`), a half-typed line (`_nbDraft`) and its refusal (`_nbPrev`) are kept on the window across redraws, and a redraw waits while she types in the add box as it does for the Change panel (`_typing`).
- **Held mind lines (0.24.0):** when a line of the monster's mind was left out of its packet (`mindHeld` on the request: it names someone the monster doesn't perceive, or a name that fits more than one it does), the card says which lines, under what it knows; Monster Minds shows the name.
- **The grey-out timer (0.23.0):** `_onRender` sets the timer that redraws the window when the listening light should go grey only while the light is on (`brainListening`), since a stored time from another world launch no longer counts (`docs/features/brain.md`).
- **Group ticks (0.22.0):** above the monsters' own rows, in italics: "All monsters (n)", then one row per kind with two or more (a kind is the creature a token was made from, its base actor's name, with "Elite" or "Weak" in front when PF2e's adjustment is on the token's actor, so numbered or renamed tokens of one creature stay together and an Elite or Weak one is its own kind). `tickGroups` (pure, `ui/ticks.mjs`) builds the rows from the same list the window shows (`tickRows`) and says whether all, some or none of a group are ticked; some shows as a half-ticked box, and clicking it ticks the rest. A click writes one `updateEmbeddedDocuments` for the tokens whose own tick differs (`groupChanges`, read again at the click), so each token gets the same `run` or `auto` flag its own box would write; the group boxes are greyed from the click until the redraw the update brings (a second click can't act on ticks that haven't arrived), and a refused write redraws the window and everything downstream is unchanged: the runner's `onTokenFlag` asks for the current monster's turn when Angel is ticked, and Auto ticked on a waiting plan starts it.
- **Monsters' reactions (0.21.0):** below the plan card, one box per offered Reactive Strike on a player's turn: the line (`offerWords`), "Not certain." with the reason when the module isn't sure, Do it and Skip (Do it greyed with the runner's problem), "It takes it by itself (Auto)" or the pause note for an Auto monster, the players' question while its hit waits for Shield Block (`rxHtml`, shared with the plan card), and what came of it until the turn moves on. A line at the foot lists the monsters' other reactions as the GM's to play. Details: `docs/features/reactions.md`.
- **Reactions (0.20.0):** while a step waits on reactions the card shows "Reactions:" with the sentence, each reactor's state, React / Pass (then Done for a Reactive Strike) for one the GM answers (no player online, or a party member she plays), Continue, and the countdown (redrawn each second, only while a player row can still be answered: 0.20.1, `countingDown`; the GM's own rows have none); and "Not asked by the module: …" for reactions the module doesn't know, with their trigger. The player's own window is `ui/react.mjs` (`docs/features/reactions.md`).
- **Change (0.19.0):** beside Do it / Next step while the plan can be changed (`changeProblem`); its panel replaces the steps list until sent or cancelled, and a refused change shows as a warning line (`changeVerdict`). The draft is kept on the window (`_draft`), so the many redraws during a fight keep what she typed; it goes when the window closes. Angel's plan reason is shown in names (until 0.19.0 it showed labels). Details: `docs/features/change.md`.
- **Who is listed (0.18.0):** every combatant Angel can run (`runnable`): monsters, and a party member the GM plays, marked "(party)" after its name, its ticks unticked until she ticks them. A character a player owns isn't listed.
- **At the map's edge (0.11.0):** in state `fled` the card shows Extend map <side> per side (disabled with the reason as its title, and the reason written on the card) and Let it get away, Do it gone, Play it myself kept; an **Escaped** section under the card lists hidden escaped tokens with Bring back. The presses are noted for the history. See `docs/features/fleeing.md`.
- **A card waiter's timer removes only its own waiter** (`waitFor`, `waitForAny`): a stage that ended early without its card (a refused shot) would otherwise have removed the next stage's waiter 20 s later (found by the 0.9.0 review).
- **A prompt nobody can click:** a roll waits for its dialog for ever, and nobody can click the runner's page. `watched` gives each stage `STEP_MS`, stops at once if a window that looks like a dialog appears, closes what appeared (a closed Modifier dialog makes the roll return with no card), and the record then says the stage may not have finished, so it goes to Rhea with the window's title and nothing repeats. PF2e's Modifier dialog is a v1 app in `ui.windows`, not in `foundry.applications.instances`, so both are watched.
- **Only this turn on the card:** `currentRequest` is this round and turn's unfinished request, and `latestNote` only the latest finished turn's note. Both came from an old turn showing up as if it were current: a stopped request after Play it myself, and a 0 HP warning while the hero stood at 5.
**Design-era text (from the plan; where it and the lines above disagree, the lines above, written from the code, win):**
**The card's buttons:** **Do it**; **Change** (edit any step, or type a note that goes back to the thinker; built in 0.19.0: `docs/features/change.md`); **Play it myself** (always there, and ends the mod's part in that turn); **Hold** (pauses before the next step).
- **0.27.0:** the Party reactions block (a box for each creature on the party's side that can react, with how it was ticked), a reaction row's own button labels, and the open repent-or-refuse question with Repent and Refuse (`choiceHtml`) under the turn's card or a monster's reaction: all described in `reactions.md`. The redraw that follows a fight also redraws the player's chip (`main.mjs`).

- **0.28.0 (`docs/features/orders.md`):** the Give orders box (`_orderBox`: on the card when the creature whose turn it is leads a band and isn't ticked; its draft is kept on the window object, and a redraw waits while one of its text fields has focus), the hearing ticks (`_hearHtml`, on the box and on the leader's card while its plan holds an order to come), and a member's "Order heard" or "Didn't hear" line.
- **Yielding (0.29.0):** a monster that stands down has Accepted and Fight on on its row where its ticks were (`data-ytoken`, so the tick handlers pass it by), a card of its own with what it said and a third button, Play it myself, and prisoners are listed under Escaped with Back in the fight. One press greys every button for that monster (`docs/features/speech.md`).

## Traps
- **An unlinked token's own actor carries the token's name, and PF2e's Elite or Weak adjustment renames nothing** (PF2e 8.7.0, seen live 2026-11-10: a token renamed "Ally Goblin" had its own actor named "Ally Goblin" and `baseActor.name` "Goblin Warrior"; an Elite one kept both names and had `actor.isElite`). To tell what kind of creature a token is, read `baseActor.name` and `actor.isElite` / `isWeak`, never the token actor's own name.
- **The Monster Turns window object is reused when reopened** (`main.mjs` keeps one `TurnsApp`): anything kept on it (the Change draft) survives a close unless `_onClose` clears it (seen in 0.19.0's first test build: the panel came back open).
- Anything shown on the Monster Turns card must belong to the current turn or the latest finished one. Old requests and old notes stay on the Combat flag.
- **PF2e's Modifier dialog is a v1 app in `ui.windows`, not in `foundry.applications.instances`.** Watch both.
- **The Monster Turns window shows the fight on the scene its browser views,** so it must redraw on `canvasReady` and on fights, combatants and tokens changing, not only on fight updates (fixed in 0.5.1; before that a scene change left "No fight on this scene").
- **Visioner's card buttons from code (found live on 0.8.0):** a press right after the card appears can come before Visioner binds its panel's click handler and does nothing, and a card drawn while the runner viewed another scene keeps a panel that finds no token ("No changes to apply"). `press` redraws the card (`ui.chat.updateMessage`) with the fight map open, waits for the panel's jQuery click handler, and counts a press only when the button is `processing` straight after the click. Read Apply's answer from the pressed button, never the card again: a redrawn card always shows Apply.
- **A card waiter's timer must remove only its own waiter** (`waitFor`, `waitForAny`): a stage that ends early without its card would otherwise remove the next stage's waiter (0.9.0 review).
- **A fold-out's own title takes focus (0.29.1, bug B-2, seen in Rhea's browser 2026-11-12):** a click that opens or closes a `<details>` leaves `document.activeElement` on its `<summary>`. `_typing()` counted any focus inside `.leb-nb` as typing, so every redraw waited until she clicked elsewhere: a ready plan showed as "Waiting for a plan". The notebook's box and buttons still hold a redraw (the press-and-release trap above); its `<summary>` holds one only while the mouse is down on it (`_pressing`, released on `pointerup`), so the opening click isn't lost either (0.29.1 review 6).
