The leader's orders in the fight (monster minds release 3, tier 3b): one order a round as data, heard by those in earshot who share its language, kept for each hearer; the Give orders box when the GM plays the leader; a band log the History reads; and the leader's fall acted on by the module (the next in line with a named second, Falls apart, Fights harder).

## Decisions
Rhea, 2026-11-11 (the design and its review against the code are in the project's design doc; this is what tier 3b builds of them):
1. **One order a round, one sentence long (R3-3).** A band's plan has three parts: the objective, who does what, when it falls back. On its turn the leader may change one thing: the objective, one kind's job, or call the fall-back. It is a line of the leader's plan (Do it approves it like any step; Auto sends it). Only members who hear it change; the rest keep the old plan. Discipline decides what a member does with it.
2. **When she plays the leader herself: a Give orders box (R3-5)** with three quick buttons (Fall back; Everyone on that one; Hold here) and a box for her own words. It goes out like any order: one a round, by hearing. With no order given the last plan stands. She was shown sample pictures of the box, of an order on the leader's card and of its line in chat before any code, and answered: "Yes, lets go with that for this build".
3. **Players hear the orders as a public chat line from the leader (R3-4), shown by language.** Tier 3b showed every player "Shouts something in <language>."; since 0.29.0 a player whose character knows the language reads the sentence the leader shouted, or the order in names made for players when it said none (`docs/features/speech.md`).
4. **An order may tell a member of an enemy it hasn't perceived: that it exists, not where (R3-14),** over "that part is dropped" and "only by Point Out". The member learns there is such an enemy and what the leader wants done about it, with a label and no square; it can't target it until it perceives it itself. Being told where stays Point Out (tier 3d).
5. **If the leader falls (R3-2), the module now does what the band's pick says** (tier 3a only told the thinker in words): Next in line takes over (a named second, else the highest level left; the band keeps its Discipline and gets new orders from the new leader's turn on); Falls apart (the plan is gone, each fights by its own mind, Discipline drops to Loose); Fights harder (the plan becomes killing the one who dropped the leader, nobody gives up). The band knows at once (R3-10).
6. **Animals in a band don't get spoken orders (R3-6):** they fight by their own Pack mind. Command an Animal is tier 3d.

From the design reviews, kept as built:
- **Orders are data, never prose (review 8):** which of the three parts changed, a label or a place. The module writes fixed words for each hearer, as notebook lines are written; the sentence said aloud is for the chat line and the History only. So nothing only the leader perceives can ride along in a thinker's prose. Her own sentence does reach the hearers: it is hers, and it goes through the name gate against what the leader has perceived, once, when the order is given.
- **Orders, the leader falling and a hearing call are no mid-turn re-plan triggers (review 13):** they are in a member's next packet.
- **One a round has a limiter (review 7):** the round of each band's last order is kept, so a re-plan, a second plan or a turn that comes back after a Delay can't give a second one; the leader's packet says whether it may still give one.
- **Who wins when they pull apart (reviews 11 and 17):** the rules and its conditions; what the GM asks for this turn; Notes; orders heard, as far as its Discipline holds it to them; the band's plan; the rest of its mind.

Settled by Angel, told to Rhea with the samples or in the release note, hers to change:
- **The chat line in this release:** everyone's browser holds "Shouts something in <language>."; a GM's browser shows the words said, the order in names and how many of the band heard. Nothing is whispered. The words, the order in names and the counts sit in the public message's flag, off the screen for players (her standing decision of 2026-11-11: what a player's browser merely holds is no reason to change a design).
- **A leader whose token the GM has hidden puts no line in chat:** the line would give an ambush away. Its band still hears it. (What a hidden monster's speech shows is asked with tier 3c.)
- **"Something else…"** is a fourth button beside her three: the whole band's goal or one kind's job, in her own words.
- **The quick buttons:** Fall back calls the fall-back the band's plan describes (a rally spot or off the map); Everyone on that one lists the creatures the leader perceives this round; Hold here takes the leader's own square or a click on the map.
- **A target on the leader's own side** makes the order "protect" it. Only a thinker's order can say that; her box sends the band at enemies (her own words can say anything).
- **Hearing:** a member gets an order when it follows a leader (Loose, Trained or Drilled), is up and awake, not deafened, above ground, understands the language and has a path for sound from the leader (core's sound walls). The language is the one of the leader's that most of its followers understand. The band is read from the band's own data, so an ambusher the GM has hidden hears its leader (review 9).
- **Her "can't hear" call** ("a switch per member for what the module can't know") is a tick beside each member, on the Give orders box and on the leader's card while its plan still holds an order. Her tick or untick wins over the module's test and lasts that round.
- **"Hasn't seen it yet"** beside a hearer on those two lists (as her approved sample showed): the order names a creature that hearer has never perceived in this fight, so it learns it is there, not where (R3-14). It is read from each monster's last-seen memory, so it is a guide for her; what the hearer is really sent is worked out when its packet is made.
- **A kind's job can be ordered only when someone of that kind besides the leader takes spoken orders:** the leader alone in its kind, and a kind of animals, aren't offered (to her box or to the thinker).
- **The deafened leader:** speech isn't an action, so its order needs no flat check; a deafened hearer gets nothing (the review's reading of the rule).
- **The leader keeps its own orders** like any hearer, so its later packets say what it last ordered.
- **An order stands until another takes its place.** A new objective replaces the old one; a call to fall back takes the objective's place and the other way round; each kind keeps the latest order for its job (four kinds at most). A band that falls apart loses its standing orders with its plan, and a band that fights harder loses the fallen leader's last order to the whole band (the new objective takes its place). A new leader's band keeps the orders it had, and the new leader may give its own on its turn, in that round too.
- **The order costs no Next step press and no Auto pause:** the plan goes straight on to its next step. It must come before the leader's last action (review 7: a re-plan with no actions left would end the turn before it speaks).
- **A named second** is a select on the band's page in Monster Minds, under the Leader. It is used only by "Next in line takes over".
- **Fights harder needs someone to avenge:** the objective names the enemy whose blow took the leader to 0 hit points, when one of the leader's own side, awake, saw that enemy at that moment (kept from that blow's own card, because a creature that is down writes nothing in its notebook); when nobody of its side saw the striker, or the card names nobody (lasting damage), the enemy whose blow the leader's notebook has last and for sure; with neither, "whoever struck the leader down". A leader that fled or whose token is gone leaves the plan as it is. "Plan stands" and "Breaks and runs" still only tell the thinker in words.
- **The one who takes over leads for the rest of the fight,** even if the old leader gets back up. If its own token leaves the map, the next in line is found again.
- **Play it myself on a leader ticked for Angel** brings her box up for that turn (`tookTurn`: this turn's latest request is "manual"; the turn's current request never is, which is why the box didn't come up in the first two test builds).

## How it works
- **The rules** (`orders.mjs`, pure): `cleanOrder` gives `{ part, target | at | text, job, say }` (`text`, an order in the GM's own words, is taken only with `gm: true`); `orderWords` writes it for one hearer ("everyone on E2", "protect M3", "hold at [4, 9]", "your job now: go for E2", "fall back to the rally spot at [2, 2]"); `canOrder`, `orderLanguage` and `hearing` decide who may order, in what language and who gets it; `applyHeard` keeps a hearer's standing orders (`{ goal: { r, o }, jobs: { id: { r, o } } }`); `ordersForPacket` builds the packet's block; `fallOutcome` says what a leader's fall changes by the band's pick and `dropperOf` who struck it down; `logWords` and `roundLine` write the band log for the GM.
- **A kind's job is named by an id** (`bandlib.mjs` `jobGroups`): `r2` for a build row; `k` and a number worked out from the kind's own key for a kind the build didn't place, so it stays the same when another kind's tokens leave the map. The leader's packet lists them in `orders.give.jobs`.
- **Where it is kept:** the Combat's turns flag, under `orders`: `log` (the band log, append-only, one key an entry), `heard` (each hearer's standing orders, replaced whole), `last` (the round of each band's last order), `calls` (her hearing calls, with their round), `pending` (an order from her box waiting for the runner) and `refused` (why the runner turned one down). Beside `orders` on the same flag, `dropped` keeps for each creature the blow that last took it to 0 hit points (`{ by: a token or null, round }`, written by the runner from the damage card in the notebooks' queue, `noteDropped`; `by` is the striker only when one of the creature's own side saw it; a card that leaves the creature above 0 again takes the entry away). What a fall changed is on the Combat's `bands` flag (`fell`, `leader`, `planGone`, `objective`) and `minds` flag (`own.<token>.discipline`, `own.<token>.givesUp`): the this-fight layers `bandlib.mjs` and `library.mjs` already read. The runner writes all of it but `calls` and `pending`.
- **The `order` step** (`turn.mjs` `checkPlan`, `runOrder`): free (`COSTS.order` 0), one a plan, only for a leader that `canOrder`, a kind its band has, a creature it perceives itself (or one of its own, to protect). It keeps a hidden creature hidden (speech is no action). `runOrder` works out the hearers on the fight's map and writes the log entry, every hearer's standing orders, the band's `last` and the step's record in **one update**, then posts the chat line; the turn loop goes straight on.
- **The packet** (`ordersBlock`, since schema 5): `orders` comes after `bandNow`: `heard` (words with the round, the objective first), `toldOf` (labels an order or a fight-harder objective names that this monster neither perceives nor remembers) and, for the leader, `give` (`can`, `why`, `jobs`). Only for members that follow a leader; never for the party's side.
- **Her box** (`ui/turns.mjs` `_orderBox`, `orderPreview`, `giveOrder`, `runPending`): shown on the card when the creature whose turn it is leads a band and isn't ticked for Angel. Her page writes `pending` (`toPending`; refused at once when the runner is away); the runner gives the leader, its allies and every creature it has perceived a label if they have none, turns party names in her words into labels (refused in words when the leader hasn't perceived that creature), and gives the order exactly like a plan's. The box then folds to one line for the round.
- **The chat line** (`orderChat`, `onRenderOrder`): one public message from the leader's token holding "Shouts something in <language>." with the words, the order and the count in a flag; on a GM's page the render hook shows those instead.
- **The leader's fall** (`settleBands`, runner, at every turn change before the turn is asked for): for each band whose leader is down, dead, fled or gone and not yet acted on (`bands.<key>.fell.was`), `fallOutcome` is applied in one update with a `fell` entry in the log. The work runs inside the notebooks' queue (the blow's `dropped` line is written there first, and labels have one writer), and a second turn change that comes meanwhile waits for it. `leaderOf` reads `bands.<key>.leader` first, so the one who took over is the leader for packets, orders and the windows; `planOf` gives no plan after `planGone` and the new `objective` after Fights harder.
- **The windows:** the leader's card shows who would hear the order its plan still holds, with her ticks; a member's card says "Order heard" with the words and how firmly its Discipline holds it, or "Didn't hear" and why. The History shows an order from a plan as that plan's step (who heard, who didn't and why), and an order the GM gave and every fall as lines of their own in their round; both are in the saved page and in the saved-or-not check.
- **API** (`api.fight.orders`): `preview(combat, tokenId)`, `give(combat, tokenId, order)` (the GM's box: never Angel's door), `hear(combat, tokenId, "yes" | "no" | null)`, `card`, `log`, `words`. `api.minds.bands`' `set` takes `second`.
- **After Extend map west or north (0.29.1):** a hearer's standing "hold at" or "take the square" moves with the ground (`shiftHeard`, called from `afterExtend`). A pending order from the Give orders box is left as it is: it may have been picked on the grown map, it is given within seconds, and rewriting it could bring back one the runner had just given (0.29.1 review 5). The band log keeps the words as they were said.
- **A leader that changed sides (0.30.0):** `settleBandsNow` takes the fall's state "turned" from the turns flag's `turned.<band>` (`sides.md`); `fallOutcome` treats it as any fall (Fights harder has nobody to avenge), and the band log reads "has changed sides". The band log also carries the lines of a failed command the GM answered and of side changes (`logWords` passes them to `command.mjs` and `sides.mjs`).

## Traps
- **Three rule files import each other now** (`turn.mjs` reads `orders.mjs` for the plan check, `orders.mjs` reads `bands.mjs`, `bands.mjs` reads `turn.mjs`; `minds.mjs` and `bands.mjs` already did): none may use another's exports while the file is first being read, only inside functions.
- **The box's order keeps its time stamp under `ts`:** `at` is the order's square. A stamp written under `at` replaced the square of every Hold here (0.28.0 review 1).
- **The name gate reads the labels it is handed** (`notePeople`'s third argument): her page and the runner pass the labels as they would stand, because a fight she plays herself has given out none.
- **An order is given in one update** (the log entry, each hearer's standing orders, the band's `last`, the step's record). Written apart, a reload between them would give it twice or leave the round's order spent with nobody told.
- **A standing order is replaced whole** (`ForcedReplacement`): a flag update merges objects, so an order with a place saved over one with a label would keep the label.
- **Labels are given out by the runner only, in the notebooks' queue:** her page shows her words with the labels they would get (`labelsFor`) and saves none.
- **A click inside the box after typing:** the text field's blur must not redraw the window when the click lands in the box, or the click is lost between its press and its release (the notebook's trap again).
- **A redraw of the Monster Turns window wipes a text field,** so the box's draft is kept on the window object and a redraw waits while a text field of the box has focus.

- **A label's letter says nothing about sides:** E or M is whoever first needed the label. A party member whose own side keeps a notebook (a party member the GM plays) can be first labelled from there and be an "M" (seen live in 0.28.0: the fighter who struck the leader down was "M1"). Ask the creature (`isEnemyOf`), never the letter.
- **A creature that is down writes nothing in its notebook,** so the blow that drops it is never there: anything that needs "who dropped it" reads `dropped`.

## Open items
- A creature brought to 0 by hit points typed by hand, or by anything else that posts no damage card, leaves `dropped` as it was: usually nothing, so the notebook decides.
- An order for a kind's job that was approved while that kind still had someone to take it is still given if they have all fallen by the time its step runs (it uses up the round's order).
- The Second select on a band's page offers a creature that follows no leader (an animal); it is passed over at the fall.
- Telepathy, and a language a creature understands but can't speak, aren't told apart: the sheet's language list decides both who can speak and who understands.
- The hearers her page shows before an order is given are worked out on her page: without the fight's map open there, only conditions, languages and her calls show (the window says so); the runner's own test on the fight's map is the one that counts.
- With Angel leading on Auto, an order goes out before she can tick a hearing call for that round.
- Her box is there only on the turn of a leader she plays. For a leader Angel plays, she changes its order with Change (leave it out, another creature, another square) or plays the turn herself.
- Her box can't order "protect" with a button; her own words can.
- A reload of the runner's page between an order's one update and its chat line leaves the order given with no line in chat (the History has it).
- The preview of her own words is redrawn only when focus leaves the box; Give the order checks them again whatever the preview shows.
- Monster Minds doesn't show who took over in a fight; the cards and the History do.
- A fight-layer line for one monster (a Discipline dropped to Loose, "never gives up") hides this fight's lines for its kind in `mindLayers`; nothing writes a kind's lines on a fight yet (the fight-start look will).
- A member whose plan is waiting isn't asked again when an order arrives. It can't happen today (an order is given on the leader's own turn).
- "Plan stands" and "Breaks and runs" are still told to the thinker in words only; the module changes nothing for them.
- A leader the GM has hidden isn't acted on when it falls (bands, open items).
- **0.29.0:** an order can also tell the band to yield (`part: "yield"`; only a thinker's order or her own words, her box has no button for it): each hearer then decides by its Discipline whether to yield on its own turn. A member that has yielded doesn't get orders ("has yielded" beside its name), and a leader that gave itself up counts as fallen (`docs/features/speech.md`).
