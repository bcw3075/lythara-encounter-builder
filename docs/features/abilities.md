Abilities: Strike follow-ups (Grab, Knockdown, Push, Pull) run for real; every other ability posts PF2e's own card for the GM.

## Decisions
- **Abilities (2026-11-07), "Strike follow-ups for real":** Grab, Knockdown, Push, Pull and the Improved ones need its last action to be a Strike that lists them (by the ability's own slug) and hits, at the same target; PF2e's own Grapple, Trip, Shove or Reposition rolls with no `multipleAttackPenalty` (none applies, none counts); the module applies Grabbed, Restrained or Prone and a critical Trip's 1d6; the GM moves a pushed or pulled creature and applies a target's choice on a critical failure (the turn pauses). Grab's hold only on a grip its own Grab made (`turns.grips`), always 1 action. **Every other ability:** PF2e's own Use (`game.pf2e.rollItemMacro(item.uuid)`) posts its card and the turn stops at Next step, Auto or not, for the GM to apply it. Passive and reaction items refused.

## How it works
- **A character's abilities (0.18.0):** for a party member the GM plays, `abilityList` adds its feats that are actions, free actions or reactions to its action items (a character's actions are mostly feats); an NPC's list is unchanged. Reactions stay out of plans, as for monsters.
- **Abilities (0.5.3).** `{ do: "ability", item: "<id>", target: "E1" }`, the item one of the monster's action items (`abilityList`: the slug, or the name made into one, `-2` when two share it). It costs the item's own actions (`abilityCost`: 0 for a free action, `actionCost.value` otherwise). Passive and reaction items are refused by name, and so is one whose frequency has no uses left. Rhea, 2026-11-07, chose to run the Strike follow-ups for real and to send every other ability to the GM:
  - **Follow-ups (`FOLLOW_UPS`): Grab, Knockdown, Push, Pull and the Improved ones.** In the remaster each is an Athletics check, not an automatic effect: Grapple (Fortitude DC), Trip (Reflex), Shove (Fortitude), Reposition (Fortitude), and it **neither applies nor counts toward the multiple attack penalty** (Rules: https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx, Grab ID 45, Knockdown 46, Push 47, Pull 71, the Improved ones 66 as free actions; Grapple https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2376, Trip 2382, Reposition 2379). **The requirement is the monster's last action:** a Strike that lists it in its effects (`attackEffects`, by the ability's own slug: a Strike listing `improved-grab` allows Improved Grab only) and hits, at the same target. `checkPlan` replays `last` through the plan: the step just before, or for a plan's first step `ctx.last` from `lastAction` (the step before it in the request with its record, or the last entry of `done` after a re-plan). At judging time the Strike hasn't happened, so the step is accepted with `needsHit`; the look before it runs `checkPlan` again with the Strike's record, so a miss or a failed flat check refuses it and the turn is asked again with the action unspent (a free action or any action never spent on a refused step). Also checked: target in reach of that Strike (melee only), the target no more than one size larger (Player Core: Grapple, Trip, Reposition, Shove; for a hidden target the refusal doesn't say why, so its size isn't given away), Restrained (attack trait), and the flat check for a concealed or hidden target, which it carries like a Strike.
  - **Grab's other use:** keep hold of a creature it has grabbed or restrained, with no check (Grab's second sentence). Only a grip this monster's own Grab made counts: the effects stage keeps it as `turns.grips[monster token][target token]`, and `held` is true only for those that are still Grabbed or Restrained (another creature's grip, or one the GM added by hand, doesn't count, and the thinker learns nothing from a refusal). Accepted when it isn't straight after a hit with a Strike that lists Grab, within that Strike's reach; it **always takes an action, Improved Grab's too** (Monster Core: it still needs an action to extend the duration). It posts the card and applies nothing, because nothing tracks how long a Grapple lasts. `runTurn` takes the step as the look before it found it, so a Grab whose Strike missed becomes a hold if the target is held.
  - **A Strike can list a follow-up the creature has no item for** (PF2e's attack effects don't need one): `abilityList` adds it by its slug, so the thinker can still use it; a hold with no item posts a plain card.
  - **Uses through a plan:** an ability with one use left can't be planned twice; `none` counts as its last action, so nothing can stand between a Strike and its follow-up.
  - **Carrying it out (`runAbility`):** PF2e's own action (`game.pf2e.actions.get("trip" | "grapple" | "shove" | "reposition").use({ actors, target, event })`) rolls the check with no dialog by the same Shift rule as Strikes, and with no `multipleAttackPenalty` passed it adds none (read in PF2e 8.7.0's `SingleCheckActionVariant#use`; tested: one modifier, the Athletics bonus). Its answer is a list of `{ outcome, message, roll }`. **PF2e applies nothing**, only notes, so `followUpOutcome` gives what the rules say for each degree and the module applies it: Grabbed (success) or Restrained (critical success) on the target, Prone on the target for a Trip (and a critical one's `1d6[bludgeoning]`, rolled as PF2e's `DamageRoll` and applied like a Strike's damage, with the undo card and `markApplied`), Prone on the monster for a critical failure on a Trip or Shove. **Left to the GM, with a note and a pause:** moving a pushed or pulled creature (the module doesn't do forced movement), a target's choice on a critical failure (Grapple: grab back or knock it prone; Reposition: move it 5 ft), and a failed Grapple on a target that was already held (if this monster held it, that ends). Stages in `log`: `flatStarted`/`flat`, `started` with `held`, `check` (`degree` 0 to 3), `effects` (what was applied and what's left), then `damageStarted`, `damage`, `applying`, `applied`. Adding a condition again changes nothing, so the effects stage is safe to run again after a reload; the rest never repeats (`nextAbility`). **A stage's record and the GM's note go in one save** (and the grip with the effects), so a reload can't keep one without the other. Before rolling, `runAbility` also checks that the Strike before it hit, in case the look before the step didn't run.
  - **A free follow-up survives a re-plan with no actions left:** when the Strike that just hit lists an Improved one the monster has, `replan` asks again instead of ending the turn.
  - **The monster falling prone on a critical failure is not taken into the baseline,** unlike its own Drop Prone: it didn't mean to, so the look before the next action re-plans. The target's new condition isn't in the baseline at all (only its square, how it's perceived and whether it's down), so a successful Knockdown doesn't re-plan; a critical Trip's damage dropping it does.
  - **Any other ability goes to the GM (mode `card`):** PF2e's own use, `game.pf2e.rollItemMacro(item.uuid)`, which is what a sheet's or hotbar's Use does (`createUseActionMessage`): its card, a frequency use, a self-effect button. The runner aims at the target first. The step then returns `gm`: `runTurn` saves the step done and stops at `ready` (Auto or not) with "Apply … from its card. Then press Next step." The look before the next action then sees what the GM's applying changed and re-plans if anything counts. An ability with the attack trait counts toward the multiple attack penalty (`mapCounts`, carried in `spentBy`) and its card step shows which attack it is; Restrained refuses attack and manipulate abilities, a move-action ban refuses move abilities. Requirements written in an ability's text can't be read, so the GM sees them on the card.
  - **Take Cover:** a follow-up or an attack-trait ability ends it like a Strike (`endsCover`, `settleCover`).
  - **Not done:** how long a Grapple lasts (Grabbed and Restrained stay until someone removes them); Push's own distance when its entry lists one; the Shove's follow-up Stride (a plan's own Stride step); the flat check a Grabbed monster needs for a manipulate action; and the inline damage link in PF2e's critical Trip note, which would roll a second 1d6 if someone clicked it.
**Design-era text (from the plan; where it and the lines above disagree, the lines above, written from the code, win):**
**Everything else.** Conditions and start and end of turn stay with Workbench. Flat checks are never rolled by PF2e or Visioner [DOC], so the mod rolls them: concealed DC 5, hidden DC 11, spending the action on a failure [RULES]. Skill actions use `game.pf2e.actions.get(slug).use(...)`; demoralize, hide, seek, take-cover, grapple, trip, shove, feint, escape, sneak, stand and create-a-diversion are registered [LIVE].
- **Anything else: the checklist.** The card says what the thinker wants to do, the mod posts the item's card, and Rhea applies it. Never faked.
- **0.27.0 (`reactions.md`):** an ability's own damage (a critical Trip's) asks the damage gate before it is applied; a refused Glimpse of Redemption's resistance is on the copy of the creature the damage is applied to.
- **An ability aimed at a friend (0.30.0, bug B-5):** a non-follow-up ability's `target` may be one of its allies' labels or its own, as a spell's may (`ctx.allies` is always gathered now); the step's token is found through `tokenIdOf`, which knows allies' labels. An ability with the attack trait aimed at its own side is refused as such. Before, "target": "M2" on a rallying ability was refused as "it doesn't know of M2" although the prompt says an ability's target is a label (found by the API usage test: both models wrote it).
- **0.33.1:** the card for a Grab that keeps hold with no item of its own escapes the ability's and the creature's names.

## Traps
- **PF2e's skill actions apply nothing.** `game.pf2e.actions.get("trip" | "grapple" | "shove" | "reposition").use({ actors, target: token.object, event })` rolls with no dialog by the Shift rule and returns `[{ outcome, message, roll }]`; its card only has notes. Without `multipleAttackPenalty` it adds no penalty. The critical Trip note's inline @Damage link would roll a second 1d6 if clicked.
- **`game.pf2e.rollItemMacro(item.uuid)`** is PF2e's own Use for an action item, unlinked tokens included (`createUseActionMessage`: card, frequency use, self-effect button). Players see those cards as "Is using an ability." through Toolbelt's Anonymous.
