Monster minds: a behaviour profile for every monster (six lines and notes) read from layers, the Minds Library that keeps them for reuse, and the two windows to read and edit them. Release 1 of 4.

## Decisions
Rhea, 2026-11-10, question by question (the design and its independent review are in the project's design doc; this is what release 1 builds):
1. **Profile shape: "A short form plus notes."** Six lines and a free notes box: Mind; Goes for; Fights by; Gives up; Works with others; Knows about its enemies.
2. **The picks are examples, not fixed lists** ("I hope those were just examples in each category"): every line takes her own words, with suggestions. Only **Mind** and **Works with others** keep a fixed pick underneath, because the module acts on them (how clever a plan may be; who will share a group plan).
3. **"Goes for" is not all or nothing:** a starting order plus what shifts it, weighed again at every plan and re-plan (the prompt says "a leaning, not a lock").
4. **Per kind, with any one monster able to differ:** a monster given its own profile stops following its kind's.
5. **A library, in Foundry** ("an efficient library system where all of the mind data will be stored so it can be reused"; "In Foundry", not the campaign notes).
6. **Profiles completely replace the Tactics page** ("This is the upgrade to that first version"): the profile is the one home for how a monster behaves; a brief's tactics text becomes that kind's Notes for the encounter; the build's journal has no Tactics page.
7. **Four releases,** each usable on its own: (1) profiles, the library and its windows, every turn going through the profile; (2) fight memory; (3) group nature, the shared plan and the leader's orders; (4) the fight-start look with Accept / Keep.
8. **The window samples** ("Looks good for a starting point"): kinds on the left with a single monster's own profile under its kind; the lines on the right with a tag saying where each comes from; a warning under a line that names a party member; Give one monster its own profile, Save to library, Save for this encounter; the library as one people with its kinds side by side, "same" for an inherited line.

Settled by Angel, told to Rhea, hers to change:
- **Five Minds:** Mindless, Animal, Simple, Cunning, Trained tactician. Their meaning is in the prompt, once.
- **"Works with others" became "Discipline" in 0.26.0** (Rhea's R3-1 and R3-11, 2026-11-11: a five-step ladder set per kind of creature; `docs/features/bands.md`): None, Pack, Loose, Trained, Drilled. The line keeps its place in the profile and its fixed pick; the four old picks are read as the nearest Discipline (On its own: None; With its pack: Pack; Follows a leader's plan and Is the leader: Loose), and who leads is a mark on the band. Decisions 1 and 2 above name the line as it was called when she chose the shape.
- **From the stat block when nothing is written** (so nothing plays blank; marked as the module's guess): the Mindless trait; otherwise Intelligence (-4 or less Animal, -3 or -2 Simple, -1 to +2 Cunning, +3 or more Trained tactician); and for Discipline: a mindless creature None, an animal Pack, a trained tactician Trained, anything else that thinks Loose (Drilled is never guessed).
- **A party member Angel plays** has only a profile written on that token (by the GM in Monster Minds, or by Angel when she asks): never the monsters' library, this encounter's lines or a stat-block guess.
- **Who wins when they pull apart** (design review 11), in the prompt: the rules and its conditions, then what the GM asks for this turn, then Notes, then the rest of its mind. Orders and a band's plan take their places in release 3.
- **Allies' deeds this round and the side's tally** (design review 15) are left for release 2, with fight memory.
- **No page for minds in the build's journal:** the Monster Minds window is the one place to read and edit.

## How it works
- **A line** is `{ pick?, text, by, at? }`: `by` is whose words they are (`gm`, `angel`, or `module` for a stat-block guess). `{ blank: true }` is a line left empty on purpose, which stops the layers below from filling it; a missing line inherits. Caps: 240 characters a line and 600 for Notes, the same stored as sent, so nothing is cut behind her back; a longer text (an old brief's tactics) is cut at a word's end when it is cleaned.
- **Layers, most specific first** (`assemble`): what was agreed in this fight (the Combat's `minds` flag: `own[tokenId]` or `kinds[{ kind, lines }]`; nothing writes it until release 4, but it is read from now on); the monster's own; this encounter's for its kind; its kind in the library; its people in the library; the stat block. For each line the first layer that says anything wins whole. An own profile is the whole profile: with one, the encounter, kind and people layers aren't read.
- **Where each layer lives:** this encounter's lines and single monsters' own profiles are in the **brief** on the scene (`creatures[g].mind`, `creatures[g].own[n]`), so Rebuild keeps them and a chat can write them with the brief; `cleanBrief` carries `minds` (the schema: 1 until 0.25.0, 2 since 0.26.0). A row's old `tactics` text is read as its Notes when the row has none (so a build from before 0.24.0 still plays its tactics) and is never stored again. Monsters the build didn't place: the scene's `minds` flag (a list of `{ kind, lines }`: a kind's key has dots, which a flag's key can't) and the token's own `mind` flag. A row's `band` is the key of the band it belongs to; bands, their leaders and plans are `docs/features/bands.md` (0.26.0). `MIND_SCHEMA` is 2 since 0.26.0 (the Discipline line; a library entry for a kind of group); data from schema 1 is read as it was, an old `worksWith` line as `discipline`, and a library file from schema 1 imports.
- **A kind's key** (`kindKey`, `kindFacts`): `src:<compendium uuid>` when the creature came from a compendium (a built copy's actor names its source in its flag; a creature read straight from its pack is its own source; a world actor counts by its import record only while it still has the pack creature's name, so a named boss made from a stock creature is its own kind), else `name:<slug>|<level>`. Elite and Weak are the base kind (the prefix off the name, the level put back). A kind names its people explicitly; the module never guesses it.
- **The Minds Library** is one journal (flag `mindsLibrary`), no ownership for anyone, one page per entry with the entry in the page's flag `mind` (`{ schema, type: people | kind | band, key, name, people, lines }`); the page's own text isn't used. Read through a cache that the journal-page hooks drop. Writes replace the flag whole (`==`). "Knows about its enemies" is never kept there. Export is a JSON file (`what: "minds-library"`, the schema); import shows new / the same / different-here and replaces a different one only where the GM ticks it; a file from a newer schema is refused.
- **Whose words:** `applyLines` never lets a write by Angel replace a line whose words are the GM's unless `force`; the skipped lines come back in `skipped`. The writer is who the call says, never who the line says (only a move between layers, Save to library, keeps each line's own). The API is the chat's door: `api.minds.set` and `api.minds.library.put` write as Angel unless told `by: "gm"` (her dictated words), and a brief through `api.submitBrief` has its mind lines and `tactics` stamped Angel's (`stampBrief`). The windows write as the GM. A line is written whole: a pick alone replaces its words too.
- **The packet** (`perceive.mjs`, schema 2): `mind` is the first key after `schema` and the same bytes from turn to turn for as long as what the monster perceives of the creatures its lines name doesn't change; `you.tactics` is gone. `forPacket` writes each line in words (a pick's label, then her words) in fixed order, blank lines left out, with `pick: { mind, discipline }` beside them. Free text goes through `noteForThinker` (the Change note's gate, now with a length; the whole text is read before the cap, and the cap falls at a word's end, so a name is never sent in part): a party-side name becomes that creature's label when the monster perceives it or knows it as an ally; a line naming anyone else, or a name that fits several, is **held back whole** (a held line with a pick still sends the pick). Held lines are saved on the request (`mindHeld`) and the card says so.
- **The prompt** (version 24; 25 since 0.25.0, which adds the notebook; 26 since 0.26.0, which adds Discipline and the band): what each line means, the five Minds and five Disciplines, "never plan above its Mind", the order of who wins, and to ask for the hello again after a summarized chat.
- **Windows** (`ui/minds.mjs`): Monster Minds per scene (the Encounters tools, and `api.minds.open`): build rows, kinds the build didn't place, party members Angel plays; edits sit in a draft until Save (typing never redraws the window: only that line's warning and tag follow, so the next click lands); a line typed back to what is saved is no change; "back to kind's" takes this encounter's line away; clearing a line over something inherited saves it blank on purpose; for a monster's own profile the only thing below is the stat block. A build made again while the window holds edits saves nothing onto the new rows. An open Build window takes the scene's minds into its own copy of the brief whenever they change (`takeMinds`), so its next Build doesn't write old ones back. Minds Library: peoples on the left, one people with its kinds as columns, a cell opens an editor, New people, New kind (by creature search), rename or move, remove, Import, Export.
- **API** (`api.minds`): `rows`, `profile`, `layers`, `of`, `kindOf`, `set(scene, ref, lines, { by, force })` with ref `{ row }`, `{ row, n }`, `{ kind }` or `{ token }`, `giveOwn`, `dropOwn`, `open`, `openLibrary`, and `library.{ list, get, put, remove, export, import }`.

- **0.28.0:** the band's page has a Second select under the Leader (the leader isn't offered; saved with Save band like the mark). The this-fight layer of a mind is written for the first time: when a band falls apart or fights harder the runner writes `discipline` or `givesUp` under the Combat's `minds.own.<token>` (`docs/features/orders.md`), and the windows tag such a line "this fight".

## Traps
- **A kind's key can't be a flag's key:** it has dots (a compendium uuid), which Foundry reads as a path. Per-kind data on a document is a list of `{ kind, … }`.
- **Replacing an object in a flag needs `==`:** a plain update merges, so a line taken away would come back. `{ "flags.<id>.==brief": … }` and `==mind` replace whole (seen on 14.370, 2026-11-10: both the dotted and the nested form).
- **A profile's text may name its own allies' kinds freely:** only party-side creatures on the scene, the world's other characters and the party's other members are run through the name gate (not users: an account's name isn't a creature's). Every word of a character's name of 3 letters or more counts as its name (4 for another party-side creature), case aside, so a party member called by an everyday word holds lines that use that word, or turns the word into its label for a monster that perceives it: the window's warning says which name, and the library's editor asks before saving one. It fails safe. Plurals of a name aren't caught.

## Open items
- A build from before 0.24.0 whose Tactics page was edited by hand: the page is no longer read, only the brief's own tactics text (as Notes). Newlines in Notes go out as spaces.
- A world actor made from a compendium creature and renamed is its own kind by name and level; one not renamed shares the stock creature's kind even if its stat block was changed.
- Two GM pages saving to an empty library at the same moment can make two library journals: both are read, the first to hold a key wins.
- **What a player's browser holds (seen live as the test player, 2026-11-11):** Foundry sends every journal and every scene's flags to every browser. The library journal has no ownership for players, so it isn't in their sidebar and can't be opened, and the windows and the API's writes are GM-only; but a player who opens the browser console can read the library's pages and a scene's brief (this encounter's lines, as they could read a brief's tactics text before 0.24.0). Nothing in Foundry keeps world data from a browser, so secrets don't belong in a profile: "Knows about its enemies" is never in the library for that reason too.
- The card names which lines were held back, not the name that held them (Monster Minds shows it under the line).
- No step for surrender or parley: "Gives up" ends in a `flee` or `none` with the reason said (design review 15, noted for release 3).
- A profile can't hold back an Auto monster's Reactive Strike (design review 15).
