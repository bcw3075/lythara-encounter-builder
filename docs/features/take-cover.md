Take Cover: rule B for when it is allowed, Visioner's per-creature cover, when it ends, PF2e's Effect: Cover as the fallback.

## Decisions
- **Take Cover (2026-11-07):** allowed by **rule B**: prone, a wall or thick trunk next to its space (thin trunks, lesser cover under 3 ft, don't count; she can change that), or standard or greater cover already from a creature it sees. What it does: **Visioner's per-creature cover** (her option 1), set through Visioner's public `setCover(..., { takeCover: true })`: standard or greater becomes greater, less becomes standard, prone with none greater against ranged only. It ends on the monster's own attack or a move off its square, and the runner accepts Visioner's "Take Cover Should End" card itself. PF2e's own Effect: Cover is only the fallback with Visioner off (one bonus against everyone, never ends).

## How it works
- **Going up or down ends it too (0.15.0):** `endsCover` counts a Climb that moved or fell, and a Fly that changed height on its own square (a gap since 0.13.0, found by the 0.15.0 review): leaving its space vertically is leaving its space, as `checkPlan` already assumed.
- **Take Cover (0.5.2).** `{ do: "take-cover" }`, 1 action (Rules: https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2307). Rhea, 2026-11-07:
  - **When it's allowed (her rule B):** prone, or a wall or thick trunk next to its space (`featureNear`: a segment through the ring of squares round it, more than a touch at a corner), or standard or greater cover from a creature it sees and could name. `checkPlan` replays it: not while already Taking Cover (`tookCover`), and a Strike or a move earlier in the plan ends one so it may be taken again; after a move only a wall next to the new square counts, because cover from a square it isn't on yet can't be read. One taken while prone ends on a Stand in the plan. The packet's `you.takeCover` (`taking`, `nextToCover`, `standardCoverFrom` as labels) tells the thinker the same.
  - **Which walls (`nearCoverFor`):** those Visioner gives cover from (its `CoverDetector`, 8.14.17): blocking sight, or set to standard or greater cover. Not an open door, not one set to lesser or none or "no cover" (`provideCover: false`), so Survey Sheet's thin trunks (under 3 ft, lesser) don't count: Rhea's rule. A movement-only wall (a fallen stone with sight none) counts only if it's set to give cover. **Known gap:** an invisible wall round a map's edge blocks sight, so it counts.
  - **What it does: Visioner's per-creature cover (her option 1).** PF2e's own "Effect: Cover" gives one bonus against everyone, takes the higher of it and Visioner's wall cover rather than adding (AC 15 behind a thick trunk was 17 with it at standard), and never ends by itself. So `takeCover` posts PF2e's card (`game.pf2e.actions.get("take-cover").use({ actors, effect: false })`) and, for each creature on the map that isn't its ally and isn't GM-hidden, calls Visioner's public `setCover(attacker, monster, level, { takeCover: true })`, which is what Visioner's own Take Cover calls. `coverAfterTaking` gives the level as Visioner works it out: standard or greater now becomes greater, anything less becomes standard; prone with none gets greater cover against ranged attacks only (Visioner's prone effect, from its own `cover/batch.js`). **GM-hidden tokens are skipped** because Visioner keeps no Take Cover record for a hidden attacker (`AvsOverrideManager.applyOverrides` returns early), so their cover would never end (found by the 0.5.2 review). Afterwards it checks: a `setCover` that returned false, or not Taking Cover at all, goes to the GM.
  - **Visioner off:** PF2e's effect, standard, or greater-prone when prone, with the ChoiceSet's `selection` preset (found by `key`), because `flags.system.rulesSelections` doesn't skip its prompt (tested: the prompt opened). The module flags its own effect with the level (`flags.<id>.takeCover`); a greater-prone one counts only while prone and is deleted on a Stand.
  - **Ending it:** its own Strike (begun or flat check rolled) or a move off its square ends it (`endsCover`). `settleCover` runs after each step, and at the start of a run for a reload; `coverSettled` (saved with `pos`) marks steps already settled, so a Take Cover the GM gives it between steps isn't ended for an earlier Strike. `endTakeCover` deletes the module's own effects, then waits up to 1.5 s for Visioner's "Take Cover Should End" card and accepts it, or ends it directly (`expireTakeCoverForToken`), both from Visioner's own `chat/services/take-cover-expiration-service.js`, imported through `foundry.utils.getRoute` so it's Visioner's very copy. Any second pending card for the token (another GM's browser can post one) is accepted too. It runs inside `watched`. If Visioner's files moved, the note says to remove it in Visioner.
  - **Reload:** `nextCover`: one begun and not recorded goes to the GM, because cover is set creature by creature; not begun and already Taking Cover is done with nothing posted.
  - **Not done:** Visioner's own Take Cover also deletes PF2e's Effect: Cover from the creature and writes an apply cache for its Revert button; this doesn't. Becoming unconscious ends it by Visioner's own hook.
- **Take Cover in the packet (0.9.0):** the ground map marks a square holding a wall Take Cover can use (`takeCoverSquares`: the same walls as `nearCoverFor`, a segment through the square's inside) `T`, anything else blocked `#` (a thin trunk). While it is Taking Cover, `takeCover.standardCoverFrom` is empty (the stealth part keeps the real list, since Taking Cover allows a Hide) and `why` says the cover it has comes from that, because the first run's thinker read its Take Cover's own cover as a reason to take it again.

## Traps
- **Visioner keeps no Take Cover record for a GM-hidden attacker** (`AvsOverrideManager.applyOverrides` returns early), and ending finds attackers only through those records, so cover written against a hidden token never ends: `coverSubjects` skips hidden tokens, as Visioner's own Take Cover does (found by the 0.5.2 review).
- **Visioner's "Take Cover Should End" card is posted by every GM page,** so with AI and the Bridge both online one move gave two pending cards. Accept every pending card for the token, or a later click on a stale one ends a newer Take Cover.
- **PF2e's Effect: Cover opens a level prompt** that nobody can click on the runner: set the ChoiceSet rule's own `selection` (find the rule by `key`); `flags.system.rulesSelections` doesn't skip it. Flag the module's own effect with its level rather than reading PF2e's stored selection.
- **A Take Cover begun before a reload always goes to the GM:** cover is set creature by creature, so "Taking Cover now" doesn't prove it reached them all. Steps already settled for ending it are marked (`coverSettled`, saved with `pos`), or a Take Cover the GM gives between steps would be ended for an earlier Strike.
