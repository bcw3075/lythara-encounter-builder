A monster's turn: the runner, turn requests on the Combat flag, plan legality (`checkPlan`), one action at a time, re-plans, stalls, Auto, Next step.

## Decisions
- **An Angel tick for a party member the GM plays (Rhea, 2026-11-09, q-7):** asked whether Angel could run Aurora with the same tick, she answered "Yes, the option is OK to be there, but always have the default be unticked"; her reactions go to the GM. Built in 0.18.0: a character no player owns is listed in Monster Turns with "(party)" and gets the Angel and Auto ticks, unticked until the GM ticks them. Angel's readings within that: no PC's name to the thinker (its ancestry and class instead), and no flee step for the party's side (leaving the fight is the GM's call). Reactions aren't built for anyone yet; when they are, hers go to the GM.
- **One action at a time, re-planned when something changes (2026-11-06):** the thinker hands in a whole plan, the module carries it out one action at a time, checks before each, and asks again with a fresh packet if anything changed (a reaction, the target down or out of reach or unseen, the monster's conditions or actions, anyone moved). Without Auto, Do it runs one action and becomes **Next step**; with Auto, actions run on their own with a pause (world setting, about 3 s). Rhea chose this over asking afresh every action (3 times the use), "to start with and see how it feels". **As built:** a change is anything about the monster (square, HP, actions this turn, conditions) or any creature it could name when asked (square, how it's perceived, down or not), or the rest of the plan no longer being legal; its own Strikes lowering HP don't count. At most 3 re-plans a turn. A plan can't be replaced once its first step is done.
- Each monster turn waits for Do it, with auto-act per monster. **With Auto ticked the module ends the monster's turn itself, 2 s after its last action (Rhea, 2026-11-07),** never after a card the GM must apply, a stop or Play it myself.
- **Stall check (Rhea, 2026-11-08):** 60 s for machine work (a plan not in, an action running), 3 minutes waiting on a person (a save, a stopped turn, a card to apply on Auto); without Auto, waiting for Next step never stalls. **Told on the card only** (amber), no chat line: her rule "no whispered chat cards" stands (she first picked a whisper and went back when reminded). The thinker gets a `stall` event with no packet and **never the card's note**.
- **Player reactions:** a pop-up that passes on a timer (world setting, default 20 s), and she can always Continue. **The mod assumes a player always has a reaction (2026-11-06):** nothing tracks them and it counts nothing; a player with none left clicks no. **Shield Block goes through that pop-up, before damage,** not a button after it; a late button only if players often miss the timer.
- **The Claude Bridge account runs monster turns, not her browser and not AI, and targets for real on its own account.** If the Bridge is offline, the card says so and waits; a turn never quietly runs in her browser. Each account has its own targets, so hers are never touched. The world setting "Account that runs monster turns" holds the name; the fallback is AI.
**Design-era decisions (from the 2026-11-05/06 plan; the lines above are the skill's summary of the same choices):**
  - **Monster turns:** each one waits for Do it, with auto-act available per monster.
  - **The mod assumes a player always has a reaction (Rhea, 2026-11-06).** PF2e and the installed modules don't track them [LIVE: settings scan and a PC's data; what Visioner's list of available reactions does was not checked], and any count the mod kept would miss reactions used by hand and feats that give extra ones. So it counts nothing. The pop-up appears, and a player with none left clicks no.
  - **Shield Block goes through that pop-up, before damage (Rhea, 2026-11-05).** A button after the damage was weighed and left out: undo at 0 HP leaves Wounded, Prone, Off-Guard and the moved initiative, and later attacks may already have been rolled. Add one later only if players often miss the timer.
  - **One action at a time, re-planned when something changes (Rhea, 2026-11-06, after watching a three-bite turn scroll past too fast to follow).** The thinker still hands in a whole plan for the turn, but the module carries it out one action at a time and checks the situation before each one. If anything changed since the plan was made, the rest is dropped and the thinker is asked again with a fresh packet (a `replan` event). Changes that count: a reaction used against the monster, the target down, dying, gone or no longer perceived or in reach, the monster's own conditions or actions left, the monster or the target moved. **As built (0.4.0):** the monster's square, HP, actions this turn and conditions, and for every creature it could name when asked (not only the target) its square, how it's perceived and whether it's down; then the rest of the plan is checked for legality. A reaction isn't tracked as such: its effect (HP, a condition, a move) is what counts. Its own Strikes lowering someone's HP don't count. At most 3 re-plans a turn, then Rhea plays the rest. On a normal turn that's still one think. Rhea weighed asking afresh after every action (about 3 times the chat use and API cost) and chose this as the compromise.
  - **Between actions (Rhea, 2026-11-06, "to start with and see how it feels"):** without Auto, Do it runs one action and the button becomes **Next step**, so she sees each roll land. With the monster's **Auto** ticked, the actions run on their own with a pause between them (a world setting, about 3 s to start). Hold still stops a running turn.
  - **Seen in the demo:** the wolf bit Test Hero twice more after he dropped, because the plan named him three times and nothing checked. The re-plan check above covers it.
- **The Claude Bridge account runs monster turns (Rhea, 2026-11-05),** not her browser and not the AI account. It targets for real on its own account, so her targets are never touched. If the Bridge is offline, the card says so and waits; a turn never quietly runs in her browser. This reverses the first design, which kept this work off the bridge's page; the section "Who runs a turn" has the reasons and what must be proved first. pf2 will need a Claude Bridge account, which Rhea creates during onboarding.
**Single writer:** the runner moves the state. After a reload it resumes at the last finished step and never repeats a step, using the step log.
**Who runs a turn**
**Decided by Rhea (2026-11-05): the Claude Bridge account.** Foundry's active GM can't be chosen and may be her browser, so the mod names its own runner in a setting. Every other browser leaves turns alone, and her Do it passes the job to the runner through the Combat flag. PF2e's and Workbench's own active-GM automations are unaffected and still run wherever Foundry puts them.
**Why the Bridge:** it's a hidden browser on her server PC that stays joined and rejoins by itself, while the AI account needs the Claude app open and its timers slow down when the pane is hidden. Its targets are its own, so a real target during the Strike touches nothing of hers, clears Workbench's "Please target" message and brings back the Target Helper rows.
**The first design ruled this out for two reasons. Both were tested on 2026-11-05 and can be made safe** [LIVE]:
1. **Wrong scene.** The Bridge opens the fight's map itself in about 1 to 3 seconds, before anything that uses the canvas. A forced sheet redraw and an exploration carry did not move its view, and a Strike during the redraw took under 0.1 s (the plan's line that a map job moves the view was wrong). Activating any scene does pull it along, so it checks again before each attack and damage roll. Viewing leaves a fog record for the Claude Bridge account on each scene it opens; nothing else was seen.
2. **Prompts nobody can click.** A real Modifier dialog on that page does hang the roll for ever. Now each stage has a 20 second limit and stops within a second if a dialog appears; it closes what appeared and hands the turn to Rhea on the card with the window's title, and nothing is repeated.
**Also checked:** the page isn't throttled (`document.hidden` false, 10 timer ticks a second, about 57 frames a second); Dice So Nice doesn't stall it (the roll returns in about 0.1 s) and the AI tab shows its dice; the card's author line shows Claude Bridge; real targets clear the reminder, and AI's own target stayed where it was. Toolbelt's row comes from the roll's target; with automatic damage on, the mod takes it off its own damage cards and hides PF2e's own damage buttons on them from players (Rhea, 2026-11-05). Others see the Bridge's target mark come and go on the token.
**If it fails:** the fallback is the AI account, by changing the world setting "Account that runs monster turns".

- **Ticking Auto runs a plan already waiting (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked whether ticking Auto while a plan waits on the card should start it, she answered "Auto should start the run as if it was checked from the beginning". Built in 0.17.0: `onTokenFlag` runs a `ready` plan (before its first step, or between steps for Next step) when Auto is ticked on the creature whose turn it is, through `pausedAuto` and `doIt` like Auto's own start; a held, stopped or running turn is left alone, and a finished one isn't ended (Auto ending the turn happens only for a turn Auto ran). A pause for the GM to apply an ability's effects (`gmWait` on the request, cleared when the turn runs again) waits for her Next step, as it would on Auto. The request is read again after the game's pause, and runs only if it is still ready at the same step (the same check now guards Auto's own start in `judgeNow`) (0.17.0 review).
- **The Change button (Rhea, 2026-11-09, 0.19.0):** the runner's `act` takes a GM's note or edit (`ask`, `edit` on the request) before a `go`, and `runTurn` clears a refused change's line when the turn runs again, as `submitPlan` does for a new plan; a note is a re-plan that isn't counted. Since 0.19.0 `checkPlan` refuses a Strike at an ally's label in its own words ("is on its side"), not as one it doesn't know of. Details: `docs/features/change.md`.
- **The game's pause pauses Auto (Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Pause would pause all actions").** While `game.paused`, Auto starts no action (`pausedAuto` waits before Auto's own start of an accepted plan and before each step Auto continues to, and stops the turn if Hold, Play it myself or a turn change arrive meanwhile) and ends no turn (`endAfter` waits before `nextTurn`), and carries on when the GM unpauses. A GM's own press (Do it, Next step) still runs the step it was pressed for while paused: she asked for it; Auto then waits before the next one. (First build checked the pause at the top of every step, which held the GM's own press too; seen live 2026-11-08.) The card says "The game is paused: Auto waits until you unpause." (redrawn on `pauseGame`). Until 0.11.0 Auto acted and ended turns through a pause.
## How it works
- **Who Angel can run (0.18.0):** `runnable` gates every turn hook (a turn's start, the turn after, a fall, a sink note): a monster (any NPC, on either side) or a party member the GM plays (`partyMember`: a character no player owns, on the party's side; a villain built as a character isn't one). Both need the token's `run` flag (the Angel tick), which nothing but the GM's click ever sets; a party member's starts unticked. From there its turn is a monster's turn: the same packet (with `you.side`, see `docs/features/packet.md`), plan contract, checks and card, with sides from PF2e's `isEnemyOf`/`isAllyOf` from the acting creature. A party member can't plan a flee (`checkPlan`'s `party`: the module's escape flow, hidden and "XP not earned", is a monster's). A dying or unconscious party member gets no request (its recovery check is the GM's; every plan would be refused). Its ticks last one fight: `untickParty` (the runner, on `deleteCombat`) clears its Angel and Auto ticks, so it never comes up ticked in the next fight.
- **Burrow (0.16.0):** the turn-change chain runs `settleBurrowers` after `settleClimbers` (a token the GM lifted back to the surface is shown and unflagged). `judgeNow` applies a plan's `ground` judgment before checking the plan (`docs/features/moves.md`).
- **Climbers at each turn change (0.15.0):** after the fall and the sink note, `settleClimbers` (moves) takes the climbing flag and the module's Off-Guard off any token no longer up its tree. The fall itself skips a creature up a tree (`clinging`). `runTurn` dispatches `climb` to `runClimb` and `release` to `runRelease`; `checkPlan` and `gather` carry the Climb fields (`docs/features/moves.md`).
- **The fall at the end of a turn (0.13.0):** `onCombatUpdate` reads `Combat#previous` on a turn change before anything awaits and runs `fallIfAloft` (moves) before the senses and the next turn's start, so the next creature's packet sees the flier where it landed.
- **The sink note at the end of a turn (0.14.0):** after the fall, `onCombatUpdate` runs `sinkNote` (moves) with the same `Combat#previous`, so a monster left in deep water without a successful Swim gets its note on the card before the next turn starts.
- **Interact and Delay (0.12.0):** `runTurn` dispatches `interact` to `runInteract` (its `gm` result takes the existing "Then press Next step" path) and `delay` to `runDelay`, whose `delayed` result marks the request done with no `endAfter`, because the Delay ended the turn itself. `gather` adds `grabbed`, `fresh` (nothing spent, done or begun this turn) and `order` (`turnOrder`) for `checkPlan`. `onTurnChange`'s guard ignores a request marked `delayed` (the monster is back for a new turn, marked `afterDelay`), and `endLeftOver` skips `delayed` and `gmEnded` requests (a turn whose last step left a card for the GM is never ended by Auto: Rhea's rule, which the 10 s check broke since 0.9.1). Details: `docs/features/interact-delay.md`.
- **Fleeing off the map (0.11.0):** a `flee` step is run like a Stride; at its edge square `runTurn` saves the request `fled` and stops, Auto or not, and `endAfter` isn't reached. The GM's buttons, the `extend` flag the runner acts on from `act`, and the re-ask after Extend are in `docs/features/fleeing.md`. New request states: `fled` (waiting on the GM), `extending` (busy). Also a stopped-short flee re-plans even though it is the last step.
- **Combat:** `turns.requests`, one per monster turn: `state`, `combatantId`, `tokenId`, `round`, `turn`, `labels` (E1 and so on, to token ids: only what it perceived at the time), `packet`, `plan`, `by` (who made the plan: route, model, generation, usage), `tries`, `gaveUp`, `steps`, `verdict`, `note`, `go`, `hold`, and `log` by step; `pos` (the next step), `seen` (what the plan rests on, taken with the packet); on a re-plan also `spent` and `attacks` (what earlier requests this turn used), `done` (what it did, in labels), `replans`, `why` (the changes, as data) and `parent`, and the old request gets `replanned`. `turns.events`, by number: `seq`, `kind` (`turn`, `retry` or `replan`), `requestId`, `tries`. `turns.labels`, token id to label, kept for the whole fight. `turns.memory`, by monster token then enemy token: `square` and `round` it last perceived them. `turns.grips`, by monster token then target token: `round`, a grip its own Grab made (for Grab's hold). On a request, `rollFor` (token id: true) is a GM's Roll it for them for a save still waiting. It all goes with the fight.
- **On a request (0.9.0):** `since` (server clock: when it last moved on), `stall` (`kind`, `state`, `since`, `waited`); events of kind `stall` carry `since`.
- **The runner does the work.** The `runner` setting names one GM account, Claude Bridge by default. Only that account's page creates requests, judges plans and carries out turns. Any other GM's Do it writes `go` on the request and the runner picks it up from `updateCombat`. If the runner is offline or doesn't exist, `runnerProblem` gives the words, the card shows them, Do it is disabled, and nothing runs anywhere else. Foundry's active GM can't be chosen, which is why the module names its own. PF2e's and Workbench's own automations still run on the active GM.
- **A turn starts:** the runner sees `turn` or `round` change in `updateCombat`, because `pf2e.startTurn` fires on the active GM only. Also when a monster is ticked during its own turn, and when the runner's page loads. A short wait, then a request in state `requested`. How long start-of-turn effects really need is still to be measured.
- **By round and combatant, not turn number.** Workbench moves a PC's initiative at 0 HP, which changes `combat.turn` in the middle of a monster's turn. `currentOf` and the guard in `onTurnChange` use the combatant so that doesn't start a second request or hide the card.
- **The scene:** `ensureScene` opens the fight scene on the runner before anything that uses the canvas, because activating any scene pulls that page along and Visioner answers "observed" for tokens it can't find. A view takes about 1 to 3 s. Map jobs (a sheet redraw, the exploration carry) don't move the view and a Strike during one wasn't slowed (measured 2026-11-05). If it can't open, the card says so.
- **The plan:** any GM may call `api.fight.submitPlan`; the runner's `judge` checks it. `ready` shows the card.
- **One action at a time (Rhea, 2026-11-06).** `runTurn` carries out one step, saves `pos`, then: without Auto it stops at `ready` and the card's button reads **Next step**; with Auto it waits `autoPause` seconds (Hold and Play it myself end the wait at once) and goes on. She chose this after a three-bite turn scrolled past too fast to follow.
- **Look again before each action (`lookAgain`).** `seen` is taken with the packet, because that's what the plan was made from. Before any step that hasn't begun, `changesSince` compares it with now: the monster's square, HP (with temporary HP), actions this turn and conditions; and for each creature it could name, its square, how it's perceived and whether it's down (no HP, dying, unconscious or dead). The monster's own Strikes lowering someone's HP don't count, but someone going down does. Then `checkPlan` runs on the rest of the plan with what's left. **Anything at all → `replan`:** the request ends (`done`, `replanned`), and a new one for the same turn gets a fresh packet with `actionsLeft` less what's spent, `attacksMade`, `done` and a `note`, its own `seen`, and a `replan` event. The multiple attack penalty carries on through `attacks`. No actions left ends the turn with a note; a fourth re-plan in one turn (`MAX_REPLANS`) goes to the GM. Notes stay with the turn: Next step keeps a note from an earlier step (only a held or stopped turn's note is cleared when it goes on), and after the turn the card shows the notes of every part of a re-planned turn (`latestNote` follows `parent`). A step begun before a reload carries on from its log and is never re-planned halfway. A reaction isn't tracked as such: what it does (HP, conditions, a move) is what counts.
- **The turn stays with the monster when it drops a PC (`keepTurn`, 0.4.4).** Workbench moves a PC at 0 HP to just ahead of whoever's turn it is, in its `preUpdateActor` hook, so on the page that applied the damage: the runner's. PF2e's `setMultipleInitiatives` then sets `turn` to keep the current combatant, but that page's own copy has often already shifted, so the update finds nothing to send and the server keeps the old turn number: the PC became current mid-turn and the monster's end of turn fired (2026-11-06: 2 in 4 real turns with AI live as active GM, 3 in 3 with the Bridge itself as active GM). On the runner's page, whether or not it's the active GM, `keepTurn` (a `preUpdateCombatant` hook) adds core's own `combatTurn` (where the current combatant will sit, from `_sortCombatants` on clones with the change) and `turnEvents: false`, as core's `rollInitiative` does, so every page keeps the same creature current with no turn event. **Core applies `combatTurn` only in each page's copy and never saves it** (the server kept the old number and every page fell back to it), so `saveTurn` (an `updateCombatant` hook on the same page) then saves the turn with `diff: false`, because the runner's own copy already matches. Tested by hand before the release: real monster turns lost the turn 2 times in 4 without it, and 6 in 6 kept it with both hooks; AI's page fired no turn event.
  - **Can't act at all** (`cantAct`): dead, paralyzed, petrified, unconscious. Every step but `none` is refused. **Can't use move actions** (`cantMove`: Stride, Step, Crawl, Stand and Drop Prone): restrained, grabbed, immobilized (named in that order, because PF2e adds Immobilized with them; Immobilized bars every action with the move trait). **Prone and no land Speed are `checkPlan`'s,** because a Stand or Drop Prone earlier in the plan changes it: `gather` passes `prone`, and the replay refuses a Stride or Step while it's prone and a Crawl while it isn't. Speed is `system.movement.speeds.land.value`.
  - **A look that fails** (an error while checking the next step) stops the turn with a note instead of leaving it "running".
  - **One save for a step's end:** the step's `log` record and the baseline's change (`seen.me.square` after a move, `seen.me.conditions` after a Stand or Drop Prone) go in the same save, so a reload between them can't leave the step done with the old baseline and a re-plan for its own action (found by the 0.5.1 review; 0.5.0 saved them apart).
- **Restrained can't Strike** (`cantAttack`): Restrained bars attack and manipulate actions except Escape and Force Open (Player Core). Found by the review of 0.5.0; Strikes before that ignored it.
- **Auto ends the turn (0.9.0, Rhea, 2026-11-07).** With the monster's Auto ticked, `endAfter` calls `combat.nextTurn()` `END_MS` (2 s) after the turn finished by itself (Auto read again when the timer fires): its last step done, or a re-plan with no actions left. Not after a card the GM must apply (`gm`), a stop, Play it myself, a turn handed to the GM after too many re-plans, nor if the turn has moved on or another request for it is unfinished. One per round and combatant (`ending`). The timer lives on the runner's page, so a reload in those 2 s lost it (the second measured run): the runner's 10 s stall check also ends a current creature's latest request that is done with Auto ticked (`endLeftOver`).
- **The stall check (0.9.0, Rhea, 2026-11-08).** `save` stamps `since` (server clock) whenever a request's `state`, `pos` or `log` changes, and new requests and a retry get one. `watchStalls` runs `checkStalls` on the runner every 10 s: `stallOf` (brain.mjs) flags the current request when it has sat 60 s in machine work (`requested`, `submitted`, `running`) or 3 minutes waiting on a person (`saves`, `stopped`, or `ready` with Auto, which means a card to apply); `ready` without Auto (Next step) and `held` never. A `requested` turn with no packet, or refused for the last time, waits on the GM, not the thinker, so it counts as a person wait worded "waiting for you to play the turn"; the machine limit grows with a long Auto pause. It writes `stall` (with the `since` it is about) and a `stall` event. Her choices: the limits; told on the card only (amber, "Stalled: …, for N minutes. Angel has been asked to look into it."), no chat line, because of her rule against whispered cards. The mark clears itself: any progress moves `since` on, and both the card and `stallLive` compare the two. The event carries no packet and **never the card's note** (it can name PCs and what the monster can't know: found by the review): the turn's state, step and refusal, in labels.
- **`api.fight.busy()`** says whether a turn is being worked on or about to be, for tools that share the runner's page (bridge 0.2.0). It counts `requestBusy` on every started combat and, on the runner's page only, the in-memory `starting`, `judging` and `running` sets, which cover the start of a turn before any request exists. Waiting on a person or a plan doesn't count.
- **Who sees it:** players' browsers do receive the Combat flag, plans included (read as Test Player). Accepted among friends; nothing shows in their UI.
- **One run at a time:** `judge` and `doIt` each guard by request id in memory. With Auto on, the `updateCombat` hook and the submitter both reached `judge` and rolled the first attack twice, which the D1 matrix didn't catch because it didn't use Auto. D1 now includes it.
- **Never twice:** `save` writes each stage to `log` before moving on, and `nextAction` reads it. `started` with no attack, `damageStarted` with no card, or `applying` with no `applied` means it can't tell, so the card stops and asks. A reload at any point resumes from there through Do it.
- **The `lythara-encounter-builder.step` hook** fires before each stage; return false to stop there. The reload tests use it.
- **Saves use `{ render: false }`** so each stage doesn't redraw the tracker. Whether that's fully quiet with PF2e HUD open isn't checked yet. **Saves send only the change:** two browsers write to a request now, so a whole copy could put back what the other just wrote.
- **A reload:** on the runner's `ready`, a request still `running` becomes `stopped` with a note, and Do it carries on from the record. A `go` or a plan left waiting is picked up.
**Design-era text (from the plan; where it and the lines above disagree, the lines above, written from the code, win):**
**Turn flow**
| State | What happens |
|---|---|
| `idle` | Turn starts on an unticked monster: nothing |
| `settling` | Ticked monster: wait for start-of-turn effects to finish. Core runs turn events on the active GM only, and `pf2e.startTurn` fires from them [CODE], so that hook won't fire on the runner when Rhea is the active GM: the runner has to notice the turn change itself, from the Combat update. Workbench's handling isn't awaited by anything, so a short delay plus a recheck. **[UNVERIFIED] how long is enough** |
| `requested` | Packet written. Timer starts, 60 s by default: "Keep waiting / Play it myself" |
| `ready` | Plan checked; card shows steps in plain words, reason, warnings |
| `running` | One step at a time; recheck before each step |
| `reaction` | Waiting on a player or Rhea |
| `done` | Summary. Rhea presses End Turn, or it advances if auto-act is on |
| `manual` | Rhea plays. The mod stays out of the way |
**Legality check**
A pure function that replays the plan against a copy of the state and returns OK or the first failing step with a plain reason. Live values are gathered first by the glue on the runner.
| Check | How |
|---|---|
| Actions left | 3, plus quickened, less slowed and stunned [RULES]. PF2e doesn't track NPC actions, so the mod does. Costs from `system.actions.value` [LIVE] |
| Can act at all | Paralyzed, unconscious and so on. Immobilized bars move actions; grabbed gives immobilized, and manipulate needs a DC 5 flat check [RULES] |
| Distance | `actor.getReach({action:"attack"})` (10 on a Dire Wolf [LIVE]) and `token.distanceTo(target)`. Range increments for ranged |
| Movement cost | **Built 0.4.6 to 0.4.9:** the module finds the route (Foundry has none), Foundry prices it; the price equalled a real move's charge on every route tested, once the diagonal count runs across the turn as the rules say. ## Traps
- **Test trap: Workbench puts Prone and Wounded on a character coming out of Dying** (0.18.0 live test): a restored test character is still prone, and every move is refused for that first. Remove them before testing moves.
- Hooks and the caller can both reach `judge` and `doIt`: keep the per-request guards. That double run only showed with Auto, so test every route, not just the button.
- `pf2e.startTurn` fires on Foundry's active GM only, so the runner watches `updateCombat` itself. In PF2 Test AI is the active GM over the Bridge, which makes AI's tab a stand-in for Rhea's browser.
- **Closing an AI window can leave AI showing online with nothing behind it** (seen several times, cause not found). A relaunch of PF2 Test clears it. It doesn't stop the pane logging in as AI.
- **A PC at 0 HP changes `combat.turn` in the middle of a monster's turn,** because Workbench moves their initiative. Requests are keyed on round and combatant for that reason.
- **Two browsers write to a request,** so `save` sends only the change. A whole copy could put back what the other just wrote.
- **A scene activation can beat the runner's own view,** so `ensureScene` looks again. Applying damage needs no canvas, so it doesn't wait for one.
- **A flag update merges objects,** so a new plan saved over an old one would keep the old one's keys: plans and `by` go through `foundry.data.operators.ForcedReplacement` (`whole`). Arrays are replaced anyway.
- **A stored request doesn't carry its own id.** `request(combat, id)` returns the flag's object; only `currentRequest` adds `id`. 0.4.0 wrote a re-plan's end under the key "undefined" because `replan` read `r.id`. Pass the id along.
- **Next step must not clear the card's note.** `runTurn` used to clear it at every start, which wiped a 0 HP undo warning one press later. Only a held or stopped turn's note is cleared (0.4.3).
- **A monster dropping a PC passed the turn on (fixed in 0.4.4 and 0.4.5, Rhea chose fixing it on the Bridge's page).** Workbench moves the PC's initiative from its `preUpdateActor` hook, on the page that applied the damage: the runner's. PF2e's `setMultipleInitiatives` then tries to keep the turn, but that page's copy has often already shifted, so its update sends nothing: real monster turns lost the turn 2 in 4 with AI live as active GM and 3 in 3 with the Bridge itself as active GM (a plain HP change or AI applying never failed, so test with real turns). The card lost the turn and the monster's end of turn fired. Fix: `keepTurn` gives the change `combatTurn` and `turnEvents: false`; **core applies `combatTurn` only in each page's copy and never saves it**, so `saveTurn` then saves the turn with `diff: false`. 0.4.4 skipped the active-GM case, which still failed; 0.4.5 always acts on the runner. Workbench only moves anyone when the page has the fight scene in view (`game.combat`).
- **What the look before each action ignores must be exactly what the monster's own action changed.** A Stride moves `seen.me.square`; a Stand or Drop Prone puts into `seen.me.conditions`, through `absorbOwn`, only the Prone and Off-Guard change it made, never a fresh copy of all conditions, or a condition a reaction added would be swallowed (found by the 0.5.0 and 0.5.1 reviews). An area that hurts its own caster moves `seen.me.hp` in the same save as the applied record.
- **A step's record and its baseline change go in one save.** Saved apart, a reload between them leaves the step done with the old baseline, and the next look re-plans for the monster's own action (found by the 0.5.1 review; 0.5.0 had it).
- **Test trap: `submitPlan` keeps the request's `log`.** A log entry set by hand for a test stays under the next plan's step of the same index (a Step then "started and isn't where it was going"). Test on a fresh turn.
- **Test trap: moving tokens after a turn's packet makes Do it re-plan** (the look before the action sees them moved). Submit again on the new request.
- **Timers on the runner's page die with a reload:** Auto's end-of-turn timer did (second measured run); anything the runner must do later needs a check that survives (`endLeftOver` in the 10 s stall check).
