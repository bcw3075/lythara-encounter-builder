Bands (monster minds release 3, tier 3a): a group's nature, who leads it and a plan written at build, sent to each member by its Discipline; the band knows at once when its leader falls.

## Decisions
Rhea, 2026-11-11 (R3-1 to R3-13; the design and its review against the code are in the project's design doc; this is what tier 3a builds of them):
1. **Four tiers, each released and tested on its own (R3-9):** 3a group nature, the leader mark and a band plan written at build, with no new step, no chat line and no change to perception; 3b the leader's orders in the fight; 3c speech to the party and yielding; 3d Command an Animal, Point Out and side changes.
2. **Discipline is a five-step ladder (R3-1), her own words on top, a fixed pick underneath because the module acts on it:** None (no plan, shares nothing); Pack (no orders and no plan, but they work off what they see each other do); Loose (the plan while it suits them; their own mind wins when the two pull apart); Trained (the plan wins until their own "Gives up" is reached); Drilled (the plan wins even at their own cost).
3. **Discipline is set per kind of creature; the band has none of its own (R3-11).** She chose this over a band default with exceptions: goblins are Loose everywhere unless changed, and one encounter can change it like any mind line. Animals are Pack.
4. **If the leader falls: five outcomes to pick from (R3-2),** per band, her words on top: Next in line takes over; Plan stands, nobody changes it; Falls apart; Breaks and runs; Fights harder.
5. **When the leader falls, the band knows at once (R3-10),** as allies are already known by default. (The first design had "only once a member perceives it"; today's packets tell every monster its allies' state through walls, so that would have been untrue. If spread-out fights feel wrong in play, what a monster knows of its own side becomes its own change.)
6. **Each band's plan says what "fall back" means (R3-12):** a rally spot on the map (the band pulls back there and keeps fighting; leaving the map stays each monster's own "Gives up") or off the map. Angel proposes it at build from the map and the brief; she changes it in the Monster Minds window. A rally spot is a place, so every member may be told it.
7. **A whole band running (R3-13)** is in `docs/features/fleeing.md`, decision 12.

Settled by Angel, told to Rhea, hers to change:
- **Discipline took the place of "Works with others"** (design review 17: no two homes for one thing). The line keeps its place in the profile; its four picks are read as the nearest Discipline (On its own: None; With its pack: Pack; Follows a leader's plan and Is the leader: Loose). Who leads is the leader mark on the band, never a pick on a kind.
- **The stat-block guess:** Mindless: None; an animal: Pack; a trained tactician: Trained; anything else that thinks: Loose. Drilled is never guessed.
- **A band exists only where it is named:** a build row names its band, or a monster the build didn't place is put in one. Monsters in no band play as before, by their own minds. Angel names the bands when she writes a brief.
- **The leader** is the monster marked, else the module's guess, shown as "Angel's pick": the highest-level member that follows a leader (Loose, Trained or Drilled), one that can speak before one that can't. A band of None and Pack creatures has no leader. **Fallen** means down, dead or fled; a marked leader whose token is gone from the map counts as gone.
- **Next in line** is the highest-level member still standing that follows a leader; a named second goes first (0.28.0: `second` on the band, set on its page in Monster Minds; `docs/features/orders.md`).
- **What each Discipline is sent:** None nothing of the band, and nothing of what its allies did or how its side stands; Pack the band's way of fighting, how its members stand toward each other and what they want, with no leader and no plan; Loose, Trained and Drilled all of it. How firmly the plan holds is said once, in the prompt.
- **Who wins when they pull apart** (design reviews 11 and 17): the rules and its conditions; what the GM asks for this turn; Notes; the band's plan as far as its Discipline holds it; the rest of its mind. Orders take their place above the plan in tier 3b.
- **The brief's "what they want" and "how they retreat"** fill a band's "What they want" and when it falls back wherever the band itself says nothing (design review 17: they reached only the journal before).
- **In 3a nothing in the fight changed a band:** the plan was what was agreed before the fight, and succession, "Falls apart" and "Fights harder" were only told to the thinker in words. Since 0.28.0 the leader's orders change the plan for those who hear them, and the module acts on a leader's fall (`docs/features/orders.md`).
- **The jobs of a plan are per kind of creature** (a build row, or a kind the build didn't place), so a member is told its own job and the others' by kind.

## How it works
- **The rules** (`bands.mjs`, pure): a band is `{ key, name, lib, leader, nature, plan: { objective, jobs, fallBack } }`. `key` is the name with letters, digits and dashes only (a flag's key can't hold a dot). A piece of text is `{ pick?, text, by, at? }` like a mind's line. `nature` has five lines (`NATURE`), of which only "If the leader falls" has picks (`FALLS`). `leader` is `{ row, n, by }` (a build row and the monster's number in it) or `{ token, by }`. `jobs` is a list of `{ row | kind, text, by }`. `fallBack` is `{ to: "spot" | "off", at: [x, y], by, when }`. Caps: 200 characters a line, 120 a job, 160 for the when, 8 other kinds' jobs sent; the per-line caps bound the block a packet carries: a unit test builds the largest and checks it stays under 3,600 characters.
- **Where bands live** (`bandlib.mjs`): in the scene's **brief** (`bands`, beside `creatures`), so a Rebuild keeps them and a chat writes them with the brief; a band made on a map with no brief is kept in the scene's `bands` flag, is still read after the map gets a brief (a key in both is the brief's), and moves into the brief when a build row joins it. Each band is written back where it lives. A build row belongs to the band whose key its `band` holds; a monster the build didn't place to the band its token's `band` flag names. For a monster of the standing build, membership and the leader are read through the brief (`builtRef`), not from the copy the builder puts on its token (design review 4); a token `builtRef` doesn't know (hand-placed, or left from an older build) goes by its own flag. A leader mark or a job must point at a row of its own band, or `cleanBrief` drops it. `cleanBrief` makes rows and bands agree: a band a row names is made, a row's `band` is stored as the key, and a brief from before 0.26.0 with `leader: true` on a row gives that band the row's first monster as leader.
- **The nature's layers** (`natureOf`): this fight (the Combat's `bands` flag; since 0.28.0 the runner writes `fell`, `leader`, `planGone` and `objective` there when a leader falls, never a nature line), this encounter's band, the kind of group in the Minds Library the band starts from (`lib`), the brief's own "what they want". For each line the first that speaks wins. A kind of group is a library entry of type `band` holding `nature` and no profile lines.
- **Whose words** (`applyBand`, `applyNature`): as for a mind's lines, a write by Angel never replaces a line, a leader mark or a fall-back choice of the GM's unless `force`; what was skipped comes back by name.
- **The packet** (`bandForPacket`, `bandPacket`; schema 4): `band` is the key right after `mind`: `pick`, `leader` (a label, or "you"), the nature's lines in words, and `plan: { objective, yourJob, jobs, fallBack }`. Its free text goes through the same name gate as a mind's lines; a held line's key joins the request's `mindHeld` as `band.<key>` and the card names it. The leader is named only by a label the monster already has (itself, an ally it is told of), so a GM-hidden leader stays unnamed. `bandNow` comes after the notebook and only once the leader has fallen: `{ leader: "Its leader is down." }`, with `leadsNow` for "Next in line takes over". Both blocks are left out for a creature on the party's side.
- **No Discipline shares nothing:** `memory.mjs` `forPacket` takes `shares: false` and leaves out `alliesDid` and `side`; the Remembers fold-out shows the same.
- **The map grows:** `shiftBandSpots` moves every rally spot with the content when an extension shifts the squares' numbers.
- **Windows** (`ui/minds.mjs`): Monster Minds lists the scene's bands under **Bands** with **New band…**. A kind's page has a **Band** select (changed at once) and the Discipline line. A band's page shows who is in it by kind with their Disciplines, the **Leader** select (the first choice is Angel's pick), **Starts from** (a kind of group in the library), the five nature lines with a tag for where each comes from, and the plan: objective, one job a kind, fall back (a rally spot with **Pick on the map**, or off the map) and when. What is typed sits in a draft until **Save band**, and only what differs from what the page showed is written, so nothing she didn't change becomes hers. **Save nature to library…** moves chosen lines into a kind of group and makes the band start from it. The Minds Library has **Kinds of group** with **New kind of group…**, cell edits, rename and remove.
- **API** (`api.minds.bands`): `list(scene)` (every band with its members, leader, nature and plan), `of(scene, token)`, `set(scene, key, change, { by, force })` (as Angel unless `by: "gm"`), `join(scene, ref, key)` with ref `{ row }`, `{ kind }` or `{ token }`, `remove(scene, key)`. A brief through `api.submitBrief` carries `bands` and has their lines stamped Angel's.

- **0.28.0 (tier 3b, `docs/features/orders.md`):** a band has `second` (cleaned like the leader mark, never the leader itself, dropped with its row or its token); `leaderOf` reads the one who took over in this fight first (`fightBand(combat, key).leader`); `planOf` is the plan as this fight left it (none after `planGone`, the new `objective` after Fights harder); `bandPacket` still tells the band its first leader fell once another leads; `jobGroups` names each kind in a band by the id an order uses; `langsOf` reads a sheet's languages. `bandViews` carries `second` and whether the leader took over.

## Traps
- **A band's key can't hold a dot** (a flag's key is read as a path): it is the name's letters, digits and dashes. A kind's key does have dots, so jobs for kinds the build didn't place are a list, never an object keyed by kind.
- **Bands point at build rows by number.** A row taken out in the Build window moves the marks behind it (`rowRemoved`); the Build window takes the scene's bands into its own copy only while its rows are still the scene's, creature for creature.
- **Asleep is not fallen.** A monster built asleep has Unconscious with its hit points whole; "down" for a leader is 0 HP or Dying (the 0.26.0 review found a sleeping boss told to its band as fallen).
- **The guessed leader is counted over every member, fallen or not.** Counted over the living only, the guess would move to the next monster the moment the leader dropped and nobody would be told the leader fell.
- **The two rule files import each other** (`minds.mjs` cleans a library entry's nature with `bands.mjs`; `bands.mjs` uses the mind rules' helpers): neither may use the other's exports while the file is first being read, only inside functions.

## Open items
- Angel's pick of a leader moves on if the fallen leader's token is deleted from the map during the fight (a marked leader reads "gone" instead): mark the leader where that matters.
- Monsters the build didn't place join a band token by token: ones dragged in later are in no band until the kind's Band select is set again (it then says only some of them are in one).
- In an open Build window with rows added or taken out in the middle, a band's marks can point at the wrong row of the same band until the window is reopened (they are dropped when they point outside the band).
- A leader the GM has hidden is never named or reported fallen to its band; if it dies while hidden the band isn't told.
- A nature line can't be left blank on purpose over a library line (a mind's line can): clearing it shows the library's again.
- The plan card and the History don't show the band block a monster was sent; the Monster Minds window is where to read it.
- A band made of several kinds with different Disciplines is told one plan; each member weighs it by its own Discipline (the design's answer to mixed bands).
- "Can speak" for the leader guess reads the creature's languages off its sheet; telepathy and a creature with languages it can't speak aren't told apart (the same list decides who hears an order since 0.28.0: orders, open items).
- A monster moved to the party's side mid-fight is no longer in its band for the module (a creature on the party's side belongs to none); what its old band is told is tier 3d's.
