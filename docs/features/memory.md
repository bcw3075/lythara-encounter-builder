Fight memory: a notebook per monster for the fight (who hurt it, what it tried that failed, who heals and casts, what it worked out), its allies' deeds this round and its side's tally, the Remembers fold-out and the GM's own lines. Monster minds release 2 of 4.

## Decisions
Rhea, 2026-11-11 (each answered "A" to a question with three choices; the design and its independent review are in the project's design doc):
1. **She sees it:** a fold-out "Remembers" on the plan card and the same in the History window, so she can check why a monster changed its target and catch a wrong notebook.
2. **She can correct it:** she can cross a line out and add a line of her own in a few words. Her lines are marked hers, go through the name gate, and the monster thinks again if it is mid-turn. Angel never changes or removes a line of hers.
3. **How much a monster works out depends on its Mind:** Mindless nothing; Animal who hurt it and who hurt it last; Simple also what it tried that failed; Cunning also who heals and who throws magic; Trained tactician also the finer things it saw (who strikes when you walk away, who blocked with a shield, what hurt one of them far more than it should, what did little).
4. **Weakness and resistance of an enemy** are kept though they are facts off a player's sheet, because she chose it: for a Trained tactician only, in words only, and only from an outcome the monster perceived, never read from the sheet ahead of time.

Settled by Angel, told to Rhea, hers to change:
- Kept for every creature that isn't a player's, ticked for Angel or not. A party member Angel plays with no written Mind gets the Cunning rung.
- Only what it perceived at the moment the effect landed; words, never numbers; magic is what it visibly did, not the spell's name, unless the monster has that same spell itself.
- Each line is sure or unsure by how the module knows it; nothing is guessed.
- The ladder is enforced by the module: the notebook stores what the monster perceived, the packet carries only the kinds of line its Mind may use.
- Lasts this fight only.

## The source table (live probe, 2026-11-11, PF2e 8.7.0, Foundry 14.370, Toolbelt 3.60.0)
What was done in the test world, as the test player and as a GM by real clicks on the cards, and what each left behind. "The card" below is PF2e's **damage-taken** card: `flags.pf2e.context.type === "damage-taken"`, posted by whoever pressed Apply, its speaker the creature that took it.

| What happened | What PF2e records | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| A player's Strike, hit or miss | The attack card: `context.type "attack-roll"`, `context.target.token` (uuid), `context.outcome`, `context.origin.token` and `flags.pf2e.origin.actor` (the attacker), `origin.type` weapon or melee | sure |
| Its damage applied from the card (Toolbelt's row or PF2e's button) | The card with `flags.pf2e.origin` (attacker's actor uuid and item uuid) and `appliedDamage` (`isHealing`, `shield`, `persistent`, and `updates`: what it took off HP and temporary HP, kept for a revert). The HP update comes **before** the card | sure |
| A weakness, resistance or immunity applied | Only in the card's HTML: `<span class="iwr" data-applications='[{"category":"weakness"\|"resistance"\|"immunity","type":"slashing","adjustment":5}]'>` (GM-visible). Nothing in the flags | sure |
| Damage brought to nothing (resisted away, immune) | The card with `appliedDamage: null`, the `iwr` span, and **no** HP update | sure |
| A spell cast | A card with `flags.pf2e.casting` and `origin.type "spell"` (item uuid: its traits, area and slug are on the item) | sure |
| A spell attack | An attack card as for a Strike, `context.action "cast-a-spell"` | sure |
| A save spell's damage | The damage card has `context.sourceType "save"`; the save itself is rolled on Toolbelt's row with **no chat card**: `flags.pf2e-toolbelt.targetHelper.saveVariants.null.saves[tokenId].success` arrives as an update of the damage card. Then the card per target, with the spell as origin | sure |
| An area spell | Nothing says area on the cards (Toolbelt's `area` was null with targets picked by hand); the spell item has `system.area`. Each creature caught gets its own card | sure per creature |
| Healing applied | The card with `appliedDamage.isHealing: true` and the healer's spell as origin; posted by the player who applied it | sure |
| Persistent damage starting | The card with `appliedDamage.persistent: [condition id]`; the condition item itself names no source (`origin` is its bearer) | sure when a card with an origin started it |
| Persistent damage at the end of its turn | PF2e rolls and applies it on the active GM's page: the card's `origin.type "condition"`, its actor the bearer | sure (what, not who) |
| Shield Block | Part absorbed: `appliedDamage.shield`. All absorbed: `appliedDamage: null` and only the card's words say so. The module's own reaction answers say it for a monster's Strike | sure through the reaction answer |
| A companion's or summon's attack | The same cards as a Strike, speaker and origin the companion's own token | sure |
| HP typed by hand | An HP update only (it carries PF2e's `damageTaken` like any other, so that proves nothing), no card | unsure |
| A condition put on by hand (Frightened) | The condition item, its `origin` the bearer: no source | none: her own line |
| The module's own monster hits | `applyDamage` with no item, so the card names nobody; the module knows the attacker itself (the step log) | sure |

So the rule the code follows: **a card is the event; an HP drop with no card for that creature within a short wait is "something hurt it, unsure what"**. Sizes seen: a small monster's packet is about 3,900 characters (its own sheet 1,800, the ground map 950, what it perceives 850); the notebook's part is capped well under a quarter of that (below).

## How it works
- **A creature with no Discipline (0.26.0):** `forPacket` takes `shares: false` and leaves `alliesDid` and `side` out (Rhea's R3-1: it shares nothing); what happened to itself stays. `notebookOf` gives the fold-out the same, so it shows what is sent.
- **Where it lives:** the Combat's flag `notebook` (`memory` already means last-seen squares): `notebook.<token id>.<key>` is one line. One key per line, written once: the runner adds lines in one `combat.update` per event (through a queue, so a burst of cards is written in order); a GM's page crosses a line out (the field `x` on it) or adds her own (its own key), so two pages never write the same key (review 7). It goes with the fight when the fight is deleted.
- **A line** is `{ k, r, at, ... }`: its kind, the round, when; then labels and fixed words only: `a` who did it (a label, or "someone it couldn't see", "something unseen"), `t` whom it was done to, `s` how bad (1 to 3, against its own hit points), `d` a damage type, `p` a kind of persistent damage (its own), `w` words built by the module, `i` the monster's own spell's name, `sure: 0` when the module couldn't pin it down. Never a name, a number off someone else's sheet, or text from a card. A GM's line is `{ k: "gm", by: "gm", text }`, its text already in labels.
- **Kinds and the rung that uses them** (`memory.mjs`, `canUse`): Animal `hurt`, `gap`; Simple `miss`, `saved`, `nothing` (its attack came to nothing because an immunity or resistance took all of it; the line doesn't say which kind of damage: that is the tactician's); Cunning `heal`, `cast`; Trained tactician `reactive`, `block`, `weak`, `resist`, `immune`. A GM's line and allies' deeds go to every Mind but Mindless. The rung comes from the Mind's pick in its profile; a creature with no Mind (a party member Angel plays that the GM wrote nothing for) is Cunning (review 6).
- **Who keeps one** (`keepsBook`): every creature in the fight that isn't a player's, ticked or not, and a party member the GM plays. Unconscious, dying, dead and escaped creatures write nothing. Last-seen memory (`noteWhatTheySense`) is kept for the same creatures now, not only ticked ones.
- **Perceived at that moment** (`witnessList`, `senser`): when an event arrives, each keeper's view of the one who did it and the one it was done to is worked out there and then with the same senses, light, walls and Visioner record as its packet (`senser` is `sense` for any one creature, enemy or ally). A line needs the target or effect perceived (itself, or seen); the actor is named by label only if seen, "someone it couldn't see" if only heard, "something unseen" otherwise. An ally's deed needs the ally seen, like an enemy would be (review 2). A label is given out only for a creature the witness sees.
- **The events** (`onMemoryCard`, `onMemoryUpdate`, `onMemoryHp`, `noteReactionAnswers`, `noteDeed`; the source table above says why each is sure): a damage-taken card (who from its origin, or, for a card this page posted itself with none, the monster whose step it is carrying out; how much from what the card says it took off; weakness, resistance and immunity from its HTML; healing; persistent damage by its kind; a shield taking part of it); a spell's cast card (its traits and area as words, its slug so a monster with the same spell knows it by name); an attack card with a target and an outcome (the attacker's own miss; a deed for its allies); a save flag from Toolbelt's row on a monster's own spell (the creature shrugged it off); an HP drop with no card within 1.5 s ("Something hurt it.", unsure; how much from PF2e's figure on the update or, when it has none, from the HP this page last saw); the answers to a monster's action (who struck as it moved, who blocked); the module's own steps other than Strikes, spells and plain moves (an ability, fleeing, Take Cover, standing, dropping prone, burrowing) as deeds.
- **Tallies, not a log** (`render`): several lines of one kind about one creature are one sentence ("E1 hurt it twice, badly once."), misses and saves never with a count ("It keeps missing E2.": review 3). Order: her lines, who hurt it, what failed, healers and casters, the finer things, gaps. At most 10 lines are sent; what its Mind can't use, and anything past the 10, is `unused` (the fold-out greys it). Allies' deeds: the last two of each ally since this monster's last turn began (`nbTurn` on the turns flag, written at each turn change), at most 6 allies.
- **The side's tally** (`sideOf`, `sideWords`): itself and its allies in this fight as standing, down and fled (an escaped monster), in one sentence; nothing for a creature alone and unhurt.
- **The packet** (schema 3): `notebook: { remembers, alliesDid, side }` right after `mind`, blank parts left out, absent when empty and for a Mindless creature. An unsure line ends "(unsure)". It is stored once, in the request's own packet, which is where the plan card's history and the History window read it (review 10 asked for keys only; lines over the cap are dropped from the flag, so keys alone could not show an old turn's notebook again).
- **The cap** (`evict`): 40 stored lines a notebook. Over it, allies' deeds from before its last turn go first, then unsure lines, then scratches and gaps, then this round's deeds, then the oldest of the rest; a GM's line never. The whole `notebook` part of a packet stays under 1,000 characters (unit-checked with a full notebook), against about 3,900 for a small monster's packet.
- **Labels:** a notebook write can give out a label, and so can a packet being built; `buildPacket` waits for writes on their way and holds new ones back until its labels are saved, so two creatures never get the same one (0.25.0 review 2).
- **Thinking again mid-turn** (`rethinkFor`, in `lookAgain`): only a heal or a casting by someone else that it perceived, written after this plan was asked for, of a kind its Mind uses. Its own miss, a block against it and a strike on its move follow from its own step and never count (review 4).
- **Her edits** (`rememberAdd`, `rememberCross`, `previewRemember`, `bookPeople`): a line of hers is gated once, at save: a party member's name becomes its label if this monster has perceived that creature at some point in the fight (its last-seen memory) or knows it as an ally, and is refused in words otherwise; what is stored holds labels only (review 5). Crossing out is reversible. If that monster has a plan in hand, the request gets `nbEdit`: with a plan waiting the runner plans the rest again at once (`runRemember`); while a step runs or saves are awaited it does so before the next step (`lookAgain`). Either way it isn't one of the turn's re-plans and the new plan waits for Do it, Auto or not. A step already begun is finished by hand, as for a Change; and with no actions left nothing is asked again (a re-plan there would end the turn), so the plan in hand goes on.
- **When the runner was away** (`noteGap`): a reload of its page, or another scene on its canvas when an event arrives, gives every notebook "It missed part of round N" once a round, instead of silence.
- **Windows:** Monster Turns has a Remembers fold-out on the plan card and one for every other keeper below it (`ui/turns.mjs` `_nbHtml`): what is sent, its allies' deeds, the tally, what it makes nothing of (greyed), crossed-out lines with a put-back button, and the add box. Open state and a half-typed line survive redraws, and a redraw waits while she types. The GM reads names beside labels there. The History window shows "Remembered" under each plan (from that request's packet) and "What they remember now" at the end; the saved page has both. A fight with no monster turn can be saved for its notebooks; at End Encounter such a fight asks Save or Discard only if she added or crossed out a line herself.
- **API** (under the fight API as `memory`): `of(combat, tokenId)`, `preview`, `add`, `cross`: GM only.
- **What a player's browser holds:** a Combat's flags reach every browser, so every notebook does, where before only ticked monsters' packets did (review 10). It holds labels and fixed words, a monster's own spell names, and a GM's line as she wrote it after the name gate.

- **0.28.0:** a creature healing itself reads "E5 healed itself." (it read "E5 healed E5."). The fallen leader's own notebook is where a band that fights harder finds who struck it down (orders.mjs `lastHurtBy`: the last sure "hurt" line).

## Traps
- **The HP update arrives before the damage-taken card,** and a card can come with no HP update at all (damage resisted to nothing, a shield taking all of it). Read the card; use the HP update only as the fallback after a wait.
- **`damageTaken` in an HP update's options is there for a hand edit too:** it doesn't tell a card's damage from a typed number.
- **A hand edit of an unlinked token's HP carries no `damageTaken` at all** (seen in 0.25.0's first test build: nothing was written): the runner keeps the HP it last saw for each creature (`hpSeen`, filled at every turn change and when its page loads) and uses the difference.
- **The card's `appliedDamage.updates` holds what was taken off, not the HP before** (the first draft read it as the old HP; the review caught it against the probe's own numbers).
- **A weakness or resistance applied is only in the card's HTML** (`data-applications` on the `.iwr` span), not in its flags.
- **A pre-update hook fires only on the page that made the change:** a watcher on another page needs `updateActor`.
- **Toolbelt rolls a save with no chat card:** the result is a flag update on the damage card.
- **An NPC's HP value can't be raised past its max in the same update that raises the max** on an unlinked token (seen: max went to 80, the value stayed): set the max, then the value.

## Open items
- A monster waiting for its first plan of a turn (`requested`) isn't asked again when she edits its notebook: the packet has gone out. Her edit is in its next packet.
- Damage with no origin on its card and no step running (a fall the module applies, a GM's own damage roll) is "something hurt it, unsure what".
- A shield that takes all of a hit leaves nothing but the card's words; the block is known only through the module's own reaction question, so a blocked hit by a monster the GM plays writes no "blocks with a shield".
- Reactive Shield and Nimble Dodge turning a hit into a miss after the attack card: the attacker's notebook keeps no miss for it.
- Allies' deeds don't include plain moves ("moved beside the one in robes" was in the design's examples): the ally's square is already in the packet.
- Private castings (Workbench) are not treated differently.
- "What frightened it" has no source: a condition records no origin. Until players' buttons or a known card give one, it is Rhea's line to add.
