Area spells: aiming, who is caught, line of effect through solid walls only, PF2e's Region look, damaging basic-save areas run for real.

## Decisions
- **Height (0.13.0):** a creature is caught only if its vertical gap from where the area starts is within the area's size (`verticalGap` in `caughtBy`): a burst starts on the ground at its corner, a cone or line at the caster's height, an emanation from the caster's whole height. So a 20-ft burst on the ground misses a flier 40 ft up, and a flier's 15-ft cone misses creatures on the ground 30 ft below. The horizontal part stays the map's own square test, so the two are combined loosely (a creature near the edge and well up can be caught though its true distance is a little more). Found by the 0.13.0 review; flying made it matter.
- **Areas (2026-11-07, built 0.7.0, 0.7.1):** a **damaging basic-save area runs for real** for everyone caught (players their own row and the turn waits for all; no player owner or offline rolled at once; damage by each one's degree); anything else is placed and goes to the GM's card. **Only solid walls (blocking movement and sight) stop line of effect; trunks never do** and give their cover to the save (thick standard +2; thin is lesser cover, which by the rules gives nothing to a save). **Instant areas are removed once resolved, lasting ones stay.** **The marker is PF2e's own Region look** (filled squares, caster's colour, measurements off), chosen "for now" from samples on a real encounter map. Aiming: a burst at a grid corner (`at`), a cone or line toward a square (`toward`), an emanation needs none and doesn't catch its caster.
**Design-era decisions (from the 2026-11-05/06 plan; the lines above are the skill's summary of the same choices):**
- **Areas:** the plan gives shape, origin and direction. The check works out which squares it covers. **Decided (Rhea, 2026-11-07): an area that would catch an ally is refused, naming the allies, unless the plan marks it as deliberate; the GM's card shows who it also hits.** **Also decided (Rhea, 2026-11-07, for the areas release 0.7.0):** (1) **A basic-save area runs for real**, like a single-target save spell: every creature in it rolls (players their own row, the turn waits for all of them; no player owner or offline rolled at once) and damage is applied to each by its own degree; anything beyond basic-save damage stays on the GM's card. (2) **Only solid walls block line of effect** (walls that block movement and sight: buildings, cliffs). **Trunks never block it; they give their cover to the Reflex save** (Survey Sheet's rule: 3 ft or wider standard, +2; thinner is lesser, which by the rules gives +1 to AC only and nothing to a save: [Cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2372)), because one trunk shouldn't make anyone immune to a burst. **Visioner does this itself [CODE, 8.14.17]:** its `createRegion` hook (`TemplateManager`) works out each creature's cover from the area's centre for a Region whose `flags.pf2e.origin.type` is "spell", and its `SavingThrowUseCase` adds it to Reflex saves (dropping lesser). Whether it reaches a save rolled with no dialog (Toolbelt's row by the Shift rule) is [UNVERIFIED]: first live test of the build. Rules for areas ([Area](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2384)): a burst starts at a corner of a square within range and catches a creature if even one square of its space is in it; a cone's first square shares an edge (orthogonal) or touches a corner (diagonal) of the caster's space, any edge for Large and up, never its own space; a line catches every creature whose space it overlaps; **an emanation's centre creature chooses whether it is affected** (default: not), so the monster in its own emanation isn't an ally refusal. (3) **Instant areas are removed once every save and damage is done; lasting ones stay on the map** for the GM. (4) **The marker is PF2e's own Region look** (filled squares in the caster's colour, "A"), "for now": Rhea compared four samples on a real encounter map from Test Player's view (filled squares, a hatched shape, a thin outline with the spell's name, an outline alone). Aiming as proposed: a burst at the corner of a named square, a cone or line toward a named square (cones in 45° steps, as PF2e's own placement), an emanation centred on the monster; a creature is in the area if any square it occupies is highlighted (the rules' test). PF2e 8.7.0 [CODE]: `spell.placeTemplate` → `placeRegionFromItem` builds the Region from `shapeDataFromEffectArea` (burst a circle, cone 90°, line one square wide, emanation from the token) with `highlightMode: "coverage"` and flags `pf2e.messageId/origin/areaShape`, then hands it to the mouse placer; Toolbelt's template targeting fires only from the card's Place button, so the module sets the card's targets itself. **Built in 0.7.0 and 0.7.1 and tested live (2026-11-07):** the module makes PF2e's own Region in code (the Place crosshair needs a real mouse) and targets the caught creatures on the runner's account before casting, which gives each a working Toolbelt row; details in the repo's DEVELOPING.md and `claude/lythara-encounter-builder.md`.

## How it works
- **Burrowed creatures (0.16.0):** `coverage` leaves out any creature burrowed under the ground (`isBelow`): earth between it and the area's origin blocks line of effect (Rules ID 2382), whatever the heights say. `docs/features/moves.md` has burrowing.
- **Area spells (0.7.0).** `{ do: "spell", item, at: [x, y] }` for a burst (a grid corner: the top-left corner of square [x, y]), `toward: [x, y]` for a cone or line (a square it points at), nothing for an emanation; naming a `target` instead aims it at that creature (a burst at the corner of its space nearest the caster). `deliberate: true` when it means to catch its allies. Rhea, 2026-11-07, and the rules ([Area](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2384)):
  - **Aiming (`aimArea`, pure):** a burst bursts from a grid corner, checked for range as the nearest of the four squares round it (a touch burst by the creature's reach; a burst with no range from a corner of its own space), and refused when a solid wall stands between the caster's centre and the corner (the rules want line of effect to the origin); a cone starts at the caster's space, in the middle of the edge it faces when orthogonal and at the corner when diagonal, turned in 45° steps as PF2e's own placement does (`directionSnap`); a line leaves the caster's space where a ray from its centre does, in 5° steps; an emanation spreads round the caster's token. A Large creature's cone starts at its own corner or edge middle. "toward" inside its own space is refused. Cubes, squares, rings and walls aren't placed (`placeable`): those spells stay cards as before.
  - **The shape is PF2e's own** (`shapeOf` in squares, `shapePx` in pixels), as `placeRegionFromItem` in PF2e 8.7.0 builds it from `shapeDataFromEffectArea`: a circle for a burst or cylinder, a 90° cone, a line one square wide, an emanation with the caster's token as its base (`base.width` and `height` stay in squares, `base.shape` is the token's).
  - **Who's caught is Foundry's own test** (`coverage`): a Region made in memory only (`new RegionDocument(data, { parent: scene })`, never saved) and its `polygonTree.testPoint(centre, 0.75)` on each square's centre near the shape, the very test core's `Region#_getCoveredGridSpaceOffsets` uses for the highlighted squares ("coverage" mode: a centre inside, with a 0.75 px tolerance; `RegionDocument#testPoint` has none, which mattered for a Large creature's orthogonal cone, whose edge squares' centres lie exactly on its sides: found by the review), so what everyone sees is what counts. Directions checked live: rotation 0 points east and −90 north (y grows downwards), and a line runs forward from its start point. Tested 2026-11-07: a 10-foot burst covers 12 squares, a 15-foot cone 1, 3 and 3, a 10-foot emanation from a Medium creature the rules' 21. A creature is caught if any square of its space is covered (`caughtBy`) and has line of effect to that square's centre from `aim.from`: for a burst its corner nudged a hair toward the caster (a corner on a wall belongs to the caster's side of it), for a cone, line or emanation the caster's centre, because a cone or line starts on the caster's edge, often on a wall along a grid line, and tracing from there let one go straight through a wall the monster stood against (found by the review).
  - **Line of effect (Rhea, 2026-11-07):** only solid walls stop it (`solidWalls`: blocking both movement and sight, `NORMAL`; not an open door; a one-way wall counts both ways), **never Survey Sheet's trunks** (`flags.lythara-survey-sheet.enc === "trunk"`), which give cover to the save instead, because one trunk shouldn't make anyone immune to a burst. That cover comes from Visioner by itself: its `createRegion` hook (`TemplateManager`, 8.14.17) works out each creature's cover from the area's origin for a Region whose `flags.pf2e.origin.type` is "spell", and its wrapper on `game.pf2e.Check.roll` (`SavingThrowUseCase.handleCheckRoll`) adds it to a Reflex save with or without a dialog. Lesser cover gives nothing to saves (rules: AC only), and Visioner drops it too.
  - **Allies (Rhea, 2026-11-07):** `checkSpell` refuses an area that catches an ally or the monster itself (a burst it stands in), naming them by label (`alliesCaught`: only allies the GM hasn't hidden and itself have labels, so a hidden one never shows in the words), unless the step says `deliberate`. An emanation never catches its caster (the centre creature chooses; here not). Creatures it doesn't perceive are caught as the rules say, but only their token ids go on the step (`area.hits`, for the GM's card); the thinker reads `area.catches`, the labels of caught creatures it perceives. **`forThinker` strips `hits` from the steps `submitPlan` and `checkPlan` hand back,** because even their count would show creatures it doesn't perceive (found by the review).
  - **Mode:** a damaging basic save area (no variants, no attack, Toolbelt's rows on) is `area` and runs for real; any other placeable area is `card` and is still placed, with its targets on the card.
  - **Carrying it out (`runArea`, stages from `nextArea`):** `cast` works out who it catches now from where the monster stands (`coverage`), targets them on the runner's account (`aimAll`) so Toolbelt gives each a row, records `caught` and `rows`, and casts; **GM-hidden creatures get no row** on a public card (their names would show), and the note tells the GM to roll and apply for them. `place` creates PF2e's Region with PF2e's flags (`messageId`, `origin` with `getOriginData()`, `areaShape`) and the module's own (`area: { combat, request, step, mode, lasting }`; no `ownership`, which isn't a Region field), and Toolbelt's `targetHelper.skip`: Toolbelt's Template Helper opens a "target the tokens in this area?" dialog from its `createRegion` hook for any spell area its own user makes (found live: it sat on the Bridge's page, where nobody can click it; the module targets the creatures itself), found again by those if it already exists, so a reload can't make two; colour the runner's, "coverage" highlighting, measurements off (the look Rhea chose from samples on a real map), visible to all. `save` (`awaitAreaSaves`): the module rolls through Toolbelt's own buttons (`rollSavesOnCard`: the d20 once for every creature no player owns that hasn't saved, or each one's row when there's no d20, then each offline player's row, and any the GM pressed Roll it for them on); players online roll their own and the turn waits in `saves` with no timer, each named on the card with its own Roll it for them. The degrees go on the record in one save with `results` (the labels the monster knows and their degree, for `doneStep`). `damage` is rolled once at the cast rank with the hurt creatures targeted; with automatic damage off the note gives each creature's share (half, full, double), because the damage card isn't tied to the saves; `apply` goes creature by creature (`applying`, `applied` per token id, never twice) by its own degree (`areaHurt`, `SAVE_MULT`); its own damage to itself goes into the baseline (`seen.me.hp`) in the same save, so it isn't news. A creature removed from the map while waiting has nothing left to save or take. Nobody caught records `results: ""` at the cast, so the step reads as finished.
  - **Instant areas go, lasting ones stay (Rhea, 2026-11-07):** `lasting` is a spell with a duration or sustained. An `area`-mode instant area is deleted once its damage is applied (`remove`), or, with automatic damage off, once its damage is rolled: she applies it from the damage card, which names everyone hurt (the doc said otherwise until 0.33.1; the review found it); a `card`-mode instant one when the turn goes on (`dropInstantAreas` at the start of each run for earlier steps, and on every turn change). An `area`-mode one left unresolved because the turn moved on during its saves stays for the GM. Lasting ones are the GM's to delete. A run already rolling or applying when the turn moves on finishes, as a single-target spell does: the damage is the spell's.
- **Off the grid, in areas (0.9.0):** `coverage` tests a creature off the grid by Foundry's own containment test points (`TokenDocument#getContainmentTestPoints`, what `testInsideRegion` uses) against the in-memory Region with the highlight tolerance, with line of effect to that point, so the check agrees with the area placed on the map; one on the grid by its squares as before. The packet marks an observed or concealed creature off the grid, and the monster itself, `offGrid`, because its square is rounded while its distance is PF2e's from where it is.
- **0.27.0 (`reactions.md`):** before a damaging area with a Reflex save is cast, each creature it will catch is asked its reaction to it (Interposing Earth: PF2e's Effect: Cover for the save, taken off when the step ends); the first application of damage asks the damage gate once for everyone hurt (a champion picks which ally), each share is then applied with its own `{ resist, less }` or not at all, and `awaitAreaSaves` notes a barrier that still stands after a save that takes no damage.

## Traps
- **Toolbelt's Template Helper opens its "target the tokens in this area?" dialog for any spell area its own user creates,** by code too (its `createRegion` hook checks PF2e's `isEffectArea`), so a Region the runner makes left that dialog on the Bridge's page (0.7.0). It skips a Region with its own `flags.pf2e-toolbelt.targetHelper.skip` (0.7.1).
- **What an area covers is Foundry's own test, done in memory:** `new RegionDocument({ shapes, levels: [] }, { parent: scene })` answers without creating anything; use `polygonTree.testPoint(centre, 0.75)`, the tolerance core's highlighted squares use (`RegionDocument#testPoint` has none; on a Large creature's orthogonal cone the edge squares' centres lie exactly on the sides). `ownership` isn't a Region field. Recipe in `claude/foundry-op-recipes.md`.
- **Line of effect for a cone or line is traced from the caster's centre,** not the shape's origin on its edge: from the edge it went through a wall the monster stood against (found by the review). A burst's corner on a wall is nudged a hair toward the caster.
- **Checked steps must not go back to the thinker with `area.hits`** (every caught creature's token id): `forThinker` strips it in `submitPlan`'s answer and `checkPlan` (found by the review).
- **Visioner gives an area's cover on the save by itself:** its `createRegion` hook works out cover from the area's origin for a Region whose `flags.pf2e.origin.type` is "spell", and its wrapper on `game.pf2e.Check.roll` adds it with or without a dialog (seen: +2 from a trunk on a module-rolled save). Don't add cover yourself.
- **Test trap: Claude Test Scene has an older leftover cone Region named "Region"** that isn't the module's; leave it.
