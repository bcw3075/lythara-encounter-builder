Spells: a spell attack or a save spell at one perceived creature runs for real, players roll their own saves and the turn waits; anything else goes to the GM's card.

## Decisions
- **Spells (2026-11-07):** a spell attack and a save spell **at one creature it perceives** run for real; **anything else is cast and goes to the GM's card** (areas, several targets, a spell at an ally, Heal and Harm, conditions by degree, buffs): "start with A and if it gets annoying I'll ask you to take it over". **A player rolls their own save from their row on the spell card and the turn waits, with no timer;** the card shows **Roll it for them**; a creature no player owns or whose player is offline is rolled at once. **An area that would catch an ally (or the monster itself, in a burst it stands in) is refused, naming them, unless the plan marks it `deliberate`;** the GM's card shows everyone it catches.
**Design-era decisions (from the 2026-11-05/06 plan; the lines above are the skill's summary of the same choices):**
- **Saves:** NPC saves through Toolbelt's button or automatically; player saves from their own row [DOC]. **Decided (Rhea, 2026-11-07):** a player rolls their own save from their own row on the spell card, and the turn waits; the module then applies the damage by degree. **No timer:** the GM's card says who it's waiting for, with a **Roll it for them** button. An offline player, or a character no player owns (the GM-run party member), is rolled by the module straight away, as Toolbelt's NPC button treats them.
- **Spells (built 0.6.0 and 0.6.1, slices 1 to 3; Rhea also chose: anything beyond basic-save damage is the GM's from the card for now, "if it gets annoying I'll ask you to take it over"; details in the repo's DEVELOPING.md and `claude/lythara-encounter-builder.md`). As proposed:** four slices. (1) Casting any spell: the packet lists each spell (rank, actions, range, area, defense, damage, uses or slots left, text); a step `{ do: "spell", item, rank, target }`; the check refuses no slot or use, out of range, not perceived, wrong actions, Restrained with manipulate; an attack spell counts toward the attack penalty and ends Take Cover. (2) Spell attacks for real like Strikes, damage applied automatically, D1 re-run. (3) Save spells against one target, damage by degree for basic saves. (4) Areas, its own release: a square for a burst, a direction for a cone or line; the module makes PF2e's own area and works out who's inside. Anything else (buffs, healing, summons, conditions by degree) goes to the GM's card at Next step. Not in step 4: Sustain, counteract, reaction spells. **Code facts [CODE, PF2e 8.7.0]:** `entry.cast(spell, {rank, slotId, consume})` spends the slot, use or focus point and posts the card only (`toMessage` with `actualCast`); `spell.rollAttack(event, attackNumber)` rolls against the account's target; `spell.rollDamage(event)` always rolls a spell attack's damage as a hit (`outcome: "success"`), so a critical's doubling is the card's DOUBLE button and the module must double it itself; an area is placed only through `canvas.regions.placeRegion` (mouse), so the module creates the Region itself, with PF2e's own flags (`messageId`, `origin`, `areaShape`). Test monster: the Claude Test Barrow Wight has only Breathe Fire (a 15 ft cone, basic Reflex, once a day, innate entry "TB Innate"); give it an attack cantrip and a single-target save spell in PF2 Test for slices 2 and 3.

## How it works
- **Spells (0.6.0).** `{ do: "spell", item: "<id>", target: "E1", rank: 2, actions: 2 }` (`rank` and `actions` optional). Rhea, 2026-11-07: a spell attack, and a save spell, at one creature it perceives run for real; anything else is cast and the GM applies it from its card (her choice to start with; she may ask the module to take more over).
  - **The spellbook (`spellBook`)** comes from PF2e's own spellcasting sheet data (`entry.getSheetData()`, what a sheet shows): prepared slots not yet expended, a spontaneous caster's spells by rank with that rank's slots, innate spells with their uses, focus spells and the focus pool, cantrips. One entry per spell per spellcasting entry, with the ranks it can be cast at, each with its **pool**: a spontaneous caster's slots of one rank (shared by every spell of that rank), a prepared caster's copies of one spell at one rank, an innate spell's uses, the focus pool (with the spell's cost in points); cantrips and at-will spells have none. `checkPlan` spends pools through the plan, so two casts can't share one slot. Rituals and staffs, wands and scrolls aren't in it. **Ids stay put through a turn:** a spell known in two entries is `slug` in the first and `slug-2` in the second by entry order, whatever is left to cast (found by the review: ids re-numbered as entries ran out). The packet's `you.spells` gives each spell's ranks and what's left, actions, range, targets, area, defense with its DC or attack bonus, its base damage (`baseDamage`: heightening is in its text), traits and text (900 characters at most).
  - **The check (`checkSpell`):** actions from PF2e's `time` (`spellActions`: "2", "1 to 3", "free"; a reaction or anything longer is refused), a choice of actions needs `actions`; the rank asked for or the lowest with enough left; a target for a spell that targets creatures (`spellTargets` reads PF2e's target text) and none for one that doesn't, except that an area spell may name the creature to aim at; the target is a label it perceives, an ally's label or its own (`alliesByLabel`); range from `spellRange` ("30 feet"), touch by the creature's own reach (`getReach({ action: "interact" })`, not a weapon's); Restrained refuses attack and manipulate spells; a spell with the attack trait counts toward the multiple attack penalty and ends Take Cover; the flat check for a concealed or hidden target (not for an area). **Mode:** `attack` (a spell attack at one creature), `save` (a save at one creature, with Toolbelt's Target Helper on), otherwise `card`. **Never run for real:** a spell whose effect changes with the actions spent (PF2e `hasVariants` and a choice of actions: Heal and Harm, whose base item reads as a basic Fortitude save at "varies" range, so 1-action Heal on an ally would have rolled the ally's save; found by the review). Variants with fixed actions don't count: Ignition's are melee or ranged, and its base item is the ranged spell (30 ft, 2d4), which runs as it is (found live: the first rule sent Ignition to the GM), a spell at an ally, an area, more than one target.
  - **Carrying it out (`runSpell`, stages from `nextSpell`):** `cast` through the entry's own `cast(spell, { rank, consume: true })`, which spends the slot or use and posts the card (type `spell-cast`; a spell with no check, damage or save, such as Mist, gets no `context` at all, only PF2e's `casting` flag, so `onCreateMessage` takes that as the cast card too: found live 2026-11-07, before which such a cast timed out after 20 s), with the runner aiming at the target so Toolbelt puts its row on the card; then the flat check (card mode too: a failure means the spell doesn't affect them); then by mode. **Attack:** `spell.rollAttack(event, attackNumber)` by the Shift rule, its card waited for as `spell-attack-roll` or `attack-roll`; a missing degree goes to the GM, never a silent critical miss; on a hit, `rollDamage(event, mapIncreases)` on the variant at the cast rank (`loadVariant({ castRank })`, because PF2e reads the rank from the card's button, which a script has none of). **PF2e rolls a spell attack's damage as a hit** (`outcome: "success"`; its DOUBLE button is the critical), so the module applies it doubled on a critical hit (`spellMultiplier`), or with automatic damage off tells the GM to use Double. A non-damaging attack that hits, and anything a spell's text gives a degree beyond damage (`critText`: its text lists a critical success), goes to the GM in a note. **Every card a spell step makes carries Workbench's `noAutoDamageRoll`** (`pending.all`): Workbench reads it on cast cards too (its damage handler skips any card with it).
  - **Saves (`awaitSave`), Rhea's decisions:** a player rolls their own save from their row on the spell's card and **the turn waits with no timer**: the step returns `wait`, the request goes to state `saves` (in `RESUMABLE`), and the GM's card shows "Waiting for … (their row on the spell's card)" with **Roll it for them**. A creature no player owns, or whose player is offline, is rolled at once. The result is read from Toolbelt's flag on the card, `targetHelper.saveVariants.<variant>.saves[tokenId].success` (`savedDegree`; Workbench reads the same). **The module rolls through Toolbelt's own buttons** in the runner's chat log (`saveButton`, `rollSaveOnCard`): the d20 `.pf2e-toolbelt-target-rollSaves` for creatures no player owns, a row's `[data-action=roll-save]` otherwise, clicked with `shiftKey` equal to the user's `showCheckDialogs` (Toolbelt flips the dialog on Shift like PF2e: a plain click opened PF2e's Modifier dialog on the Bridge's page, tested). That keeps one result per target on the card, where players and Workbench see it. **Rows carry no token id:** Toolbelt orders them for a GM by whether a player owns the token (no owner first) then by name (`createTargetsRows` in its 3.60.0 `main.js`), and the row's name is checked too. Basic save damage is rolled once (`rollDamage`) and applied by degree with the roll's own `alter` (none, half, full, double: `SAVE_MULT`, what PF2e's Half and Double buttons do); a non-basic or non-damaging save spell goes to the GM with the degree. A card with no rows (Target Helper off on that page) is the GM's (`noRows`).
  - **Resuming a waiting turn:** the runner's `updateChatMessage` hook (`onMessageUpdate`) resumes it when Toolbelt writes to the cast card; Roll it for them writes only `rollFor` (no `go`, so it can't run the next step if the save landed first: found by the review) and `act` resumes on it; `doIt` looks once more after a run ends in `saves`, for a save that landed between reading the card and writing the state (found by the review); the runner's `ready` resumes one too. The result, `waiting` cleared and the `rollFor` it made unnecessary go in one save. **The turn moving on while a save waits** ends that request with a note (`leaveWaiting`), so the GM knows what's left on the card.
  - **Checked live before the build (2026-11-07):** `entry.cast` posts only the card; the Wight's cast card stored Toolbelt's targets and `saveVariants.null { dc, basic, statistic }`; the d20 and a PC's row both rolled and wrote the flag from a scripted click with Shift; the Bridge's message mode is public, so the card and its rows reach players.
  - **Not done:** rerolls on a row (a Hero Point) after damage is applied don't correct it; the chat log must have the card drawn on the runner's page for its buttons (after a reload with many newer messages it may not: the card says it couldn't find the button and the GM clicks the row on their own card); Sustain, counteracting, reaction spells; heightened damage in the packet; staffs, wands and scrolls.
**Design-era text (from the plan; where it and the lines above disagree, the lines above, written from the code, win):**
- **Spells:** read from `actor.spellcasting` with slots and DCs. The call is `entry.cast(spell, {rank, slotId, consume, message, messageMode})` [CODE, not run]. First test of this step.

## Traps
- **Spells: PF2e's own calls do one thing each.** `entry.cast(spell, { rank, consume: true })` spends the slot or use and posts the card only (`spell-cast`); attack, damage and saves are separate. A spell attack's card came as `attack-roll`, not `spell-attack-roll` (both are waited for). **PF2e rolls a spell attack's damage as a hit** (`outcome: "success"`): the module doubles it on a critical. `rollDamage` reads the cast rank from the card's button, so heightened damage goes through `loadVariant({ castRank })`. An area is placed only by mouse in PF2e (`placeRegion`), so the module makes the Region itself.
- **PF2e "variants" aren't all by actions:** Heal and Harm change with the actions spent (base item: a basic Fortitude save at "varies" range, which would have rolled an ally's save), but Ignition's are melee or ranged with fixed actions, and its base item is the ranged spell. Only spells whose actions vary go to the GM for this (0.6.1; 0.6.0 sent Ignition to the card).
- **Toolbelt's save buttons from code need the Shift rule** (as PF2e's rolls do): a plain click opened PF2e's Modifier dialog on the Bridge's page. Rows carry no token id; for a GM they're ordered no player owner first, then by name, and the name is checked. Clicking in a detached `renderHTML()` does nothing; the chat log's copy has the listeners. Toolbelt's d20 rolls only creatures that haven't saved yet. Details in `claude/foundry-s8-toolbelt.md`. A save on the card's flag carries its total as `value` (zod schema in Toolbelt 3.60.0's `scripts/main.js`).
- **A saved request's `rollFor`, `go` and the waiting state:** Roll it for them writes only `rollFor` (with `go` it could run the next step if the save landed first), and the result, `waiting` cleared and `rollFor` cleared go in one save (found by the review). A save landing between reading the card and writing `saves` fires nothing the runner acts on, so `doIt` looks once more after a run ends in `saves`.
- **A spell with no check, damage or save posts a cast card with no `context` at all,** only PF2e's `casting` flag (Mist), so waiting for type `spell-cast` timed out after 20 s (0.6.0 to 0.7.0, single-target card spells too). `onCreateMessage` takes the `casting` flag as the cast card since 0.7.1.
