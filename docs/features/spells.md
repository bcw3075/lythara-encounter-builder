Spells: a spell attack or a save spell at one perceived creature runs for real, players roll their own saves and the turn waits; anything else goes to the GM's card.

## Decisions
- **Spells (2026-11-07):** a spell attack and a save spell **at one creature it perceives** run for real; **anything else is cast and goes to the GM's card** (areas, several targets, a spell at an ally, Heal and Harm, conditions by degree, buffs): "start with A and if it gets annoying I'll ask you to take it over". **A player rolls their own save from their row on the spell card and the turn waits, with no timer;** the card shows **Roll it for them**; a creature no player owns or whose player is offline is rolled at once. **An area that would catch an ally (or the monster itself, in a burst it stands in) is refused, naming them, unless the plan marks it `deliberate`;** the GM's card shows everyone it catches.
**Design-era decisions (from the 2026-11-05/06 plan; the lines above are the skill's summary of the same choices):**
- **Saves:** NPC saves through Toolbelt's button or automatically; player saves from their own row [DOC]. **Decided (Rhea, 2026-11-07):** a player rolls their own save from their own row on the spell card, and the turn waits; the module then applies the damage by degree. **No timer:** the GM's card says who it's waiting for, with a **Roll it for them** button. An offline player, or a character no player owns (the GM-run party member), is rolled by the module straight away, as Toolbelt's NPC button treats them.
- **Spells (built 0.6.0 and 0.6.1, slices 1 to 3; Rhea also chose: anything beyond basic-save damage is the GM's from the card for now, "if it gets annoying I'll ask you to take it over"; details in the repo's DEVELOPING.md and `angel-mod-private/logs/lythara-encounter-builder.md`). As proposed:** four slices. (1) Casting any spell: the packet lists each spell (rank, actions, range, area, defense, damage, uses or slots left, text); a step `{ do: "spell", item, rank, target }`; the check refuses no slot or use, out of range, not perceived, wrong actions, Restrained with manipulate; an attack spell counts toward the attack penalty and ends Take Cover. (2) Spell attacks for real like Strikes, damage applied automatically, D1 re-run. (3) Save spells against one target, damage by degree for basic saves. (4) Areas, its own release: a square for a burst, a direction for a cone or line; the module makes PF2e's own area and works out who's inside. Anything else (buffs, healing, summons, conditions by degree) goes to the GM's card at Next step. Not in step 4: Sustain, counteract, reaction spells. **Code facts [CODE, PF2e 8.7.0]:** `entry.cast(spell, {rank, slotId, consume})` spends the slot, use or focus point and posts the card only (`toMessage` with `actualCast`); `spell.rollAttack(event, attackNumber)` rolls against the account's target; `spell.rollDamage(event)` always rolls a spell attack's damage as a hit (`outcome: "success"`), so a critical's doubling is the card's DOUBLE button and the module must double it itself; an area is placed only through `canvas.regions.placeRegion` (mouse), so the module creates the Region itself, with PF2e's own flags (`messageId`, `origin`, `areaShape`). Test monster: the Claude Test Barrow Wight has only Breathe Fire (a 15 ft cone, basic Reflex, once a day, innate entry "TB Innate"); give it an attack cantrip and a single-target save spell in PF2 Test for slices 2 and 3.
- **Missiles and healing run for real (0.46.0; Rhea at the 2026-11-24 live test: card-only actions should "roll and apply" like Strikes do).** Force Barrage and Magic Missile at one creature always hit: actions × (1 + one more missile per two ranks above 1) missiles, rolled together as one damage roll (`missileFormula`, "1d4+1" × 3 = "3d4+3") and applied. Heal (and any healing spell with per-action variants) at one ally that isn't undead is cast with the variant for its actions (PF2e's overlays: touch for 1 action, 30 feet for 2), rolled and applied as healing received, with a line when the target was dying. Several targets, a 3-action Heal's emanation, or Heal at an undead still go to the card.
- **Sustained spells are weighed (0.46.0; her rule at the live test: every Sustain spell or ability is weighed in the minds and the turns).** `you.sustaining` lists what a monster sustains; a Sustain is a 1-action step, once a round, never in the round of the cast. A damaging basic-save area whose Sustain repeats it (Earth's Bile) is aimed and run again with no slot spent. One not Sustained by the end of its caster's turn ends: its area leaves the map and the chat says so.
- **What's left after a spend (0.46.0; Rhea: "when a power or use is used, the amount they have left should be stated").** A spent step's line on the card reads the sheet: slots of that rank, prepared copies, innate uses or Focus Points left.
- **Fixes from the 2026-11-25 playtest (0.46.1).** An emanation or burst that is for allies (its target line names allies but not "non-allies" or enemies, or, with no target line, its text says "you and allies"; no damage, no save, no attack: Bless, Courageous Anthem) is no longer refused for catching allies. A save spell whose text says "critically fails" or "critical failure" (Daze: Stunned 1) counts as having a critical text, so the GM gets the note for it (`critText` matched only "critical success"). An effect with a ChoiceSet that has only one choice the creature can take (Kinetic Aura) takes it instead of going to the GM, and a toggleable roll option on an effect the module makes is switched on (Channeler's Stance: the +rank to healing was missing).
- **Buffs are put on by the module (0.47.0, P-1; Rhea, 2026-11-25, 12:14 PM, her pick on the playtest's u-176: "Apply them automatically", so an Auto turn keeps going instead of waiting on a card).** A spell with no damage, save or attack whose text links one spell effect ("Spell Effect: ..." from PF2e's spell effects) is cast and its effect put on by the module: on the monster itself for a spell with no target and no area (Shield, Mystic Armor), on the one friend it targets (Haste, Heroism; at a foe it stays the GM's card), or on itself and every friend its area catches (Courageous Anthem), with no area left on the map. The effect gets the cast rank as its level (Heroism's bonus, Bless's aura size) and the caster as its origin, as PF2e's own drop sets them. An effect that spreads itself as an aura (Bless: PF2e's Aura rule hands out the same effect) goes on the caster only, and the aura gives it to friends inside. An effect that asks a choice nobody here can answer goes to the GM, as before. Angel's readings: a dead friend is refused; a buff already on a friend isn't put on twice (the card says it is on). Closes u-176.
- **Heal, Harm and their kin at every count of actions, chosen with real thought (0.47.0, P-2; Rhea, 2026-11-25, 1:09 PM, after the playtest's u-177: "She should be able to do those actions yes, but that needs to be a real thought call because thats giving up a spellslot that can't heal now so thats a big chooise to make").** By PF2e's own variants (`vitalOf`): at one friend of the kind it heals (Heal and Lay on Hands: living; Harm: undead) it heals, with 1 action by touch and 2 within 30 feet; at one foe of the kind it hurts (Heal: undead; Harm: living) it is cast as its own "vs." variant and the foe rolls a basic Fortitude save, damage by degree, as any save spell (players roll their own); with 3 actions its 30-foot emanation heals every creature of its kind in it, friend or foe, the caster too (that version "targets all living and undead creatures", with no willing clause), and every creature of the other kind in it rolls the save and takes the damage, friend or foe (a friend it would hurt, or a foe it would heal, is refused unless the plan says `deliberate`). With 1 or 2 actions a foe of the kind it heals is unwilling and is never healed (those versions heal only a willing creature), and a construct is touched by neither. The save is the variant's own (Lay on Hands' base item has none), and a destroyed foe is refused. Aiming one the wrong way is refused with the reason. Lay on Hands also puts its effect on: +2 AC on a friend other than itself, and on an undead foe that fails its save the AC penalty. **The thinker's side:** the packet's `vital` says what each count of actions does, `being` marks an ally or a perceived creature undead or a construct, and the instructions' "Healing or harming" asks it to weigh its slots left, its own HP and its allies' health words, who else heals, and the foes, before spending a slot on damage that could heal later, and to say in the reason what it weighed. Closes u-177.
- **A friend of the kind Harm heals is only an undead creature (0.47.0, Angel's reading).** A living creature with void healing (some dhampirs and others) would be healed by Harm under the rules; the module reads only PF2e's mode of being (`modeOfBeing`), so it treats them as living and refuses Harm on them as a friend. The GM's to cast from the card if it ever comes up.

## How it works
- **`leaveWaiting` also closes a `fled` request (0.11.0)** whose turn moved on without a press, with the note "The turn moved on while it stood at the map's edge." (`docs/features/fleeing.md`).
- **Spells (0.6.0).** `{ do: "spell", item: "<id>", target: "E1", rank: 2, actions: 2 }` (`rank` and `actions` optional). Rhea, 2026-11-07: a spell attack, and a save spell, at one creature it perceives run for real; anything else is cast and the GM applies it from its card (her choice to start with; she may ask the module to take more over).
  - **The spellbook (`spellBook`)** comes from PF2e's own spellcasting sheet data (`entry.getSheetData()`, what a sheet shows): prepared slots not yet expended, a spontaneous caster's spells by rank with that rank's slots, innate spells with their uses, focus spells and the focus pool, cantrips. One entry per spell per spellcasting entry, with the ranks it can be cast at, each with its **pool**: a spontaneous caster's slots of one rank (shared by every spell of that rank), a prepared caster's copies of one spell at one rank, an innate spell's uses, the focus pool (with the spell's cost in points); cantrips and at-will spells have none. `checkPlan` spends pools through the plan, so two casts can't share one slot. Rituals and staffs, wands and scrolls aren't in it. **Ids stay put through a turn:** a spell known in two entries is `slug` in the first and `slug-2` in the second by entry order, whatever is left to cast (found by the review: ids re-numbered as entries ran out). The packet's `you.spells` gives each spell's ranks and what's left, actions, range, targets, area, defense with its DC or attack bonus, its base damage (`baseDamage`: heightening is in its text), traits and text (900 characters at most).
  - **The check (`checkSpell`):** actions from PF2e's `time` (`spellActions`: "2", "1 to 3", "free"; a reaction or anything longer is refused), a choice of actions needs `actions`; the rank asked for or the lowest with enough left; a target for a spell that targets creatures (`spellTargets` reads PF2e's target text) and none for one that doesn't, except that an area spell may name the creature to aim at; the target is a label it perceives, an ally's label or its own (`alliesByLabel`); range from `spellRange` ("30 feet"), touch by the creature's own reach (`getReach({ action: "interact" })`, not a weapon's); Restrained refuses attack and manipulate spells; a spell with the attack trait counts toward the multiple attack penalty and ends Take Cover; the flat check for a concealed or hidden target (not for an area). **Mode:** `attack` (a spell attack at one creature), `save` (a save at one creature, with Toolbelt's Target Helper on), otherwise `card`. **Never run for real:** a spell whose effect changes with the actions spent (PF2e `hasVariants` and a choice of actions: Heal and Harm, whose base item reads as a basic Fortitude save at "varies" range, so 1-action Heal on an ally would have rolled the ally's save; found by the review). Variants with fixed actions don't count: Ignition's are melee or ranged, and its base item is the ranged spell (30 ft, 2d4), which runs as it is (found live: the first rule sent Ignition to the GM), a spell at an ally, an area, more than one target.
  - **Carrying it out (`runSpell`, stages from `nextSpell`):** `cast` through the entry's own `cast(spell, { rank, consume: true })`, which spends the slot or use and posts the card (type `spell-cast`; a spell with no check, damage or save, such as Mist, gets no `context` at all, only PF2e's `casting` flag, so `onCreateMessage` takes that as the cast card too: found live 2026-11-07, before which such a cast timed out after 20 s), with the runner aiming at the target so Toolbelt puts its row on the card; then the flat check (card mode too: a failure means the spell doesn't affect them); then by mode. **Attack:** `spell.rollAttack(event, attackNumber)` by the Shift rule, its card waited for as `spell-attack-roll` or `attack-roll`; a missing degree goes to the GM, never a silent critical miss; on a hit, `rollDamage(event, mapIncreases)` on the variant at the cast rank (`loadVariant({ castRank })`, because PF2e reads the rank from the card's button, which a script has none of). **PF2e rolls a spell attack's damage as a hit** (`outcome: "success"`; its DOUBLE button is the critical), so the module applies it doubled on a critical hit (`spellMultiplier`), or with automatic damage off tells the GM to use Double. A non-damaging attack that hits, and anything a spell's text gives a degree beyond damage (`critText`: its text lists a critical success), goes to the GM in a note. **Every card a spell step makes carries Workbench's `noAutoDamageRoll`** (`pending.all`): Workbench reads it on cast cards too (its damage handler skips any card with it).
  - **Saves (`awaitSave`), Rhea's decisions:** a player rolls their own save from their row on the spell's card and **the turn waits with no timer**: the step returns `wait`, the request goes to state `saves` (in `RESUMABLE`), and the GM's card shows "Waiting for … (their row on the spell's card)" with **Roll it for them**. A creature no player owns, or whose player is offline, is rolled at once. The result is read from Toolbelt's flag on the card, `targetHelper.saveVariants.<variant>.saves[tokenId].success` (`savedDegree`; Workbench reads the same). **The module rolls through Toolbelt's own buttons** in the runner's chat log (`saveButton`, `rollSaveOnCard`): the d20 `.pf2e-toolbelt-target-rollSaves` for creatures no player owns, a row's `[data-action=roll-save]` otherwise, clicked with `shiftKey` equal to the user's `showCheckDialogs` (Toolbelt flips the dialog on Shift like PF2e: a plain click opened PF2e's Modifier dialog on the Bridge's page, tested). That keeps one result per target on the card, where players and Workbench see it. **Rows carry no token id:** Toolbelt orders them for a GM by whether a player owns the token (no owner first) then by name (`createTargetsRows` in its 3.60.0 `main.js`), and the row's name is checked too. Basic save damage is rolled once (`rollDamage`) and applied by degree with the roll's own `alter` (none, half, full, double: `SAVE_MULT`, what PF2e's Half and Double buttons do); a non-basic or non-damaging save spell goes to the GM with the degree. A card with no rows (Target Helper off on that page) is the GM's (`noRows`).
  - **Resuming a waiting turn:** the runner's `updateChatMessage` hook (`onMessageUpdate`) resumes it when Toolbelt writes to the cast card; Roll it for them writes only `rollFor` (no `go`, so it can't run the next step if the save landed first: found by the review) and `act` resumes on it; `doIt` looks once more after a run ends in `saves`, for a save that landed between reading the card and writing the state (found by the review); the runner's `ready` resumes one too. The result, `waiting` cleared and the `rollFor` it made unnecessary go in one save. **The turn moving on while a save waits** ends that request with a note (`leaveWaiting`), so the GM knows what's left on the card.
  - **Checked live before the build (2026-11-07):** `entry.cast` posts only the card; the Wight's cast card stored Toolbelt's targets and `saveVariants.null { dc, basic, statistic }`; the d20 and a PC's row both rolled and wrote the flag from a scripted click with Shift; the Bridge's message mode is public, so the card and its rows reach players.
  - **Not done:** rerolls on a row (a Hero Point) after damage is applied don't correct it; the chat log must have the card drawn on the runner's page for its buttons (after a reload with many newer messages it may not: the card says it couldn't find the button and the GM clicks the row on their own card); Sustain, counteracting, reaction spells; heightened damage in the packet; staffs, wands and scrolls.
**Design-era text (from the plan; where it and the lines above disagree, the lines above, written from the code, win):**
- **Spells:** read from `actor.spellcasting` with slots and DCs. The call is `entry.cast(spell, {rank, slotId, consume, message, messageMode})` [CODE, not run]. First test of this step.
- **0.27.0 (`reactions.md`):** a spell attack's hit asks Shield Block before its damage (`hitGate` with the spell's damage types); the apply stage asks the damage gate first and passes `{ resist, less }` and the block to `applyFromCard`; with automatic damage off `harmByHand` asks after the roll.

- **The save moments (0.38.0):** before a spell with a save is cast, the party's reactions to that save are asked in the cast question (`beforeAction`; a creature spared by Skeptic's Defense skips its save and damage, `log.<step>.spared`), and once the target's save is in `awaitSave` asks the reactions to the result (`saveAfter`) before writing it: one may change the degree, or stop the turn ("stopped" once the save is written). The detail is the reactions feature's (`docs/features/reactions.md`, RX-5).
- **Missiles** (`checkSpell` mode `missiles`): `MISSILES` names the slugs; `missileCount(actions, rank)`; `spellToRoll` clones the spell with the summed formula and rolls with Shift so no damage dialog opens on the runner's page. **Heals** (mode `heal`): `spellData` adds `vital` from `vitalOf(base)` (0.47.0: `mends`, `hurts`, and per action count `heal`, `hurt` and `area`, each with its overlay id, range and touch or area; `heal` stays on the spell for the older code) and `effect` from `spellEffectOf(base)`; `spellToRoll` loads one overlay (`loadVariant({castRank, overlayIds:[id]})`, never two: PF2e refuses two override overlays); the apply stage calls `actor.applyDamage` with the total as a negative number.
- **Sustain:** each cast of a spell marked sustained is recorded on the fight (`noteSustain`, `sustain.<token>.<key>`, with the region ids of its area from `sustainArea`); the packet's `you.sustaining` comes from `sustainingOf`; a `sustain` step either runs the area again through the normal area stages (`sustain: true`, no slot) or posts "Sustains X" (`runSustain`). `endUnsustained` runs at each turn change after the old turn ends: anything of that creature not Sustained this round is dropped with its regions (`dropSustainAreas`) and a chat line.

- **Buffs** (mode `buff`, 0.47.0): `checkSpell` gives it to a spell with `effect` and no damage, save or attack, at itself, one friend, or an area for friends (the `forFriends` test). `nextSpell` and `nextArea` run cast then `effect`; `runSpell`'s effect stage and `runArea`'s put it on through `putEffect` (abilities) with `spell: { tok, item, rank }`, which sets `system.level.value` and the effect's `system.context` origin; `spreadsItself` finds an Aura rule handing out the same effect (Bless), and then only the caster gets it. `doneStep` says who it is on (`effectOn`: labels, "itself").
- **Heal and Harm** (0.47.0): `checkSpell` picks by the target's kind (`undead`, `notLiving` from `beingFlags`, a hidden one too; `dead` refuses a destroyed one) and the actions: `heal` mode with the mend overlay, `save` mode with the hurt overlay and `castOverlay` (`runSpell` casts `spell.loadVariant({ castRank, overlayIds })`, so the card and Toolbelt's row carry that version's save), or with 3 actions `area` mode on the 3-action overlay's emanation (`spA` stands in for the spell with that area) with `vital: { mends, hurts }`. `runArea`'s cast stage gives a row only to the kind it hurts and lists `mends` (friends of its kind, itself included, not dead); `nextArea` rolls once if anyone is hurt or mended, applies the hurt by degree, then `mend` applies the same total as healing to each. `spellToRoll` loads the step's overlay for any mode. `effectOnFail` (Lay on Hands at an undead foe) puts the effect on after a failed save, before the damage.

## Traps
- **Spells: PF2e's own calls do one thing each.** `entry.cast(spell, { rank, consume: true })` spends the slot or use and posts the card only (`spell-cast`); attack, damage and saves are separate. A spell attack's card came as `attack-roll`, not `spell-attack-roll` (both are waited for). **PF2e rolls a spell attack's damage as a hit** (`outcome: "success"`): the module doubles it on a critical. `rollDamage` reads the cast rank from the card's button, so heightened damage goes through `loadVariant({ castRank })`. An area is placed only by mouse in PF2e (`placeRegion`), so the module makes the Region itself.
- **PF2e "variants" aren't all by actions:** Heal and Harm change with the actions spent (base item: a basic Fortitude save at "varies" range, which would have rolled an ally's save), but Ignition's are melee or ranged with fixed actions, and its base item is the ranged spell. Only spells whose actions vary go to the GM for this (0.6.1; 0.6.0 sent Ignition to the card).
- **Toolbelt's save buttons from code need the Shift rule** (as PF2e's rolls do): a plain click opened PF2e's Modifier dialog on the Bridge's page. Rows carry no token id; for a GM they're ordered no player owner first, then by name, and the name is checked. Clicking in a detached `renderHTML()` does nothing; the chat log's copy has the listeners. Toolbelt's d20 rolls only creatures that haven't saved yet. Details in `angel-mod-private/docs/foundry-s8-toolbelt.md`. A save on the card's flag carries its total as `value` (zod schema in Toolbelt 3.60.0's `scripts/main.js`).
- **A saved request's `rollFor`, `go` and the waiting state:** Roll it for them writes only `rollFor` (with `go` it could run the next step if the save landed first), and the result, `waiting` cleared and `rollFor` cleared go in one save (found by the review). A save landing between reading the card and writing `saves` fires nothing the runner acts on, so `doIt` looks once more after a run ends in `saves`.
