Interact and Delay: an Interact posts a card with the thinker's words for the GM to apply; a Delay ends the turn and moves the monster's initiative to just after the creature it waits for.

## Decisions
- **Interact is the GM's to apply (Angel's design, 2026-11-08; Rhea chose to build the step kinds in her listed order and skipped trimming the packet first).** The module can't know what a monster does with its hands (draw a weapon, open a door, pick something up), and on the Survey Sheet's outdoor maps there is little it could do by itself anyway, so `{ do: "interact", what }` posts a public chat card "Interact: <what>" from the monster and the turn pauses for the GM, exactly as an ability the module can't run does. `what` is the thinker's words, labels only (the prompt forbids names), at most 120 characters; on the card its labels become token names (as the Monster Turns card does) and the text is HTML-escaped. Rejected: a table of things the module could do itself (equip an item, toggle a door): no map has doors yet, and NPC Strikes aren't inventory items, so nothing would be gained for the cost.
- **Interact's traits are enforced:** restrained refuses it (Rules: Restrained bars manipulate actions), grabbed asks a DC 5 flat check first (Rules: Grabbed), rolled by the module; a failure spends the action on nothing, as a Strike's failed flat check does. It comes out of hiding like any other action (`revealsBefore`). The same grabbed check isn't yet asked of manipulate abilities or spells: an open item, below.
- **Delay by moving the initiative (Angel's design, 2026-11-08).** Neither PF2e 8.7.0 nor any installed module tracks Delay, so the module does it the way the rules read: `{ do: "delay", after }` must be the plan's only step and nothing may have been done this turn (Delay is a free action as the turn begins), and `after` must name a creature the monster knows of whose turn is still to come this round. The module ends the turn as usual (so start- and end-of-turn effects happen at once, as the rules say) and then sets the monster's initiative to that creature's, with PF2e's own tie-break priority (`overridePriority`) just above theirs, so core's turn order brings the monster back right after them with a normal turn start and a fresh packet. **PF2e HUD's own Delay (found during the 0.12.0 live round):** its tracker has an hourglass button per combatant that sets the combatant flag `pf2e-hud.delayed` (shown in the tracker); a second press clears it, re-places the creature right after the current combatant (`setInitiativeFromDrop` numbers tie priorities by position, then `setMultipleInitiatives`) and calls `nextTurn`. The module sets that flag when a monster Delays, so the tracker shows it delayed, and clears it when the monster's turn comes back; the GM can also bring it back early with the HUD's own button, which starts its turn and the module's `afterDelay` path picks it up. Rejected: a separate "delayed" state that watches for the creature's turn ending and re-inserts the monster; the order change does the same with no hooks, and survives a reload. Rejected: allowing a creature that has already acted this round (the rules then lose the turn's actions and leave the initiative where it was, so the step would only waste the turn).

## How it works
- **Burrowed allies (0.16.0):** a Delay can name a burrowed ally (`turnOrder` uses `knownAllies`, the packet's own list), but not one the GM had hidden before it went down.
- `checkPlan` (turn.mjs): `interact` needs `what` (a non-empty string, cut to `INTERACT_MAX` = 120); `ctx.cantAttack` (restrained) refuses; `ctx.grabbed` adds `flat: GRABBED_FLAT` (5). `delay` needs `n === 0` and a one-step plan, `ctx.fresh` (nothing spent, done or begun this turn: `gather` sets it from the request's `spent`, `done` and `used`, and false for a mid-plan check), and `ctx.order[after] === "later"`; `"self"` and `"earlier"` and unknown labels each get their own words. Costs: interact 1, delay 0. `delay` is in `KEEPS_HIDDEN` (no action at all); `interact` isn't.
- `cleanPlan` (brain.mjs) keeps `what` (cleaned text, 120) and `after` (a label).
- `turnOrder(combat, tok, req)` (fight.mjs): for each label the monster could name (the request's perceived labels, its allies that aren't hidden, itself), `"later"` when that creature's index in `combat.turns` is after the monster's, `"earlier"` otherwise, `"self"` for itself; creatures not in the fight or defeated are left out, so the refusal says "doesn't know of".
- `runInteract`: stages by `nextInteract` (turn.mjs, unit-tested) from the step's log: `flat` (a plain `1d20` card, flavour "Flat check, DC 5 (grabbed, a manipulate action)"; a failure adds the note and returns `next`), then `post` (one `ChatMessage` from the token, `<strong>Interact</strong> <span class="action-glyph">1</span>: <what>`, the record and the note "Apply what it does from its Interact card." in one save), then `gm`: `runTurn`'s existing path adds "Then press Next step." and waits, Auto or not.
- **The delayed request is marked `delayed`** and the returned turn's request `afterDelay`: `onTurnChange`'s round-and-combatant guard ignores delayed requests (so the monster gets a new request and packet when it comes back the same round), `endLeftOver` never ends a turn for one, `currentOf` already leaves done requests out, and the history keys the returned turn apart ("<name> (back after delaying)", the delayed-on turn placed by the `turn` it was at). Found by the 0.12.0 review.
- `runDelay` saves the turn pointer itself after the initiative move (`combat.update({ turn }, { diff: false, turnEvents: false })` for the creature whose turn it now is): seen live on the test build, the server's turn number stayed where `nextTurn` put it and named the monster again after the re-sort. Then PF2e HUD's `delayed` flag.
- `runDelay`: finds the monster's and the other creature's combatants (the other through `tokenIdOf`, so an ally label works); stops the turn for the GM with a note (nothing has happened yet, so "stopped", not "ask") if the other left the fight, is defeated, or has no turn still to come this round (its place in `combat.turns` is before the monster's), or if it isn't the monster's turn; (no note about start-of-turn effects since 0.23.0: Traps); records `ended` with the target's initiative and the priority it will use (`overridePriority(initiative) ?? actor.initiative.tiebreakPriority ?? 3`, plus 0.5: PF2e sorts ties by priority ascending, then id); `combat.nextTurn()`; `combat.setMultipleInitiatives([{ id, value, statistic, overridePriority }])` (PF2e's own, which writes `flags.pf2e.overridePriority[value]`); records `moved`; returns `delayed`, and `runTurn` marks the request done without `endAfter`. On the runner, `keepTurn` keeps the current turn (the next creature's) through the initiative change, as for Workbench's moves. A resume after a reload: `moved` → done; `ended` without `moved` → `ask` (the GM checks the tracker).
- Words: `describeStep` ("Interacts: … Its card goes to chat for you to apply." / "Delays until after E2's turn: its turn ends now, and it comes back into the order right after E2 (free action)."), `doneStep` for the next packet, `stepResult` in history.mjs (the card tagged; "turn ended; back in the order right after <name>"), `actWords` "its Interact" for coming out of hiding. Prompt version 14 explains both.

## Traps
- **A request is keyed by round and combatant, and a Delay gives the same monster two turns in one round:** without the `delayed` mark the guard in `onTurnChange` skipped the returned turn (no request, no card) and `endLeftOver` ended it under Auto within 12 s (found by the 0.12.0 review before any live test).
- **Start- and end-of-turn effects do not run twice (seen live 2026-11-10 on 0.22.0, PF2e 8.7.0; the opposite was assumed from 0.12.0 on and never tested).** PF2e runs a creature's turn start and turn end once a round (its combatant's `roundOfLastTurn` and `roundOfLastTurnEnd` flags). A monster with persistent damage (1, DC 21) that Delayed took it once, as its turn ended by the Delay (`pf2e.endTurn` on the active GM's page); when it came back the same round, by the order or by PF2e HUD's hourglass, neither `pf2e.startTurn` nor `pf2e.endTurn` fired for it and its HP didn't move at the end of the returned turn. That is what the rules say too. So 0.23.0 took the card's note ("if they are applied again when it comes back, undo them") out of `runDelay`.
- **`endLeftOver` ended a turn whose last step left a card for the GM** (an ability, now an Interact too), against Rhea's rule that Auto never ends a turn after such a card: requests ended that way are marked `gmEnded` and skipped (pre-existing since 0.9.1, found by the 0.12.0 review).
- **A reload between `nextTurn` and the initiative move** leaves the request `running` on a turn that is no longer current, where nothing resumes it: `onRunnerReady` closes it (`done`, `delayed`) with a note to check the tracker.
- **Ties at the other creature's initiative:** the monster takes that creature's priority plus 0.5, so when several creatures share both the initiative and the priority (two PCs at 15, both priority 2) it lands after all of them, not right after the one named. Documented, not fixed: PF2e's tie-break has no finer grain without renumbering the others.
- **A Delay after a creature that already acted this round is pointless:** the rules lose the turn's actions and leave the initiative where it was, so `checkPlan` refuses it rather than ending a turn for nothing.
- **The turn pointer isn't kept through the re-sort on its own** (first live run of the test build): `keepTurn` ran, but the server ended with the turn number `nextTurn` had written, which after the re-sort pointed at the monster again. `runDelay` saves the pointer for the creature whose turn it is, with `diff: false`.
- **Order of the two writes in `runDelay` matters:** `nextTurn` first, while the monster still sits at its old place, so its end of turn and the next creature's start fire once and in order; the initiative move after, kept from shifting the current turn by `keepTurn`. Moving first would make PF2e try to keep the monster current at its new place.
- **PF2e sorts ties by `overridePriority(initiative)`, then the actor's `tiebreakPriority`, then id, ascending:** the monster takes the other's priority plus 0.5 to land right after it. A third creature at the same initiative with a priority between would come between them; none has fractional priorities unless the GM dragged them.

## Open items
- The grabbed DC 5 flat check isn't asked of manipulate abilities or spells yet (only of Interact).
- Interact can't do anything itself (equip an item, open a door); a table of things it could run is a later slice if maps with doors or loot to grab arrive.
- Reactions to a manipulate action (Reactive Strike) are the reactions feature's, not built.
- A returned turn fires no `pf2e.startTurn` (Traps), so anything another module does at a turn's start doesn't happen for it either: right by the rules for persistent damage and durations; not looked at for anything else.
- Several creatures tied at the named one's initiative and priority: the monster lands after all of them (Traps).
- A reload between `nextTurn` and the initiative move (Traps): closed by `onRunnerReady`, not reproduced live.
