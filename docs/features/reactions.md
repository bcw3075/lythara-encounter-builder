Player reactions during a monster's turn: Reactive Strike, Shield Block, Reactive Shield and Nimble Dodge asked at the right moment, in the player's window or on the GM's card. And monsters' Reactive Strikes on players' turns (0.21.0): offered on the GM's card, or taken at once by a monster with Angel and Auto ticked. And the party's own reactions (0.27.0): a reaction box for every party member, a champion's reaction to an ally's damage with the monster's repent or refuse, a reaction spell that shields its caster, and every reduction said in chat with its reason.

## Decisions
- **Monsters reacting on players' turns, who decides (Rhea, 2026-11-09, 0.21.0):** asked who decides when a player's action gives a monster a Reactive Strike, she chose "1": a monster with Angel and Auto ticked takes it by itself at once; any other monster shows Do it / Skip on the Monster Turns card. Rejected: always her click; the thinker deciding each time (seconds on a player's turn and usage for a choice that is nearly always yes).
- **Which actions trigger it (Rhea, 2026-11-09, 0.21.0):** asked whether to cover moves only, moves with ranged attacks and spells, or everything, she chose the third: "basically it should be anything that the rules state". And: "It's not all spells, it's only spells that have the manipulate tag": a spell counts through its own traits, never for being a spell.
- **Unsure triggers (Rhea, 2026-11-09, 0.21.0):** asked whether an Auto monster should strike when the module isn't sure a trigger happened, she chose "1": unsure cases always come to her card with the reason and Do it / Skip, even for an Auto monster. "For cases where you are unsure, we can later build a button for the players to click to do these actions" (Open items). Rejected: Auto monsters striking on anything that looks like a trigger.
- **Angel's readings within those (2026-11-09, 0.21.0):** only Reactive Strike is run for a monster; its other reactions are listed on the card as hers to play. The reaction comes just after the action shows (the design's first version: a player's drag isn't interrupted), measured from the square the creature left. A monster reacts once a round and has its reaction back when its own turn begins. An offer nobody answers lapses when the turn moves on, and Skip leaves the monster its reaction. A critical hit on a manipulate action is noted as disrupting it; undoing the action is the GM's call, since it already shows. What is sure: a token move made as Stride, Crawl, Climb, Swim, Fly, Burrow or Leap of more than one walked square on the mover's own turn; a ranged attack roll; a cast card whose spell has manipulate; an action card tagged manipulate (not Release) or move (Stand, Drop Prone, any move action that isn't itself a token move); a potion, elixir, oil, mutagen or drug used. What is never sure: one walked square with no Step card before it; a move outside the mover's turn or made by a script; an item changing hands with no card; Prone coming off with no Stand card; another kind of consumable.
- **Two releases (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked whether to build player reactions as one release or two, she chose "Two: players first": this release is players reacting during a monster's turn; monsters reacting on a player's turn (the `moveToken` hook and "Goblin can Reactive Strike: Do it / Skip" on her panel, from the design) is the next. Rejected: both halves at once.
- **The timer (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked what range the world setting allows and whether 0 means no countdown, she chose "Option one, but only if a player has the right feat to get a reaction": 5 to 60 seconds, default 20, 0 = no countdown (the turn waits for the player or the GM's Continue); and **a player is asked only when their character has a reaction that fits the trigger**. Rejected: 5 to 60 always timed; no timer at all.
- **No countdown on the GM's own rows (Rhea, 2026-11-09, 0.20.1):** asked whether the countdown should also run on reactions she answers on the Monster Turns card (a party member she plays, or a character whose player is offline), she chose "No timer on my card": her rows wait for React, Pass or Continue; players' windows keep the countdown. Rejected: the same timer for everyone (as 0.20.0 built it: her Shield Block passed in testing before the card was read). A reaction the module doesn't know is never asked: the card lists it with its Trigger text ("Not asked by the module: …") so the GM can Hold if it applies.
- **From the design (2026-11-05/06), unchanged:** a prompt in the player's browser through the module's own socket (no socketlib); React then the player rolls as usual (PF2e HUD or the sheet) and presses Done; the GM's card always has Continue and shows who is pending; a player offline, or a party member the GM plays, is the GM's to answer, on the card; the module counts no reactions (a player with none left passes); Shield Block goes through the prompt before damage, not a button after it; after any reaction the rest of the plan is rechecked (the look before the next action re-plans on any change).
- **Rules corrections from the review (2026-11-09):** Release asks nothing (it doesn't trigger reactions that manipulate actions trigger, Player Core pg. 417, checked on Archives of Nethys); Shield Block and Reactive Shield don't need the reactor to see the monster (only Reactive Strike and Nimble Dodge are asked of a creature that perceives it); Nimble Dodge is asked only for a Strike (the one attack whose degree the module reads again); a Climb is asked from where the monster was before it climbed; confused characters aren't asked.
- **Angel's readings within those (2026-11-09):** a reaction that needs no roll (Shield Block, Reactive Shield, Nimble Dodge) is used by pressing React; only Reactive Strike asks for Done. Closing the player's window without a button is a Pass. A reaction to a manipulate action or a move happens and the action still goes on, unless the monster is down, can't move (the move stops there) or a Reactive Strike critically hit a manipulate action (the action is spent, as the rules say). A move action's reaction doesn't re-plan the move itself: what changed is seen by the look before the next step.
- **The party's own reactions must be handled robustly (Rhea, 2026-11-11, 0.27.0):** "Please make sure you work these options into the build since we know the party can do them, they need a robust system to handle them." Built as a release of its own, before the leader's orders ("Your choice with whatever makes the most sense. Timing isn't important").
- **(PR-1) Repent or refuse, who decides (Rhea, 2026-11-11: "Lets go with 2"):** asked who decides a monster's answer to Glimpse of Redemption, she chose the thinker deciding at the moment, through the monster's mind (its notebook and how the fight stands). A mindless creature, or one unable to repent, refuses by the rule with no question (Player Core 2 pg. 93, checked on Archives of Nethys 2026-11-11); a monster she plays herself gets Repent and Refuse buttons on her card. Rejected: decided ahead by a new line in the mind (no pause and no usage, as with monsters' Reactive Strikes); always her click. Cost she accepted: a few seconds and usage each time the reaction is used, which may be every round.
- **(PR-2) The party's reactions are tracked (Rhea, 2026-11-11):** "we are going to need to build in a checkbox somewhere to track if a player has their reaction still for that round. It would auto track when an action is used that consumes it and it would come back at the start of their turn. I'd treat it like a focus point, kinda that refreshes every round." This replaces the 0.20.0 line "the module counts no reactions (a player with none left passes)": a character whose reaction is used is no longer asked that round.
- **(PR-3) Where the box lives (Rhea, 2026-11-11, after a sketch: "Ok, lets start with that"):** a "Party reactions" block on the Monster Turns window, one box per party member (tick = used this round, with how it was ticked), which she can click for anyone; and each player gets a small marker for their own character only, which they can click to mark or undo. Each box clears when that character's turn begins. Nothing on tokens, nothing in PF2e HUD's tracker. Rejected: her card only; a PF2e effect on the token. The card says why someone wasn't asked ("Shield Block not asked (reaction already used)").
- **(PR-4) The player's marker (Rhea, 2026-11-11: "1"):** a small chip the player can drag anywhere; it shows only while a fight is running, starts in a corner and remembers where each player put it. Rejected: only inside the reaction question window (or a token-controls button); a chat line with Undo.
- **(PR-5) Which damage it covers (Rhea, 2026-11-11: "1"):** everything a monster does through the module: Strikes, spell attacks, area spells and abilities with saves, and monsters' own Reactive Strikes. Rejected: Strikes only now, the rest in the next release. Persistent damage stays hers to rule (Workbench applies it; whether it is "an enemy damages your ally" is a GM call).
- **(PR-6) Ask only when there is a reaction to use, think only when it is used (Rhea, 2026-11-11):** "the popup should only apply when they have a reaction to use. Also, Angel only needs to stop and think about it if they have a reaction, otherwise resources don't need to be used for it." So: no window for a character whose box is ticked or who has no reaction that fits; and the thinker is called for repent or refuse only after React is pressed, never on a Pass, a timeout, a ticked box, or for a mindless creature.
- **(PR-7) Interposing Earth's barrier (Rhea, 2026-11-11: "Sure, I'm fine with that. Make sure we don't forget it"):** this release handles the moment: standard cover against the triggering attack or area, 2 off the damage if the caster is still damaged (the barrier is then destroyed), the slot spent; a barrier that survives (3 rounds, on the border between two squares, AC 5, Hardness 2, 5 HP; Rage of Elements pg. 95, checked on Archives of Nethys 2026-11-11) is a line on her card and hers to rule on. Putting the slab on the map is part of the future full pass (Open items). The spell has the manipulate trait, so casting it can draw a monster's Reactive Strike: it goes through the same watcher as any manipulate cast card.
- **(PR-8) Glimpse of Redemption is asked only when the aura rule holds (Rhea, 2026-11-11):** "it needs to know to only ask if the aura rules apply." Both the enemy that did the damage and the damaged ally must be inside the champion's aura at that moment (the ally is never the champion); otherwise no window, no thought. The aura's size is read from the creature (PF2e's own champion's aura), never a typed 15 feet.
- **(PR-9) Before a character's first turn (Rhea, 2026-11-11: "1"):** everyone starts the fight with their reaction ready; she ticks one by hand for anyone caught off guard. The rule leaves it to the GM (Player Core, Reactions in Encounters, checked on Archives of Nethys 2026-11-11). Rejected: no reaction until the first turn; a "Party starts ready" tick each fight.
- **(PR-10) Every reduction says why (Rhea, 2026-11-11):** "make sure, when you reduce the damage, it's displayed why." Wherever damage is reduced, undone or turned into a miss by a reaction, the reason is shown with the numbers: in chat for everyone (the monster named as players see it), on her card and in the History. Never a whispered card.
- **(PR-11) Who is tracked and asked (Rhea, 2026-11-11: "1"):** the players' characters, a party member she plays, and any ordinary NPC fighting on the party's side (a guard, a rescued prisoner): a box each, asked like a party member, always on her card when nobody else plays them. Rejected: party members only. **Never asked, no box:** anything with the minion trait (summons, animal companions, familiars: minions can't use reactions) and a character who is Delaying (no reactions until back in the initiative order, Player Core pg. 416; both checked on Archives of Nethys 2026-11-11).
- **Angel's readings within these, told to Rhea 2026-11-11 and accepted ("They look fine"):** (a) the thinker is told the choice in words, not numbers; (b) Weight of Guilt: after a refusal the champion's player gets a second small window, Enfeebled 2 or Stupefied 2, and no answer in time is Enfeebled; **the window says what each one does, so her player never has to look it up (Rhea: "can you also include what Enfeebled and Stupefied do so she doesn't need to look it up?")**, in plain words checked against Player Core pp. 443 and 446; (c) an area that damages several allies in the aura: one reaction, her player picks which ally in the window; (d) Shield Block is found by what the character can do (a shield it can block with), not only a feat with that slug; (e) a reaction spell is offered only while it can be cast, and React casts it so the slot is spent and the cast card shows; (f) a look at the party's reactions at every level-up is a line in the module's skill, not code.
- **Settled by Angel in the build (0.27.0), told to Rhea, hers to change:** the GM's Repent and Refuse buttons are on her card for every monster, Angel's included, so she can answer when the thinker doesn't; a monster's Reactive Strike on a player's turn, which has no packet of its own, sends the thinker the packet from that monster's latest turn; a reaction spell is cast at the lowest rank that has a slot left; when a champion's reaction and Interposing Earth meet on one hit both apply (the resistance by PF2e, the 2 off the roll); with automatic damage off the question is still asked after the damage roll and her card says what to apply; a reaction whose card is posted from a sheet or PF2e HUD ticks the box (it can't be told from an ability only shown in chat, which is why a click clears it).

## How it works
- **The answers reach the notebooks (0.25.0):** when a question closes, `noteReactionAnswers` writes who struck as the monster moved (Reactive Strike) and who blocked (Shield Block) for every creature that saw it (`docs/features/memory.md`). Nothing about asking or answering changed.
- **Pure (`react.mjs`):** `KNOWN` (slug to name, gates and needs; each row checked against Archives of Nethys 2026-11-09; `attack-of-opportunity` is the old slug of Reactive Strike), `reactionsOf(items)` (reaction-type actions and feats; known ones by slug, one per name; others with `triggerOf` their description), `fits(slug, gate, who, what)`, `routeGates(route, mover, reactors)` (each reactor once per move action, before the mover leaves the first route square within its reach, measured with move.mjs `distanceBetween` including height; the end square isn't left), `degreeAgainst(total, natural, dc)`, `untilFor` and `countingDown` (the countdown: player rows only, 0.20.1), `gateWords`, `finished`, `answerWords`.
- **Gates** (runner, `fight.mjs`): before an action (`beforeAction`, key `before`): a manipulate action (Interact, an ability or spell with the manipulate trait; not Release), a ranged attack (a ranged Strike, a spell with the attack trait), a Strike at a character (Nimble Dodge for its target); Strike follow-ups (Grab and the like) are left out. Along a move (`moveGates` in `runStride`, keys `move:<route index>`): a Stride's, Crawl's or flee's whole route, the token moving in parts and stopping in the square it is leaving; a Fly, Swim or Burrow only where it starts. At the end of a Stand, Drop Prone or Climb (`stayGate`, key `stay`), with reach measured from where it was before the action. A hit before damage (`hitGate` in `runStrike`, key `hit`): Shield Block (shield raised and not broken; physical damage or unknown types) and Reactive Shield (melee; a shield held and not raised). A Step asks nothing.
- **Who** (`reactorsFor`): characters in the fight on the other side, not hidden, escaped or below ground, able to act (not dead, at 0 HP, unconscious, paralyzed, petrified, stunned or confused; restrained can't Reactive Strike), with a known reaction that fits; for Reactive Strike and Nimble Dodge also perceiving the monster (Visioner: not hidden, undetected or unnoticed; without Visioner, seen). Reach is `getReach({ action: "attack" })`. **One row per reaction** (`id` = token and slug): a character with Reactive Strike and Nimble Dodge gets two questions and two answers.
- **Asking** (`askReactions`): writes `react` on the request (`key`, `step`, `gate`, `list` with `to` "player" or "gm", `until` on the server clock, `answers` by row id, `cont`), moves `since` on (the stall check counts a wait for a reaction as a wait on a person: `stallOf` state `reacting`, 3 minutes) and marks `gates.<step>.<key>.asked`; sends `ask` on the socket for the players' windows; polls every 250 ms for answers (a player's arrive by socket and the runner writes them, checking the sender as Foundry reports it owns that character; a GM's card writes them directly), the timer (a player's row unanswered becomes `timeout`; the GM's own rows have none, and a question with only GM rows has no `until`), Continue (unanswered become `continue`), Hold (the same, and the step stops before it resolves: before an action or before a hit's damage; a move goes on to its end and the turn holds before the next step), the monster going down, or the turn moving on (`gone`). A question left open on the GM's card for 3 minutes turns the card amber like any wait on her (the stall check), and Angel is told. Each answer is written as its own key, so one arriving at the same moment isn't overwritten. On every way out `react` is cleared, `gates.<step>.<key>` keeps `{ done, list, rolls }` (so a step is never asked twice), `close` goes on the socket, and a note lists what was used (`answerWords`). A reload while asking keeps what was answered, skips the rest with a note to check the chat, and the turn goes on. Attack rolls made while a question is open are collected by the `createChatMessage` hook (`noteReactionRoll`), not compared by clocks.
- **What a reaction changes:** Nimble Dodge: the degree is read against the attack's own DC (which holds anything that applied only to that attack, such as flanking) plus what +2 circumstance adds over the circumstance bonus the target already had (`circNow`: they don't stack), recorded in the same save as the attack. Reactive Shield: the module raises the shield if the player didn't, and the degree is read against the attack's DC plus what the shield's bonus adds over the circumstance bonus the target had without it; a hit that becomes a miss skips damage. Shield Block: the damage is applied with PF2e's `shieldBlockRequest` (with automatic damage off, a note says to use the card's Shield Block). A critical Reactive Strike by a reactor who used it, at the monster, while the question was open, on a manipulate action marks the step `disrupted` (spent, never run; any slot or use is the GM's call). A monster at 0 HP after any reaction ends its turn with a note.
- **The prompt** (`ui/react.mjs`, players only): one DialogV2 per reaction row with the sentence, React and Pass, and the countdown; Reactive Strike's React opens "Make your Reactive Strike now… then press Done". Closing the question is a Pass, closing the Done window is Done; a window the runner closes sends nothing. Foundry's DialogV2 runs `submit` before it closes (read in `foundry.mjs`, 14.370). The monster is named as players see it (`moverName`: its name when the token shows it, otherwise "A creature").
- **The card** (`ui/turns.mjs`): "Reactions:" with the sentence naming the monster (`gmWords`; the players' windows may say "A creature"), each reactor's state, React and Pass (then Done) on the GM's own ("yours to answer"), and Continue; the countdown redraws each second, only for the turn in hand while it runs. `otherReactions` lists the unknown reactions on the other side.
- **The thinker** (prompt version 23) is told which actions draw reactions and that a Step avoids Reactive Strike.
- World setting `reactionTimer` (0 to 60 in steps of 5, default 20). `module.json` has `"socket": true`.

- **Monsters' reactions (0.21.0), pure (`react.mjs`):** `MONSTER_REACTIONS`; `moveTrigger`, `cardTrigger`, `equipTrigger`, `proneOffTrigger` each answer `{ gate, sure, kind, why }` or null (`cardTrigger` also `{ step: true }` for a Step card); `squaresAlong` (the squares between a move's waypoints), `reactionStrike` (the best melee Strike that reaches), `offerWords` (the card's line); `readCard` (a card's title, slug, traits and whether it is an item only shown in chat, from its HTML and roll options).
- **Monsters' reactions, the runner (`fight.mjs`):** four hooks on the runner's page only: `moveToken` (`onTokenMoved`: the squares passed, from the movement's origin and the waypoints it has passed, the movement action of the first waypoint; methods undo, config and paste are ignored, `api` is never sure), `createChatMessage` (`onActionCard`, through `readCard`: PF2e's context type and options, a check's own `action:` option, the origin's roll options, and the card's own tags, since PF2e's action cards (Interact, Stand, Step) carry no flags; a used-up consumable is read from its compendium source and an item only shown in chat is no trigger; a spell attack is ranged unless the spell's range is touch), `updateItem` (`onItemHands`: only a change of carry type or hands held, against what the item was seen as before (`carried`, filled by `noteCarried` at each turn change and when the runner loads; an item never seen is not guessed at)), `deleteItem` (`onConditionGone`: Prone, decided 1.5 s later so a Stand card can land). `offerAll` asks `monsterReactors` (NPCs that are enemies of the creature, not GM-hidden, able to act and attack, with Reactive Strike, perceiving it as their own turns would (`sense`: observed or concealed; Visioner's record alone only when the runner isn't on the fight map), reaction unspent, no offer open or being written (`offering`)) and tests reach: for a move with `routeGates` over the squares left, otherwise from where the creature stands. `offerReaction` writes **a request of its own** (`reaction: { by, gate, sure, kind, why, strike, auto }`, `turnOf`, one Strike step with `variant` 0 and the flat check its sight of the creature needs, the creature under the label T), so `runStrike` runs it unchanged and the players' Shield Block and Reactive Shield are asked through the usual `hitGate`. `runReaction` marks the monster under `reacted` on the turns flag before the roll and takes the mark back if nothing was rolled; **one roller at a time**: `doReaction` queues reactions (`reactionLine`, `queued`) behind whatever is running, and `doIt` waits for a reaction in flight (`reactionsClear`), because a Strike's cards are matched by one waiter; `turnReactions` (every turn change) lapses unanswered offers and clears the mark of the creature whose turn begins. `doReaction` from another GM's browser writes `go` for the runner (`act`), `skipReaction` ends the offer. A reload mid-Strike ends it with a note (`onRunnerReady`), never repeating it.
- **A reaction request is never the turn's:** `currentOf` and `latestNote` (turn.mjs), `onTurnChange`, `fallIfAloft`, `sinkNote` and `endLeftOver` skip requests with `reaction`; `askReactions` tests "the turn moved on" against `turnOf`.
- **The card** (`ui/turns.mjs`): one box per offer ("Reaction: … can make a Reactive Strike (…) at …", "Not certain." with the reason, Do it and Skip, what came of it until the turn moves on), the players' question under it while a hit waits (`rxHtml`), and "Monsters' other reactions are yours to play" (`monsterReactionsLeft`). The history lists each as its own entry on the turn it happened (`historyOf`). API: `fight.reactions`, `fight.doReaction`, `fight.skipReaction`.

- **The party's reactions (0.27.0), pure (`react.mjs`):** two more `KNOWN` rows (each checked on Archives of Nethys 2026-11-11): Glimpse of Redemption (gate `harm`, `ally`, `aura`) and Interposing Earth (gates `attack` and `area`, `ac` 2, `spell`); `reactionsOf` takes reaction spells as items with `spell: { entryId, spellId, rank }` (a spell's row only for the spell); `fits` knows `isAlly`, `inAura` and `canCast`; `AC_ROWS`; `spentRows` (a creature's other rows once one is being used); `canReact` (a character or an NPC, never a minion); `shieldGrantsBlock`; `glimpseResistance` (2 + level); `earthLess` (2, 8 from 4th rank); `castRank`; `untilNextTurnEnds`; `GUILT`, `guiltLines`, `guiltWords`; `notAskedWords`; `harmWords` and `missWords` (the lines for everyone); `barrierWords` (the surviving barrier's Hardness and HP by the rank it was cast at); `choiceWords` and `CHOICES` (what the thinker is told).
- **The boxes** (`fight.mjs`): `turns.rx.<token id>` on the Combat's flag (`{ round, at, by: "module" | "card" | "hand", reaction, user }`), written by `markUsed` (the runner, when a row's answer becomes `react` or `done` in `askReactions`, or when `onReactionCard` sees the card of a reaction-type action or feat, or of a reaction spell being cast, spoken by a tracked creature), by `setReactionUsed` (a GM's page writes it; a player's goes to the runner as socket `rx` and is taken only for a character that player owns), and cleared in `turnReactions` when that creature's turn begins. A new fight has no flag, so everyone starts ready. `partyReactors(combat)` lists the rows (party alliance, `reactsLikeParty`, not defeated; `mine` on a player's page; `delaying` from PF2e HUD's `delayed` flag on the combatant). `reactorsFor` skips a creature whose box is ticked or that is Delaying and names it in `o.skipped`; `noteSkipped` puts "X: Shield Block not asked (reaction already used)." on the card once (moves too: `moveGates(...).skipped` holds those whose reach the route leaves).
- **Who** since 0.27.0 (`reactsLikeParty`): a character, or an NPC whose alliance is the party's, without the minion trait; `beforeKinds` asks the attack gate for any of them as the target. `reactionsFor` adds Shield Block for a held shield that grants it (`shieldGrantsBlock` on the shield's slug and text) and the creature's reaction spells (`reactionSpells`, `rankLeft`: read straight from the entry's slots, prepared spells, uses or focus points, with no sheet data, since it runs at every gate).
- **The damage gate** (`harmGate`, key `harm`): called just before each application of a monster's damage (a Strike's, an ability's, a spell's, each creature's share of an area's, a monster's Reactive Strike's) with `[{ target, hit }]`; for an area it is called with everyone hurt at the first application, so one question holds a row for each ally (`group`, `label`) and the player's window has a button for each. Rows come from `reactorsFor(..., "harm", { target })`: an ally of the target, never the target, with `inAura` true for both the monster and the target (PF2e's own `TokenAura#containsToken` where the map is drawn on the page, else the distance from the bearer's edge against the aura's radius read from the token or actor; no aura when it is suppressed). What came of it is saved on the step's record: `harm.<token id> = { asked, glimpse: { by, token, choice, who, reason, resist, guilt }, said }`, so a reload asks and applies nothing twice.
- **Repent or refuse** (`glimpseChoice`): mindless: refuse, `who: "rule"`. Otherwise `choice` is written on the request (`seq`, `kind`, `ally` (the creature's label as the monster knows it, or null), `allyName`, `by`, `words`, `angel`) and, when the monster is ticked for Angel and a packet exists (`packetFor`: the request's own, or the one from that monster's latest turn), a `choice` event goes out (brain.md). The answer is `choice.answer` with `answeredBy` "angel" (`submitPlan`) or "gm" (`chooseFor`, the card's buttons); the result is kept at `choices.<token id>` on the step's record. The turn being taken over, moving on or held ends the wait. The question carries `step` and `allyId`, so after a reload of the runner's page the same open question is waited on again (never written, or sent to the thinker, a second time), and an answer given meanwhile is taken. A thinker's answer may carry the event's `seq`; one that names another question is refused.
- **Weight of Guilt** (`guiltPick`): a second question (key `guilt:<ally's token id>`, one for each refusal) with one row marked `free` (no box is ticked, and it is asked whatever the box says), its own `lines` and `labels` (`react` = Stupefied 2, `pass` = Enfeebled 2); the players' countdown applies, and no answer is Enfeebled 2.
- **Applying it:** repent: nothing is applied to that creature. Refuse: `applyFromCard(..., { resist })` puts a one-rule effect (Resistance, all-damage) on the copy of the creature the damage is worked out on (`getContextualClone(origin, [effect])`), so PF2e does the arithmetic by damage type and its damage-taken card shows the resistance; nothing is added to the real creature. Interposing Earth's 2 comes off the roll once with `roll.alter(multiplier, -less)`, which keeps the damage types. Then `afterHarm`: `guiltEffect` gives the monster a PF2e effect that grants Enfeebled 2 (or Stupefied 2) and ends at the end of its next turn (`duration` in rounds from `untilNextTurnEnds`, `expiry: "turn-end"`, the initiative in `system.start` set to the monster's own just after it is made, because PF2e writes the start itself on creation); `sayToAll` posts the line for everyone as the hurt creature (flag `reason`), and the same goes on the card's note with who decided. A Shield Block that a repentance made needless, and a second champion's Glimpse of Redemption for an ally another champion had already answered for, are given back (`refund`: only the module's own tick for that reaction), with a line on the card.
- **Interposing Earth:** against a Strike it is asked at `beforeAction`'s attack gate with Nimble Dodge; `acRaised` gives the best AC bonus among the target's used rows and the degree is read again as for Nimble Dodge. Against a damaging area with a Reflex save it is asked at `beforeAction` too, before the spell is cast, for every creature the area will catch (`coverage`); a React adds PF2e's own Effect: Cover at standard to the caster (`addEarthCover`, flag `earth`), which `dropEarthCover` takes off when the step ends, when a monster's reaction ends and at every turn change. `castReaction` casts the spell with the entry's own `cast` (slot spent, card posted), waits up to 2 s for an Auto monster's Reactive Strike to that card to be offered, and steps out of `running` while it is made. A miss leaves "…the earth barrier stands for 3 rounds (AC 5, Hardness 2, 5 HP)…" on the card (`barrierWords`). An area the GM resolves from its card (not a basic save) is asked too: the cover stays until the turn moves on and the card tells her to take the 2 off herself.
- **Shield Block against a spell attack** (0.27.0): `hitGate` takes `{ melee, damageTypes, kind }` and is also called before a spell attack's damage; `applyFromCard` gets the block.
- **A monster's Reactive Strike** (0.27.0) asks `beforeAction` before its Strike (Nimble Dodge, Interposing Earth) and goes through the damage gate like any Strike. If the turn moves on or the monster goes down during that question, nothing was rolled and it keeps its reaction. **A creature on the party's side that the module also runs has one reaction:** its own Reactive Strike ticks its box (`markUsed` in `runReaction`), `reactorsFor` reads `reacted` as well as `rx`, and `monsterReactors` skips a creature whose box is ticked.
- **The windows:** `ui/react.mjs` groups rows by `group` into one window with a button for each row, takes a row's `labels` and `lines`, and closes one row's window on a `close` that names it. `ui/turns.mjs` shows the Party reactions block (`partyReactors`, a box each, how it was ticked), a row's own labels, and the open choice with Repent and Refuse (`choiceHtml`, under the turn's card or a monster's reaction). `ui/chip.mjs` draws the player's chip (never on a GM's page): one button per owned character in a running fight, green "reaction ready" or grey "reaction used", starting at the top of the map just left of the sidebar (the top-left corner sat on the scene tabs), a press that doesn't travel 4 px is a click (`setReactionUsed`), one that does moves the chip, and the place is kept in the client setting `chipPlace`.
- **With automatic damage off** (`harmByHand`): the gate is asked after the damage roll, the card says what to apply ("apply it with resistance 5 to all damage") and a line for everyone says the same without the final numbers; the monster's condition is still given by the module. A question cut short there (Hold, the turn moving on) isn't asked again: the card says to finish it by hand.
- **0.29.0:** a monster that has yielded or is a prisoner is offered no reaction (`monsterReactors` skips it).

## Traps
- **One Strike at a time on the runner (0.21.0 review):** `runStrike` matches its cards through the single `pending` waiter, so two Strikes at once (two monsters reacting to one move, a reaction during a turn's action) lose each other's cards. Anything new that calls `runStrike` goes through the queue in `doReaction` or waits as `doIt` does.
- **The history shows a turn's parts only when `angel` is true** (`historyHTML`): an entry marked false is one "played by the GM" line, whatever its parts hold (0.21.0 review: a reaction's Strike vanished).
- **`flags.pf2e.origin` is on an item's card whether it was used or only shown in chat:** tell them apart by the card's markup (`item-card`), never by the origin alone (0.21.0 review).
- **What PF2e's cards carry (seen live 2026-11-09, PF2e 8.7.0):** a real drag is one `moveToken` with method `dragging` and one waypoint a square; a spell attack roll has `item:ranged` and `item:type:spell` in its options; an action card from `game.pf2e.actions` has empty flags (title and tags only in its flavor); drawing or stowing through `changeCarryType` posts no card; PF2e rolls a melee Strike at a target that has already moved out of reach.
- **A test Reactive Strike needs the reactor's reaction back:** it returns only when its own turn begins, and Skip leaves it; a broken shield can't be raised (Valeros's was, from earlier tests).
- **Two hooks for one action arrive before the first write lands** (an Interact card and the item's update): check and mark in memory (`offering`), not only on the flag (0.21.0 review).
- **Test trap: "can't perceive" can't be set with Visioner's hidden alone** (0.20.0 live test): the module's out-of-hiding rule makes a hidden monster observed just before any action but Hide, Sneak or Step, so a Stride is rightly asked of a reactor it was hidden from. Test it with a monster that Sneaks (not asked yet), or a reactor that is blinded.
- **The timer ran for the GM's own rows too (0.20.0):** with the test timer at 5 s, Valeros's Shield Block timed out before the card was read; Rhea then chose no countdown on her rows (0.20.1).
- **A question left on the request** (`react`) after the GM took the turn kept dead buttons on the card and redrew the window every second for the rest of the fight (review finding 3): clear it on every way out, tick only for the current request.
- **Answers keyed per character** gave one answer to two reactions (Reactive Strike and Nimble Dodge on one Swashbuckler): key them per reaction row (review finding 6).
- **A Crawl is prone all along:** "stop the move if it's prone after the reaction" cancelled every Crawl in reach (review finding 1). Stop only if a reaction made it prone.
- **Reading AC again after the roll:** the actor's AC lacks what applied only to that attack (flanking), so start from the attack's own DC and add only what the new circumstance bonus beats (review finding 5).
- **An "all-damage" resistance applies to each damage type of a roll** (PF2e 8.7.0, seen live 2026-11-11: 8 slashing and 6 fire against resistance 5 took 4), and two of them on one creature count as the higher only: a flat reduction is not a resistance. Take a flat amount off with `roll.alter(multiplier, -n)`, which keeps the types and takes it off the first instance ("8 - 2 slashing + 6 fire").
- **A damage formula with two types needs braces** (`{8[slashing],6[fire]}`): `8[slashing]+6[fire]` evaluates as one untyped instance, and a probe built on it proves nothing about types.
- **PF2e writes an effect's `system.start` itself when it is made** (the initiative came back null with no fight): set the initiative in `system.start` with an update just after creating it.
- **`actor.applyDamage` needs a token** (a world actor with none on a scene throws inside PF2e): probes need a token document, which can be on a scene nobody is viewing.
- **`TokenAura#containsToken` answers false on a scene that isn't drawn on the page** (both tokens side by side, 2026-11-11): use it only when both tokens have a canvas object, and measure otherwise.
- **A question that was asked once must never be worked out again** (0.27.0 live test): after a reload of the runner's page in the middle of a repent-or-refuse, the champion who had reacted had her box ticked, so a fresh `reactorsFor` left her row out, the list was empty, `askReactions` was never reached and her answer was lost (the full damage was applied and the card said she was "not asked"). `askReactions` now returns a stored gate before it looks at the list, the damage gate calls it whenever that gate was asked before (`askedBefore`), and no "not asked" note is written for a gate that was.
- **`hitGate` and the damage gate run inside a stage's `pending` block; the reactions asked before an action don't.** Anything that must let another roller in (a reaction spell's cast drawing a monster's Reactive Strike) belongs in `beforeAction`.
- **The module's own cover effect must not carry the `takeCover` flag:** that flag marks the creature's own Take Cover, which other code ends. Interposing Earth's copy of Effect: Cover carries `earth`.
- **Never press `doIt` on the Bridge's own page while a reaction question may come** (the 0.28.0 close-out pass, 2026-11-11): the call waits inside for the answer, so the script times out and the turn hangs half done. Press it from a hidden AI session (`session start gm`) or from its card.
- **Cover that is only in the attack's DC (0.29.1, bug B-3, found by the 2026-11-12 close-out):** Visioner's cover is an AC modifier predicated on the attacker (`origin:signature:…`), so outside the roll it reads as disabled and `circNow` counted 0, while the attack's DC already held it: Nimble Dodge's +2 went on top of lesser cover's +1 (DC 12 read as 14, a hit turned into a miss). Circumstance bonuses don't stack, so the bonus already in the DC is now also read from the card's roll options (`target:cover-bonus:<n>`, `coverInDc`) and a reaction adds only what it beats (`acAdds`); Interposing Earth (through `acRaised`) and Reactive Shield read it the same way.
- **Visioner's manual cover doesn't reach a Strike's DC** (PF2e 8.7.0, seen 2026-11-12): `setCover` shows in the packet and in the roll options (`target:cover-level:standard`) but its AC modifier's predicate `origin:signature:<attacker>` isn't among a Strike's options, so the DC leaves it out, on the module's Strike and a plain GM Strike alike. Wall and creature auto-cover do reach the DC. To test cover in the DC, stand a creature between (lesser) or use a wall.
- **Test tip:** one Nimble Dodge question per Strike: untick the reactor's box between Strikes (the API's `partyReactions` `set`, with false) or let its own turn pass; a second Strike in the same round is (rightly) not asked.

## Open items
- A Fly, Swim or Burrow passing through someone's reach later on its route isn't asked (only where it starts); burrowing down in its own square isn't asked.
- A Sneak that starts observed is a move action too; it isn't asked yet (Sneak usually starts hidden, when no one can react).
- Nimble Dodge against a spell attack (not asked: its degree isn't read again). Since 0.27.0 Shield Block is asked on a spell attack's hit and the damage gate covers areas. Before 0.27.0: reactions with a spell attack's hit (Shield Block against a spell attack's physical damage) and after an area's saves aren't asked.
- **Buttons for players (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** "for cases where you are unsure, we can later build a button for the players to click to do these actions" (a Step, drawing a weapon, standing up), so those become sure. A later release.
- Monsters' reactions other than Reactive Strike aren't run (listed on the card as the GM's to play), and that includes a variant under its own name or slug; one that keeps the plain slug is run as the plain one.
- A monster's Reactive Strike (it asks Nimble Dodge and Interposing Earth of its target since 0.27.0) doesn't bring a Visioner-hidden monster out of hiding and doesn't end its Take Cover; a question left open on it doesn't turn the card amber.
- After a Skip the same action can offer again: each key press of a keyboard move, a rerolled ranged attack. (A spell's attack roll within a minute of its manipulate cast card doesn't: `castCards`.)
- Prone put on with no Drop Prone card (the condition clicked on) offers nothing: it is usually a Trip or a fall, not the creature's own move action.
- While Angel runs a party member's whole turn on Auto, a monster's reaction to it waits for that turn's actions and can lapse when the turn ends.
- A player's drag isn't stopped mid-move: the Strike comes once the move shows, from the square left. A critical hit's disruption of a manipulate action is a note; the module undoes nothing.
- Reach is tested along straight runs between the move's waypoints; a path Foundry bent between two waypoints is read as straight.
- A reaction from a creature the GM changed to "react twice" (Quick Reflexes and the like): the module asks once per gate; the GM plays any second one.
- **A full pass on every way a character can use a reaction (Rhea, 2026-11-11):** "we will need to do a full pass on every way a char can use their reaction and build a way to track every one of them. That's for a future pass." This release tracks what it can see and gives the click for the rest. The pass includes reactions no card shows, extra reactions for a set purpose (a second one a turn), and **putting Interposing Earth's surviving barrier on the map** (drawn on the square edge, seen and attacked by monsters, its 3 rounds counted, removed): "Make sure we don't forget it."
- A monster's Reactive Strike to Interposing Earth's cast is made at once only when that monster is on Auto; an offer waiting for the GM's Do it is made when the turn's action has finished, and lapses when the turn moves on.
- Interposing Earth's flat reduction and a resistance to all damage the caster already has: the 2 comes off the roll before PF2e applies resistances, which is the rule's order. A champion's resistance and a resistance to all damage the ally already has: PF2e counts the higher only, as for any two resistances of one kind.
- Glimpse of Redemption is asked for damage the module applies. Persistent damage, damage the GM applies from a card with automatic damage on, and anything a monster does outside the module are hers to rule.
- A reaction spell is cast at the lowest rank with a slot left: the player can't choose a higher rank from the window.
- A line of effect between the champion and the creatures in its aura is PF2e's test where the map is drawn; the fallback by distance doesn't look at walls.
- The thinker's packet for a choice is the one its turn began with (or its latest turn's, for a Reactive Strike): what changed since isn't in it.
- An ability's damage (a critical Trip's 1d6) asks Glimpse of Redemption but no Shield Block.
- A repent-or-refuse on a monster's Reactive Strike never turns its card amber (the stall check reads the turn in hand only), like any question left open on a monster's reaction.
- `castReaction` waits 2 s for an Auto monster's Reactive Strike to the cast to be offered; one offered later is made when the turn's action has finished.
- A reaction spell answered in the instant between its question closing and its cast, with the runner's page reloaded in that instant, is not cast (its +2 and its 2 off still apply): spend the slot by hand.
