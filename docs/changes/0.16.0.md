## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- Rhea's order (from 0.15.0's record and the plan): burrowing next, "soil, not rock; unseen while below", and test the speeds in a dungeon setting with tight corridors and rooms.
- Which ground on a Survey Sheet encounter map counts as rock a monster can't burrow through? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Crag and solid features" (Angel's recommendation): rock is crag ground, outcrops, big boulders, standing stones, ruin walls and caves; everything else is soil, snow, mud and scree included. Deep water is out either way.
- What about scenes the module can't read (a hand-built dungeon, an imported battle map)? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Let Angel make a judgment call", then, asked whether that meant Angel picks a rule now or judges each map in play: "Angel judges each map in play". Built as: on such a scene, a monster with a burrow Speed is asked once to look at a picture of the map with numbered squares and name its soil areas; the answer becomes GM-only soil regions she can edit or delete; anywhere unmarked is rock.
- Angel's reading of the rules (Player Core Burrow, Actions ID 2310; burrow Speed, Rules ID 2348; tremorsense, Rules ID 2413; line of effect, Rules ID 2382), reasons in `docs/features/moves.md` Decisions: one depth just below the surface; going down or coming up costs 5 ft of the Burrow; no tunnel left; underground there is no line of effect, so it can't attack, cast or be attacked and must come up first; it is hidden from everyone (GM-hidden token) and senses only by tremorsense; breath-holding waits for the underwater release, as Rhea chose for swimming.

## Review
<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
An independent agent read the diff, the record and the docs, ran the unit tests and two probe scripts (no live testing); 10 findings, each checked against the code:
1. A reload after a surfacing Burrow's move and before the token was shown again left it GM-hidden at the surface (`nextMove` answers "arrived", `finish` didn't show it): **true**; fixed, the `arrived` path shows it.
2. A move that throws left a burrower hidden at the surface (`setBelow(true)` ran first): **true**; fixed, the move is in a `finally` that leaves it hidden only if it is below.
3. Below ground without tremorsense it perceives no one, so a plan of Burrow up then Strike was always refused, and Burrow up alone ended the turn with actions unused; with tremorsense the look before the Strike spent a re-plan: **true** (seen in the unit test too); fixed, coming up from below must be the plan's last step and the runner asks again at the surface uncounted (`surfaced`), also when it was stopped short; prompt and unit tests changed.
4. A Delay after a burrowed ally was refused though the packet listed it (`turnOrder` filtered every hidden token): **true**; fixed with `knownAllies`, shared by the packet and Delay. A spell at a burrowed ally is still refused with "doesn't know of" rather than "no line of effect": kept, noted in Open items.
5. An ally the GM had hidden before it burrowed became visible to the thinker: **true**; fixed, `knownAllies` leaves it out (`wasHidden`).
6. Stopping one square short could cut a Large burrower two or more squares short (its footprints overlap along the route): **true**; fixed, only the route's last square is dropped.
7. A Burrow up then a Stride in one plan, priced with the burrow history: **moot** after fix 3 (coming up from below ends the plan); a dip from the surface then a Stride is still allowed and its pricing is a live check (mv-22).
8. Nothing tidies burrowed tokens at End Encounter (climbing has the same gap): **true**; not changed (a monster still below at the end is arguably still there), noted in Open items for Rhea.
9. The ground picture could be drawn on a non-runner GM's page through `perceived()`: **true**; fixed, drawn only on the runner's page. The scene's name going to the thinker: kept (the thinker is Angel, the names are Rhea's own map names).
10. A plan's ground judgment stays even if that plan is refused: **true**, by design (asked once); said in the doc.
Tests the reviewer flagged as weak: the "nothing allowed below" name (renamed), the stubbed "come up on a creature it knows of" (mover's own check is a live check, mv-20), the prompt checks being substrings (kept: they guard the words, not behaviour).
