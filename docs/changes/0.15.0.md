## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- Rhea's order (from 0.14.0's record): climbing next (trees; a climb Speed climbs automatically, others Climb with Athletics, two free hands, off-guard, 5 ft a time).
- Where a climbing monster can go, given the maps know each tree's square and trunk but not its height? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Up/down, height by size" (over a flat 20 ft and over moving through the branches). The height formula is Angel's guess (`treeTop`).
- Hands: Climb needs both hands free; what should that mean for monsters? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "By the rule" (Angel's recommendation): it can't Climb holding a weapon or shield until it Releases it (free, dropped on its square); up the tree it may attack with what's in hand; to Climb again its hands must be free.
- Angel's reading of the rules, with reasons in `docs/features/moves.md` Decisions: DC 15 for every tree (typical tree, trained); a critical failure falls; off-guard while up a tree without a climb Speed; no ground moves up a tree and no fall there; no Strike with a weapon on the ground; falling damage only above 5 ft (fixes 0.13.0's flier fall).
