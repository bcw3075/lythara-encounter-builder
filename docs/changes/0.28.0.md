## Review
<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
- An independent reviewer that hadn't seen the design talk read the code diff, `docs/features/orders.md`, `docs/features/bands.md` and `scripts/orders.mjs`, and 12 further excerpts (its ceiling), on the build before the first test build. 16 findings; each was checked against the code by Angel before anything was changed.
- 1 (high) verified true, fixed: an order from the box was stored with its time stamp under `at`, the key of the order's square, so every Hold here (and a job order with a square) lost its square and was refused by the runner. The stamp is `ts`; the shape is now a pure pair (`toPending`, `fromPending`) with a unit test.
- 2 verified true, fixed: her own words were refused for a creature with no saved label yet (the name gate read the saved labels, not the would-be ones, and allies got none). `notePeople` takes a label map; `labelsFor` labels the leader and its allies too.
- 3 verified true, fixed: the leader was never told its own orders, so a later packet couldn't tell it what it last ordered. The leader keeps its own order in its standing orders; the prompt says so.
- 4 verified true, fixed: after Fights harder the fallen leader's last order to the whole band still ranked above the new objective. The new objective now takes that order's place for every member.
- 5 verified true, fixed: the box's draft was dropped before the runner had accepted the order, and nothing checked the runner was there. The draft stays until the order shows as given; `giveOrder` refuses with the runner's problem in words.
- 6 verified true, kept as it is, by Rhea's standing decision of 2026-11-11 (what a player's browser merely holds is no reason to change a design): the words, the order in names and the counts are in the public message's flag, off the screen for players. Written in the feature doc under what Angel settled.
- 7 verified true, fixed: a leader that got back up (when nobody had taken over) was still told "Its leader is down." That line is now sent only while another leads.
- 8 verified true, fixed: two turn changes close together could both act on one fall. `settleBands` has a lock for each fight.
- 9 verified true, fixed: the GM's view of the chat line put two numbers into the page unescaped and trusted any message with the flag. Numbers are read as numbers, and only a GM's own message counts.
- 10 verified true, fixed: an old refusal showed on later rounds' boxes. A refusal carries its round.
- 11 verified true, fixed: the id of a kind the build didn't place came from its place on a list, so it could pass to another kind when a token left the map. It comes from the kind's own key now.
- 12 verified true, fixed: no box after Play it myself on a leader ticked for Angel. The box shows once she has taken the turn.
- 13 verified true, fixed: before the module had acted on a fall, a packet could name another successor than the named second; and when the one who took over left the map nobody was found again. The same rule is used in both places, and `settleBands` looks again when the heir's token is gone.
- 14 verified true, fixed: a blow from the leader's own side could be named as the one to avenge. Only an enemy's label counts.
- 15 verified true, nothing to fix today: a fight-layer line for one monster hides this fight's lines for its kind (`mindLayers`); nothing writes a kind's lines on a fight yet. Noted in the feature doc's open items.
- 16, the smaller points: a new leader couldn't order in the round the old one already had (fixed: the band's round is cleared when one takes over); a click outside the map for Hold here gave a negative square (fixed: refused in words); the box's target wasn't checked again on the runner (fixed); a reload of the runner between the order's write and its chat line leaves the order given with no chat line (true, kept: open item); the preview of her words refreshes only when focus leaves the box (true, kept: open item); the Second select offers Angel's guessed leader (true, harmless: skipped at the fall); Monster Minds doesn't show who took over (true, kept: open item); a square in a thinker's order can stand where an enemy the hearers haven't perceived is (true, by design: a place may be told, and being told where a creature is stays Point Out).
- Checked by the reviewer and found fine: flag merges (`whole()` everywhere it is needed), one update for an order, the one-a-round rule over re-plans, second plans, Delay, the box and two bands, every place the `order` step kind touches, nothing of the sentence said aloud or of a player's character in a packet, the fall acted on once and written where minds and bands read it, fights with no band behaving as before.
- Not checked by the reviewer: the unit tests, the live server, the other docs, `noteDeed` and the stall words for an `order` step (read by Angel: neither names the step, so nothing is written or said for it).
