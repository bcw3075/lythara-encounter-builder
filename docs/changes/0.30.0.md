## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
Rhea's answers were all given before this chat (2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12; the plan's q-15 to q-19, the design doc's R3-6, R3-8 and R3-15 to R3-17) and are now in the feature docs' Decisions:

- q-16, 2026-11-12: the turncoat by the simple rule (`sides.md`, decision 1).
- q-17, 2026-11-12: an animal is a creature with PF2e's `animal` trait only (`command-animal.md`, decision 2).
- q-18, 2026-11-12: the four sample pictures, "They look good" (`command-animal.md` 3, `point-out.md` 1, `sides.md` 2).
- q-19, 2026-11-12: a Speak / Yield box for a monster she plays, wanted as its own small release right after tier 3d (`speech.md`, Open items).

Settled by Angel in this build, each listed in its feature doc as hers to change: the instruction as data (attack, go, stay); the command told to the thinker and not enforced by the plan check; one command an animal; a failed command's turn passed whether or not the animal is ticked; "understands" as hearing plus a shared language; no card for the Perception roll; a wrong square one or two squares off; a Strike at a wrong square as a public miss into the empty square that ends the belief; any move ending the beliefs about a creature; `alliesLost` told to the thinker; a side as PF2e's `alliance`; a turncoat keeping its mind; its packet carrying the orders it last heard and not the band's plan as written.

Bugs fixed: **B-4** (an untick that lands while a handed-in plan is being checked was lost; `judgeNow` reads the tick again after its save) and **B-5** (an ability step aimed at an ally's label was refused as unknown).
