6. true, fixed: a monster's repent or refuse shown under its reaction card was only caught by the window's net, so it always read "its"; it is turned with the reactor's forms.
7. true, fixed: the Remembers fold-out's side tally read "Its side, counting itself".
8. true, fixed: "It is carrying out a step" never changed (`changeProblem`'s callers passed no forms).
9. true, fixed: picking "other…" and saving with fewer than two words stored a line "blank on purpose", hiding the kind's own pronoun; the draft is now left alone until her words read as a pronoun.
10. true, fixed: an enemy built on a character sheet got pills that did nothing (it is written by its sheet); every character-type row now shows the sheet's row.
11. judged, left: the chat net turns the exact marker shapes in any chat message, not only this module's. Only `{they}`-style words and `{a|b}` pairs are touched; restricting it to the module's own cards would miss a card another module posts for a step. Left, and listed under Open items.

15. true, fixed: the fallback reason "it is no longer one of its allies" is written with the animal's and the handler's forms.
16. not certain, settled: a pronoun look-up that threw would stop the Monster Turns or History window drawing; both now catch it and read "it".
