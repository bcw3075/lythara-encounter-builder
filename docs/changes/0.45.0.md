- An independent agent read the diff (-U10), this record and the touched feature docs, with the functions the diff calls into cut into its file; 31 findings, each checked against the code:
- 1 true: `partyLog.open` was written by merging, so one member's consumables leaked into the next turn's snapshot. Now written whole (`==open`).
- 2 true: a spell's damage roll was read as a second casting (`originType` spell). The damage branch comes first; `originType` spell counts only on a card with no kind.
- 3 true: `get` didn't take a page's own `party:` key. It does.
- 4 true for a token deleted mid-turn (its turn was lost): `open` keeps the actor id and name, and the turn is built from its cards. False for a deleted current combatant: `combatTurnChange` already fires (the traps doc).
- 5 true: End Encounter could ask about a record that saves nothing. `hasPartyRecord` uses the same test as saving.
- 6 true but rare: two pages writing one party page could overwrite with an older copy. The cache is dropped before each write; the rest is an open item in the feature doc.
- 7 true: a token line the module guessed (`by: "module"`) carried over as hers. Now as Angel's.
- 8 false: `isRunner` is the same test as fight.mjs's.
- 9 true: a missed `combatTurnChange` lost a turn change. `updateCombat` with a turn or round in it is heard too (it also gives the first turn, replacing a timer on `combatStart`); the guard skips the same change twice.
- 10 true: a reaction on a monster's turn was filed under the wrong round. A monster's turn opens with its round and time.
- 11 true: at save, the other party members' cards on the last turn were missed. They are found by time too.
- 12 true: cards were kept by actor, so two tokens of one actor mixed and another fight's cards were taken. Now by token, only the fight's own.
- 13 true: damage after a Strike that hit and a second that missed joined the miss. It joins the earliest of the same item that hit, else the latest.
- 14 true: a flexible caster's slots weren't counted. They are; wand and staff charges are an open item.
- 15 true: 200 fights was too few for a long campaign, and a Relink lost the fights saved under the old id. 500, and `formerIds`.
- 16 true: Relink offered actors that already have a page (the press then failed). They're left out.
- 17 true: a page made but empty silenced the token's old lines. A page is played by only once it has a line.
- 18 true: a page whose character left the party didn't open from Monster Minds. It does.
- 19 true: "Show the monster peoples" fell through to the first party page when no peoples exist. It has a value of its own.
- 20 to 25 false: the name gate's people include every character and the creature itself; the packet carries only the gated mind; `held` keeps keys only; every `libraryEntries` caller filters by type.
- 26 true: a turn begun while another page was the runner wasn't marked part-seen. `open` names the runner; another one marks the turn `partial`.
- 27 true: fights given as records became "[object Object]". Their combat ids are taken.
- 28 true (nit): a wrong comment on `cleanSuggestion`. Fixed.
- 29 true: a caller's suggestion id could clash. Ids are always the module's own and unique on the page.
- 30 true: Angel's `set` could change a line with no Accept, against 9-3. `set` writes only her words; Angel goes through `suggest`.
- 31 true: an error in the card hook could stop the others. Caught.
