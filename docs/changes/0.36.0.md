An independent agent read the diff, the touched functions and the reactions doc's new lines (test build 1's code). Each finding checked against the code:
- 1. A missing spell DC made every counteract check a critical success (`degreeAgainst` with a null DC): verified true. Fixed: no Counterspell or Shadow Siphon row without a finite DC (`castFacts`).
- 2. The counters ran before the down, held and disrupted checks: verified true. Fixed: `castAnswers` runs after them in `beforeAction`.
- 3. Recognize Spell's +1 (and Interposing Earth's cover) stayed when the spell was countered (`dropEarthCover` only after a step's work): verified true. Fixed: dropped in the countered branch of `runTurn`.
- 4. A reload in the middle of a counteract, then Do it, could cast a spell the chat shows countered: verified true. Fixed by the note: "If it was countered, play the rest of the turn yourself; if not, Do it casts the spell." (Open item.)
- 5. The spell's name reached players in other rows' words ("uses a manipulate action ... (casting Fear)", an area's "Fear is about to catch you"): verified true (the first since 0.20.0). Fixed: players' words say "casting a spell" / "spell", her card keeps the name (`gmKind`).
- 6. The socket carries the GM's words and the cast to every browser: true but not changed, by Rhea's standing rule (2026-11-11: her players don't use the developer console; don't design around it).
- 7. A flexible caster was offered Recognize Spell for a spell in its collection, and told "repertoire": verified true. Fixed in `castFacts` and `castWords`.
- 8. Nullify could leave its caster at 0 HP with no Dying: verified true. Fixed by a note to her (HP loss isn't damage, so the module doesn't apply Dying).
- 9. Shadow Siphon's halving wasn't said when the GM applies the damage: verified true. Fixed: a note on her card.
- Smaller: two Counterspells both spending and rolling: fixed (the second is "wasn't needed", nothing spent). Spell Riposte against a several-target spell: false here (a spell step names one target; an area never asks it). A cantrip's counteract rank: read in the code, not seen live: the step's rank for a cantrip is PF2e's own `spell.rank` (`spellBook`), which PF2e heightens with the caster's level; the only test cantrip caster is 1st level, where both read 1.
