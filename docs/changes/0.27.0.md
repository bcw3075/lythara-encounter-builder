Rhea's decisions PR-1 to PR-11 and the six readings she accepted (the design pass of 2026-11-11) are what this builds; they moved from the private plan into `docs/features/reactions.md` under Decisions, with her words and reasons. Her go for the build: "We are good to go now" (2026-11-11).
- Settled by Angel in this build, told to Rhea, hers to change (also in `docs/features/reactions.md`):
  - **Her Repent and Refuse buttons are on the card for every monster**, Angel's too, so she can answer when the thinker doesn't; whoever answers first stands.
  - **A monster's Reactive Strike has no packet of its own**, so the thinker's choice for it goes out with the packet from that monster's latest turn; a monster that hasn't had a turn yet is hers to answer.
  - **A reaction spell is cast at the lowest rank with a slot left.**
  - **A champion's reaction and Interposing Earth on one hit both apply:** the resistance through PF2e, the 2 off the roll.
  - **With automatic damage off** the question is asked after the damage roll and her card says what to apply.
  - **A reaction's card posted from a sheet or PF2e HUD ticks the box**; it can't be told from an ability only shown in chat, which is why a click clears it.
  - **The thinker's choice is a new kind of event (`choice`)** answered through the same door as a plan (`{ choice, reason }`), so the bridge needed no change; the prompt is version 27.
  - **Shield Block is asked on a spell attack's hit** (physical damage), and a monster's Reactive Strike now asks Nimble Dodge and Interposing Earth of its target: two open items closed on the way.

## Review
<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
An independent agent read the diff, this record, the reactions and brain feature docs and the code around each suspicion (2026-11-11; about 237,000 tokens, more than the 120,000 it was given as a ceiling). It reported 15 findings; each was checked against the code. 14 were real and are fixed or written down, 1 is a design point taken as it suggested.
1. **True, fixed.** `castReaction`'s wait for an Auto monster's reaction counted the request that was asking: a monster's own Reactive Strike answered with Interposing Earth would have waited a minute on itself. It now leaves itself out.
2. **True, fixed.** An area the GM resolves from its card lost Interposing Earth's cover before anyone rolled, and its 2 off was never mentioned. The cover now stays until the turn moves on (the `gm` result keeps it) and the card tells her to take the 2 off.
3. **True, fixed as far as it can be.** With automatic damage off, a Hold or a turn moving on during the question was dropped. `harmByHand` now hands the status back (the four damage stages act on it) and the card says the question wasn't finished; it isn't asked again, since the damage is already rolled (written in the feature doc).
4. **True, fixed.** Weight of Guilt's question had one key a step, so a second champion in one area would have inherited the first one's pick. The key now names the ally.
5. **True, fixed.** A repent-or-refuse left open by a reload of the runner was written again with a new number and sent to the thinker a second time. The open question is now recognised by its step and ally and waited on again; an answer given meanwhile is taken.
6. **True, fixed.** A thinker's late answer could land on the next question on the same request. The event now tells the thinker to pass its number back, and an answer naming another question is refused.
7. **True, fixed.** With automatic damage off nothing was said in chat for everyone. `afterHarm` now posts the line there too. (Shield Block alone has PF2e's own damage card: to see in the live test.)
8. **True, fixed.** Answers given before a reload were kept but a reaction spell among them was never cast, and its box might not be ticked. The reload path now ticks and casts.
9. **True, fixed.** A step held and started again added Interposing Earth's cover effect a second time. It is added once.
10. **True, fixed.** A monster's Reactive Strike that ended during the question before its roll kept its reaction marked spent and could leave the cover on. Both are cleared.
11. **True, fixed.** The surviving barrier's words were fixed at Hardness 2 and 5 HP whatever the rank. `barrierWords` follows the rank (8 and 20 from 4th).
12. **True, fixed.** A creature on the party's side that the module also runs had two reaction counts that didn't read each other. Its Reactive Strike now ticks its box, the party's questions read the monsters' mark, and the monsters' offers read the box.
13. **A design point, taken.** Shield Block is asked before the damage roll and Glimpse of Redemption after, so a block followed by a repentance was a reaction spent on nothing; the same for a second champion answering for an ally already answered for. Both are now given back, with a line on the card.
14. **True, written down.** A repent-or-refuse on a monster's Reactive Strike never turns its card amber (the stall check reads the turn in hand only): in the feature doc's Open items with the question it resembles.
15. **Small ones, true:** the 2 s wait for an Auto reaction to a cast (written in Open items); the card's "Reactions:" note called a champion's unpicked ally rows "skipped" (left out now); the player's chip jumped back to its saved place on any fight update in the middle of a drag (a redraw now keeps it where it is); an empty note could be added (not any more).
**Found by the live test, after the review (2026-11-11), each fixed and seen again:**
- **Build 2:** the player's chip started on top of the scene tabs: it now starts at the top of the map just left of the sidebar. The thinker's choice event carried the whole packet again (about 3,000 tokens for a caster): it is cut down to what the choice rests on (`choicePacket`, unit-tested). Three allies' buttons side by side wrapped inside their buttons: a group's buttons are one under the other.
- **Build 3:** a reload of the runner's page while a repent-or-refuse waited lost the champion's reaction altogether (her box was ticked, so a fresh list no longer held her row, the stored question was never reached, the full damage was applied and the card said she was "not asked"). A stored question is now returned before the list is looked at, for every gate (the same hole was there for a Shield Block answered before a reload). Run again on build 3: one question, one event, the damage once with its resistance.
- The fixes in builds 2 and 3 were small and were not sent back to an independent reviewer: the unit tests, the gate and the live checks that touch them (r-22, r-35 and the regression Strikes after them) ran again.
