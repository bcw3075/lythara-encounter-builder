## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- Rhea's order and rule (from 0.13.0's record): swimming next, "Movement could be movement. Take the land type into account and the type of movement the monster has."
- Should monsters also go under the water this release (dive, sink, hold breath, hidden below), or swim at the surface only? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Surface only for now" (Angel's recommendation). A monster that would sink or drift at the end of its turn gets a note on the GM's card (Player Core's Swim leaves sink or current to the GM); diving and breath are a later release.
- Swim's details are Angel's reading of the rules (Player Core Swim, Actions ID 2381; swim Speed, Rules ID 2346), with reasons in `docs/features/moves.md` Decisions: deep water only, in from or out onto the bank; a swim Speed needs no check; without one, calm water (pond, lake) is an automatic critical success (15 ft) and flowing water (river) an Athletics check at DC 15 (trained task) for 10 ft; no ground moves in deep water; treading water; the sink note for anyone, swimmers included, without a successful Swim that turn.

## Review
<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
Independent reviewer (an agent that hadn't seen the work), on the first test build's diff and this record: 10 findings, each checked against the code by Angel; all fixed in the second test build, with unit tests.
1. verified true (reproduced with `findRoute`): a Large monster on dry land could never swim in (every passed square needed its whole footprint in deep water), and could only climb half out. Fixed: a place counts as "dry" only when none of its footprint is deep water; `checkPlan` uses `waterIn` (any deep water under the footprint) for "something to swim in" and flowing, `waterAt` (all of it) for swimming, the packet, the ground-move refusal and the sink note.
2. verified true (read): a reload between PF2e's Swim card and its record would roll again; nothing was saved before the roll, and a missing result returned "ask" with nothing saved. Fixed: `swimStarted` before the roll, a second pass asks the GM; a missing result adds a note.
3. verified true (read): a flier above deep water was refused a Stride with the water words, and its packet said `swimming`. Fixed: the air check comes first and the water refusal only on the ground; the packet's `swimming` only at height 0.
4. verified true (read): a rolled success not yet moved read "no progress" in the done words and the History line. Fixed: by degree.
5. verified true (read against Survey Sheet's river width): deep water more than 5 squares from a wide river's line counted as calm. Fixed: flowing is a connected patch of deep water a creek or river line runs through or along, outside ponds and lakes.
6. verified true (read): a Fly landing in deep water at height 0 got the sink note, though its last action was entering the water. Fixed: no note when the last step begun was a Fly.
7. verified true (read): the prompt and moves.md said a failed Swim always asks again; it does only when steps follow. Reworded both.
8. verified true (read): the failed-Swim note replaced an earlier note on the card. Fixed with `joinNote`.
9. verified true (read): "far beyond its swimming distance of 20 ft" named the search budget, not the real limit. Fixed: "farther than one Swim can go."
10. verified true: the unit tests' route helper accepted "far" with no route (the old behaviour) and the land-to-land test allowed either refusal. Fixed, and tests added for a Large monster in and out, a half-wet footprint, a flier above water, the ground letters, the History lines, the fly landing, a wide river and the unknown-distance words (1204 pass).
The reviewer found unchanged and right: Stride, Step, Crawl, Fly, flee and the fall; a reload after the roll was saved; a failed middle or last Swim; `shouldSink`'s guards; no information leaks; the PF2e Swim call's shape; the swim distances; the map read's format. Not checked by it: the live server, Foundry's swim action internals, the zip, the UI beyond the done words.
