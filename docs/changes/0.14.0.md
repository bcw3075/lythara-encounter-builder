## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- Rhea's order and rule (from 0.13.0's record): swimming next, "Movement could be movement. Take the land type into account and the type of movement the monster has."
- Should monsters also go under the water this release (dive, sink, hold breath, hidden below), or swim at the surface only? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Surface only for now" (Angel's recommendation). A monster that would sink or drift at the end of its turn gets a note on the GM's card (Player Core's Swim leaves sink or current to the GM); diving and breath are a later release.
- Swim's details are Angel's reading of the rules (Player Core Swim, Actions ID 2381; swim Speed, Rules ID 2346), with reasons in `docs/features/moves.md` Decisions: deep water only, in from or out onto the bank; a swim Speed needs no check; without one, calm water (pond, lake) is an automatic critical success (15 ft) and flowing water (river) an Athletics check at DC 15 (trained task) for 10 ft; no ground moves in deep water; treading water; the sink note for anyone, swimmers included, without a successful Swim that turn.
