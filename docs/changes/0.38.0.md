<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- **How the rows were checked (Rhea, 2026-11-16, standing for the pass: "Compendium first (Recommended)"):** the fourteen texts of Moments D and F were read from `pf2e.feats-srd` and `pf2e.spells-srd` in PF2 Test on 2026-11-17 in one script (traits, level, rarity, traditions, range, target, defense, frequency, source and text); all were found there, none is legacy. Archives of Nethys was read once, for Counter Element (it looked odd for the party): its prerequisite is "exactly one kinetic element" (Rage of Elements pg. 22), which a two-gate kineticist can't meet.
- **Corrections to the design (plan/reactions-pass-design.md):** Counter Element is not built (above; the party's kineticist has two gates). Martyr's Intervention is divine, occult and primal (the design said divine) and needs the caster not wounded; Breath of Life's healing is 5d8 (+1d8 per 2 ranks); Love's Sacrifice is arcane and divine; Word of Revision is rare (arcane, occult); Inspiring Resilience is uncommon and gives its bonus to allies only (not the champion), +2 against an undead's effect; Skeptic's Defense is once a day and stupefies the source; Prophet's Luck is rare and its +1 or -1 after the result lasts 10 minutes; Keep Up Appearances and Know Oneself are uncommon human feats with an access line (Taldan, Vudrani) and Know Oneself is once a day; Champion's Sacrifice is a champion focus spell already asked on a hit since 0.37.0, and now on a failed save too.
- **The line after a reaction records the full damage (Rhea, 2026-11-17, in the 0.37.1 chat: "You should be recording the full damage"):** shown that at 0 HP the line read "Test Hero takes 8 (the hit was 16; Blazing Spirit…)" beside PF2e's "takes 15 damage", she chose the full damage dealt after the reaction. Built: around each application the runner watches the creature's own HP update for PF2e's `damageTaken` (its `calculateHealthDelta` total, which goes past 0 HP and is what its card shows: read in PF2e's `actor/base.ts`, `applyDamage`), and the lines after Glimpse of Redemption, Interposing Earth, Shield Block and the Moment B and C rows give that (`fullDamage`, `dealing`); with no update from PF2e (nothing taken, or the creature already at 0) it falls back to the HP lost as before. The figure is also kept on the step's record (`dealt`).

- Independent review (a general-purpose agent given the diff, this record and reactions.md, 2026-11-17; ~196,000 tokens: it went past the 120,000 ceiling it was given). Ten findings, each checked against the code:
- 1 verified true (possible, Workbench's hook order not read): another module's `preUpdateActor` hook runs before the module's one, which is added only during the application, so it could see the 0. Fixed: after a guard fired, `keepUp` takes off Dying, Unconscious and the defeated mark if anything put them on, with the wounded value kept.
- 2 verified true: for a spell she resolves from its card, the step's effects (Divine Grace, Inspiring Resilience, Prophet's Luck) were taken off when the step returned "gm", before the save. Fixed: they last the turn then (`end` "turn"), the step otherwise.
- 3 verified true: with automatic damage off, a stopping row skipped her instructions for the other rows. Fixed: `harmByHand` gives them, then returns "stopped".
- 4 verified true: `saveAfter` had no started mark (a reload could roll Schadenfreude's save again) and its stored answer always said "don't stop". Fixed: a started mark (a reload notes it and stops for her) and the stored stop.
- 5 verified true: (a) a spell countered by Counterspell left the save-before rows used; fixed: given back with a note (a slot already spent is hers to give back); (b) a Martyr's Intervention kept on a blow the monster repented of was never given back; fixed: the guard is armed always, and `afterDrop` gives it back.
- 6 verified true: a reload between the step's applied record and `afterDrop` would skip the rest of Moment D. Fixed: `afterDrop` runs before the record (its own `dropDone` mark first).
- 7 verified true: `castReaction` read `x.spell.entryId` before its own check. Fixed there, and `afterDrop` notes a missing spell instead of casting.
- 8 verified true (possible): PF2e raises Wounded when Dying goes, so the fix-up could double it. Fixed in `keepUp` (wounded back to what it was).
- 9 verified true: Inspiring Resilience was asked for a Fortitude or Reflex save. Fixed: a Will save only (`will`, `saveStat`, a unit check). The other save rows are the reactor's own (target), so one row per reactor is right.
- 10 verified true: the area path didn't keep `dealt` (fixed: kept per creature); the "never see it drop" line now names the fix-up (reactions.md); the Intelligence check's `?? -5` did nothing (fixed: an unknown Intelligence counts).
- Its first point (an unevaluated roll in `afterDrop`) was already fixed before it read the tree.
