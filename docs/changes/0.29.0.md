## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- 2026-11-11, build order (3c or release 4 next): Rhea: tier 3c first.
- 2026-11-11, who may talk to the party (any monster, leaders only, or a setting): Rhea: "Any monster" (only creatures that know a language; one sentence a round each).
- 2026-11-11, a monster the GM has hidden speaking (no line, "a voice shouts something", or the line as usual): Rhea: "A voice shouts": players see the speaker as "A voice" and "A voice shouts something in <language>." or the words if they know it; the GM always sees who.
- 2026-11-11, samples shown before any code (the chat line as a player who knows the language, one who doesn't, a hidden monster as "A voice" with the GM's view; a yield's gesture line, readable in any language; a yielded row with Accepted and Fight on; a Prisoners list with Back in the fight, said to be Angel's addition): Rhea: "They look good". The mock-ups were pictures sent in the chat, not kept in the repo.
- Settled by Angel, told to Rhea in the release note, hers to change (the list is in `docs/features/speech.md`): the language a monster speaks to the party in (Common when it has it, else the first on its sheet; never chosen by what the party understands); a creature with no language can still yield, without words; the yield's sign by whether it holds something; one yield a fight; a yield is the plan's last step with an action unspent; a yielded monster's turns pass by themselves, ticked or not, Auto or not; Play it myself on its card sets the yield aside and takes its ticks off; Back in the fight on the Prisoners list; a leader that gave itself up counts as fallen for its band; a leader hidden by the GM still posts no line for an order (tier 3b's rule), her "A voice" answer being applied to speech to the party; an order's line for a player who knows the language is the sentence, else the order in names made for players; a leader can order its band to yield (`part: "yield"`).
- Looked into and fixed, not asked: u-81 (two turn requests for one turn, seen once in 0.28.0's test). The cause is in `onTurnChange`: a turn asked for late (a tick during the turn) read the round after its wait, so when the round moved on in that wait and the same creature came up again it made a request stamped with the new round beside that round's own. The round is now read once, and the call gives up when it has moved on. Seen on test build 2: a wolf ticked during its own turn, the tracker stepped six times in under a second to its turn in the next round: one request, for the new round, and none for the old.
- Found by the live test and fixed in test build 2, not asked: a ticked monster's turn could be missed altogether when two turn changes came about 200 ms apart (three of four tries on test build 1, each with AI online as the active GM). The runner now looks again 1.2 s after every turn change. On test build 2: seven tries, none missed, one of them picked up by the second look (the request came 1.4 s after the change instead of 0.7 s).
- Close-out pass (32 open at the start, at least 3 to close), done before the test build on installed 0.28.0: u-82 merged into u-26; u-6 seen as far as a hidden session shows it (the picture is carried on as u-84); u-10 dropped after reading the code again (its guard can't be reached in play). u-81 was then closed on test build 2 (four closed in all).
