## Review
<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
- An independent reviewer (it had not seen the design talk) read the code diff, `docs/features/speech.md`, this record, `speech.mjs` and the new section of `fight.mjs` in full, and about twenty other functions in part; not the unit tests, `docs/features/orders.md` or the rest of `ui/turns.mjs`. Ten findings; each was checked against the code by Angel.
- 1 verified true, fixed: Accepted was three writes and a prisoner still in the tracker was handled nowhere. A prisoner in the tracker now reads as yielded (`yieldOf`), so a cut-off Accepted, or a prisoner the GM puts back in the tracker by hand, has its row and buttons and Accepted finishes the rest; Fight on clears both flags.
- 2 verified true, fixed: a leader that yielded used up its band's fall, so being struck down later did nothing. A fall recorded as "yielded" can now be followed by a real one. The mixed words for a Fights harder band while its leader stands down are left, as an open item.
- 3 verified true, fixed: an order's line for a player who knows the language could carry a kind's name (possibly a hidden creature's). Players' words now say "some of its band", and an order in the GM's own words shows players no words at all.
- 4 verified true, fixed: a yield that ended while `passTurn` waited for the turn to settle left the turn never asked for. It is asked for once the wait is over.
- 5 verified true, fixed: a reload of the runner (or a pause across one) lost the pass timer. `passTurn` starts it again for a turn that is passing or was yielded on.
- 6 verified true, partly fixed: a yield ended on the turn it was made leaves that turn for the GM to end. The card now says so; asking again would need the turn's spent actions carried over. The feature doc's open item says it plainly.
- 7 verified true, fixed in part: Back in the fight now makes the combatant before it clears the flag, so a cut-off press leaves a monster that reads as yielded. Two GMs pressing it in the same instant can still make two combatants: an open item.
