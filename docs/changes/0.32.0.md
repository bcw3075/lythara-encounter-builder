One independent review (an agent that read only the diff, the record and speech.md, with a ceiling), 8 findings, each checked against the code:
1. verified true: a yield read whose turn it ends only after its waits, so a turn ended by hand meanwhile could end the next creature's turn. Fixed: the turn is noted before anything waits, and `endYieldTurn` checks it is still that creature's, once per turn (shared with `passAfter`'s guard).
2. verified true: her words (and the older yielded card's) were turned by the windows' pronoun nets ("Leave {them} be!" read "Leave it be!", and a redraw put that in her draft). Fixed: `literal` for typed words in the log lines, the chat line and an order's sentence; braces as HTML entities in the Monster Turns window; unit-checked.
3. verified true: a reload between the log line and the token's mark left a logged yield that wasn't one. Fixed: the token is marked first, and a press whose mark is already there finishes on the runner's return.
4. verified true: a ticked monster she took with Play it myself, yielded, then a runner reload before its turn ended: the turn sat. Fixed: `passTurn` ends it when her `gave` line is there.
5. verified true: a second press overwrote one still waiting. Fixed: refused ("A line is already waiting to be said."); two GMs in the same instant remain (speech.md, Open items).
6. verified true: her draft was cleared before the line was said, and a press waiting for an absent runner had no way back. Fixed: the draft stays until it is said; Cancel on a waiting press.
7. verified true (minor): a name with braces was read for markers in the box and in `showYielder`'s note. Fixed: names outside the turned text.
8. verified true, not changed: the chat flag's `who` holds a hidden speaker's real name, reachable only through the console (the existing 0.29.0 behaviour; Rhea, 2026-11-11: players' consoles are not a concern). Noted in speech.md, Open items.
