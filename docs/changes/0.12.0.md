# 0.12.0 — Interact and Delay (started 2026-11-08)

## Features touched
- interact-delay (new): the `interact` step (a card for the GM, grabbed's DC 5 flat check, restrained refused) and the `delay` step (the turn ends, the initiative moves to just after the named creature). `docs/features/interact-delay.md`.
- turn-loop: `runTurn` dispatches the two new kinds; `delayed` ends the request without `endAfter`; `gather` adds `grabbed`, `fresh` and `order`; the ask-note words for both.
- brain: prompt version 14 (Interact and Delay paragraphs); `cleanPlan` keeps `what` and `after`.
- history: result lines for Interact (card tagged) and Delay.
- stealth: `actWords` says "its Interact"; a Delay keeps hiding (`KEEPS_HIDDEN`); one line in its doc.
- turn-loop (review fixes): `onTurnChange`'s guard and `endLeftOver` skip `delayed` requests; `endLeftOver` skips `gmEnded` ones (a pre-existing break of Rhea's "never after a card the GM must apply", since 0.9.1); `onRunnerReady` closes a Delay cut off by a reload.
- doc-unchanged take-cover: the diff hunk only sits next to `takeCover` in fight.mjs (the new runners were inserted before `runTakeCover`); no Take Cover code or behaviour changed.

## Decisions made
- Trim the packet first, or go on to step 2? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Skip the trim, start step 2 (Recommended)" (the hello is ~14,600 characters once a chat, an event ~4,600, a fight under 1% of her week; the chat's own history is the cost that grows, not the packet).
- Interact and Delay are Angel's design within the plan's step list (2026-11-08), recorded with reasons in `docs/features/interact-delay.md` Decisions: Interact posts a card for the GM (the module can't know what the hands do); Delay moves the initiative with PF2e's own tie-break after ending the turn (no state to watch, survives a reload); a Delay after a creature that has already acted is refused.
- Other speeds (fly, swim, climb, burrow) are not in this release: their scope is a question for Rhea (swim across the creek maps first, or flying with elevation), asked after this release.

8. GM-hidden allies in `order`: **real, fixed** (`!a.hidden`, as the packet).

11. `endLeftOver` ended a turn whose last step left a card for the GM (abilities since 0.9.1, now Interact), against Rhea's rule: **real, fixed** (`gmEnded` mark, skipped).
12. History merged the delayed turn and the returned one: **real, fixed** (keyed apart, "(back after delaying)", the delayed-on turn placed by its `turn` index; unit test).

## Learned, and where it was filed
- After an initiative change that follows `nextTurn` on the runner, the server kept the turn number `nextTurn` wrote, which named the wrong creature after the re-sort: save the pointer yourself with `diff: false` (`runDelay`): `docs/features/interact-delay.md` Traps, code comment.
- PF2e HUD has its own Delay (hourglass, flag `pf2e-hud.delayed`, second press re-places after the current combatant and calls `nextTurn`, nothing when the order wouldn't change): `docs/features/interact-delay.md` Decisions; for the operator docs `claude/foundry-s8-hud.md`.
- Requests keyed by round and combatant break when one creature has two turns in a round; mark the request (`delayed`/`afterDelay`): Traps.
- `endLeftOver` ended turns whose last step left a GM card (since 0.9.1): `gmEnded`, Traps and turn-loop doc.
- Wrapping a macro that writes `window.__smoke` in your own `window.__smoke = ...` loses its result: run it as the smoke-test doc says (no lesson for the repo).
