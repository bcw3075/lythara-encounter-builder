## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- Should Auto end a turn while the game is paused? Rhea, 2026-11-08: "Pause would pause all actions." Built as: while paused, Auto neither starts an action nor ends a turn, and resumes on unpause; the GM's own presses still act.
- The ten fleeing decisions (Rhea, 2026-11-08, designed one question at a time) are recorded in `docs/features/fleeing.md`; this release builds them as designed, with these build choices within them: the ring is read from the scene's current bounds; a flee must be the plan's last step; Extend adds 10 squares (the Survey Sheet's default) and is greyed at the 120-square limit, with the Survey Sheet off, or off an encounter map; the Extend press goes to the runner through the request flag; Bring back rolls initiative with `combat.rollInitiative`; the XP not earned is PF2e's `calculateXP` for one creature at the party's level and size.

2. Escaped or GM-hidden ally in every ally's packet with name, square and health (`buildPacket`): **real, fixed** (`!a.hidden`, as `knownTo` already did). A pre-existing leak the feature made frequent.
3. `afterExtend` went back to `fled` before `replan` closed the request, so Let it get away could orphan a fresh request: **real, fixed** (stays `extending`).
4. A Survey Sheet timeout offered Extend again while its drawing went on, so a second press grew the map twice: **real, fixed** (180 s, and a timeout carries on as finished).
5. A `fled` request whose turn moved on without a press stayed `fled` for ever: **real, fixed** (`leaveWaiting` closes it with a note).
6. Two GMs pressing Let it get away together could call `nextTurn` twice: **real, narrowed** (button disabled on press; the escape record is written first and a second press finds it). Routing it through the runner would remove it entirely; not done.
7. After a north or west extension, remembered squares (`turns.memory`) and the token's movement history were in old coordinates: **real, fixed** (shifted by the Survey Sheet's returned pixel shift; history cleared if the shift was recorded). Live check in fl-5.
8. The 60 s machine stall fired during a legitimate extension: **real, fixed** (240 s for `extending`).
9. A second escape of the same monster erased its returned record: **real, fixed** (records keyed by request id).
10. `extend` left set when the state changed under it: **real, fixed**.
11. Escaped tokens of an earlier fight listed in a new one; after End Encounter they stay hidden with Bring back unreachable: **first part fixed** (flag names its combat); the second is an open item for Rhea.
12. With the press waiting on the runner the card had no buttons but still asked the GM to choose: **real, fixed** (new words; Let it get away kept).
13. Wording: "she" → "the GM" in the public prompt; `routeWords` "Flee"; `letGo` overwrote an earlier note (now appends); a monster already on the ring must move to another ring square (prompt says so); Bring back's initiative roll posts a chat card (doc says so): **all taken**.
Unit test gaps it named (Large flee, already on the ring, no actions, extended line, latest note, returned ordering, extending stall and busy): added; 1085 pass.
