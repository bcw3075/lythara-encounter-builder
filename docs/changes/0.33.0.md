# 0.33.0 — Monster minds release 4: the fight-start look and the escaped monster's knows lines (started 2026-11-13)

Release 4 of monster minds, built straight from the approved samples: when a fight starts Angel looks at it from what the monsters perceive and suggests changes to their minds on a card on Monster Turns (each Accept / Keep, Accept all / Keep all, lasting this fight only; it waits until round 1 ends, and says when it didn't happen or the route is off); at End Encounter each monster that got away offers a few sure lines of what it saw of its enemies for its own "Knows about its enemies", each Accept / Keep, standing whether the history is saved or discarded. With it, bridge 0.4.1, whose API worker answers the look. Its truth is `docs/features/look.md`.
