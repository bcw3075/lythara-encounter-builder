## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- **What this release is (Rhea, 2026-11-16, q-39: "Five releases (Recommended)"):** release 5 of the full reactions pass: Moments G (a monster moving near someone), H (a condition gained), I (a monster beginning a reaction) and the Track list on her card (the approved sample 6), with the one-line fix for her card's wording of a Moment A to C row and a close-out pass (24 open).
- **How the rows were checked (Rhea, 2026-11-16, standing for the pass: "Compendium first (Recommended)"):** the four texts (Everdistant Defense, Zephyr Slip, Comforting Presence, Miraculous Intervention) and the thirty Track names were read from `pf2e.feats-srd`, `pf2e.spells-srd` and `pf2e.actionspf2e` in PF2 Test on 2026-11-17 in one script (slug, action type, level, traits, source and text); all were found, none is legacy, so Archives of Nethys wasn't needed.
- **Corrections to the design (plan/reactions-pass-design.md):** Miraculous Intervention answers only an adjacent **fiend or undead** beginning a reaction (Knights of Lastwall; the design didn't name the creature types); Everdistant Defense also answers **a ranged attack at a creature in the aura** (Pathfinder #168), so it is asked at the attack moment too, and it needs an aura from a champion feat or feature; Zephyr Slip's trigger is a creature **entering** a space within 5 feet (Rage of Elements), so the last square of a move counts, which no reach gate asks; Comforting Presence lowers **every** adjacent ally's frightened and stupefied, not only the one who became so (Season of Ghosts).
- **Settled by Angel in the build, hers to change (in reactions.md, RX-6 and the line after it):** Comforting Presence's "by the same amount" read as 1 for each kind lowered; it answers a condition created during a monster's turn the module runs; Everdistant Defense on a move stops the move and the turn for her ruling (its triple cost isn't worked out), and Do it re-plans from where the monster stands; Miraculous Intervention reads the reactor's attack roll made while its question is open, and with none seen her card asks her to rule; the monster's AC on her card only.
- **The close-out pass (24 open at the start):** u-134 and u-136 dropped (known limits listed in reactions.md's Open items, not behaviour left unproven); u-132 (Shield Block against a monster's Reactive Strike) tested live with Moment I.
- **Her card's wording of a Moment A to C row (the 0.38.0 open item):** `rx3Facts` now gives `rxWords` the reactor's name for her card ("Dire Wolf attacks Test Hero."); the player's own window still says "you".
