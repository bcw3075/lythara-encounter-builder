## Decisions made
- **The reaction timer (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked what range the world setting allows and whether 0 means no countdown, she chose "Option one, but only if a player has the right feat to get a reaction": 5 to 60 seconds, default 20, 0 = no countdown (the turn waits for the player or the GM's Continue); and a player is prompted only when their character has a reaction that fits the trigger. Rejected: 5 to 60 always timed; no timer at all.
- **Scope (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked whether to build player reactions as one release or two, she chose "Two: players first": 0.20.0 is players reacting during a monster's turn (every gate: leaving reach, a move that doesn't leave a square, manipulate and ranged, before an attack, on a hit before damage); monsters reacting on a player's turn is the next release. Rejected: both halves in one release.
