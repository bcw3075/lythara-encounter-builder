## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- B-11 (u-149; Rhea ruled 2026-11-18: not by design, "the leader takes back over"; it replaces 0.28.0's "the one who takes over leads for the rest of the fight"): a leader that gets back up, or returns from a side change or a yield, leads again and the heir hands the band back; the packets stop saying the leader is down. Built so: `orig` (the leader the module acted on the fall of, written with the fall, never an heir) is kept on the Combat's `bands` flag; `leaderOf` given `standing` returns the original instead of the heir while it is standing, so packets and orders read it at once; at the next turn change `settleBandsNow` forgets the fall (`leader`, `fell`, `orig`), sets `turns.leads` and writes a `leaderBack` band-log line, so a second fall is acted on afresh. A fight begun before 0.39.7 has no `orig`: its heir keeps leading. Falls apart, Fights harder, Plan stands and Breaks and runs have no heir to hand back: their packets stop saying the leader is down at once, but what the module changed (plan gone, objective) stays, and a second fall of that leader isn't acted on again (told to Rhea as an open item in orders.md; hers to rule).
