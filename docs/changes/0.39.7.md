## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- B-11 (u-149; Rhea ruled 2026-11-18: not by design, "the leader takes back over"; it replaces 0.28.0's "the one who takes over leads for the rest of the fight"): a leader that gets back up, or returns from a side change or a yield, leads again and the heir hands the band back; the packets stop saying the leader is down. Built so: `orig` (the leader the module acted on the fall of, written with the fall, never an heir) is kept on the Combat's `bands` flag; `leaderOf` given `standing` returns the original instead of the heir while it is standing, so packets and orders read it at once; at the next turn change `settleBandsNow` forgets the fall (`leader`, `fell`, `orig`), sets `turns.leads` and writes a `leaderBack` band-log line, so a second fall is acted on afresh. A fight begun before 0.39.7 has no `orig`: its heir keeps leading. Falls apart, Fights harder, Plan stands and Breaks and runs have no heir to hand back: their packets stop saying the leader is down at once, but what the module changed (plan gone, objective) stays, and a second fall of that leader isn't acted on again (told to Rhea as an open item in orders.md; hers to rule).

- Independent review (a fresh agent, reading only the diff with wide context, the touched functions and the feature docs; ceiling about 40,000 tokens, it used about 140,000: it read that one file only and reported what it couldn't prove as "to check"). Findings and what was done, each checked against the code: (1) `checkSidesNow` kept writing the heir into `turns.leads` while the leader stood, and a pending `turned` for the heir could later be acted on as a leader's fall: true; `checkSidesNow` now passes `standing`, and the hand-back drops a `turned` for the heir. (2) The hand-back didn't reset `orders.last` as a takeover does: true; reset, so the leader may order that round too. (3) `orig` and `fell` stayed stale when the heir had left the map and the leader was cleared with nobody to lead: true; the hand-back now also fires when `fell.pick` is next with `orig` standing, though no heir is stored. (4) packet and module reading "standing" differently: not so, `bandPacket` is passed `fellWord`, the same as the module. (5) `bandPacket`'s default `fallen` (never fallen): only a caller that omits it, and none does; the Monster Minds window and the History read the heir until the hand-back at the next turn change (a documented limit). (6) a hidden leader: the hand-back ignored hiding: fixed, `leadsAgain` also needs the leader shown (a hidden leader is never acted on). (7) the unit tests only cover the pure rule and the words, not the wiring: true, fight.mjs needs Foundry; the wiring is covered by od-12 live. (8) `leaderBack` in the History: the History builds a line for every kind except others' orders (read in `historyOf`); checked live in od-12.
