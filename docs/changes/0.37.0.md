- **How the rows are checked (Rhea, 2026-11-16, asked at the start of this release: "Compendium first (Recommended)"):** asked whether to check every row by an Archives of Nethys search (about 10,000 tokens each), read all the rows' texts from PF2e's own compendium in PF2 Test in one script (the same Paizo text) and check on Archives of Nethys only those that look odd, legacy or missing, or split the release, she chose the compendium first. So: 28 rows read from `pf2e.feats-srd` and `pf2e.spells-srd` on 2026-11-16 (traits, level, traditions, range, targets, duration, defense and text); the four legacy texts (Sacrifice Armor, Advanced Player's Guide pg. 120; Preemptive Reconfiguration, Ancestry Guide pg. 77, an aphorite feat that PF2e's compendium lists as nephilim; Rebounding Barrier, Legends pg. 37, rare; Overselling Flourish, Firebrands pg. 90, uncommon) and the one missing name (Whisper of Warning, War of Immortals pg. 26; PF2e's compendium calls it Whispers of Warning) checked on Archives of Nethys the same day. Rejected: Archives of Nethys for every row; splitting the release.
- **Where the rules texts differ from the design's rows (read 2026-11-16, Angel's corrections, told to Rhea):** Curse of Recoil answers a ranged attack only, at anyone (an enemy the caster sees, 120 ft), not an attack at the caster; Cloud Dragon's Cloak answers a ranged attack at the caster or an ally within 60 ft and the target stays hidden from ranged attacks for 1 round; Anchoring Air answers a melee Strike at the caster; Vanish Weapon answers a melee weapon Strike that hits or misses (not a critical either way), so it is asked after the roll, not only on a hit; Eat Fire is a cantrip on the arcane, occult and primal lists (the design said not arcane); Brine Dragon Bile is spat at the creature that was wounded, so it answers the party wounding a monster, not a monster's damage: it moves to the Track list; Blood Vendetta answers piercing, slashing or bleed damage to the caster; Blazing Spirit's attacker also takes persistent fire; Calcifying Sand needs an unarmed or non-reach melee attack and has the incapacitation trait; Scintillating Safeguard protects up to 5 creatures within 30 ft; Deflecting Wave needs the reactor not off-guard against it.

## Review
<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
An independent agent read the diff of scripts/, this record and the 0.37.0 parts of reactions.md (12 findings, 2026-11-16). Each checked against the code:
- 1 verified true: `runSpell` rolled a spell attack's damage after Whisper of Warning made it a miss. Fixed: `missed` continues, as in `runStrike`.
- 2 verified true: a monster's Reactive Strike (`runReaction`) ignored "paused" and "ask", and a hit's Pause row ended it as done with no damage. Fixed: rows that stop the turn aren't asked on a monster's reaction request (before the roll and on a hit), and a reload's "ask" ends it with a note.
- 3 verified true: `hitGate`'s Moment B loop could reroll Whisper of Warning and add Hidebound twice after a reload. Fixed: `rxHitStarted` saved before the loop; a reload in it leaves the rest to the GM with a note.
- 4 verified true: Calcifying Sand's hour per attacker was never checked by `fits` (no `freq`). Fixed: `perAttacker: 3600` and the check; unit test added.
- 5 verified true: with automatic damage off, `harmByHand`'s hits carried no damage types or Strike facts. Fixed on all four calls.
- 6 verified true: a reload in `beforeRx` lost the flat check. Fixed: the flat DC is saved with the first `started` record and kept.
- 7 verified true: `combat.previous` was read after awaits. Fixed: read in the `updateCombat` handler and passed to `turnReactions`.
- 8 verified true: the follow-up ability's critical damage path said melee Strike. Fixed: `attack: true, strike: false`.
- 9 verified true (a wrong note, not a bug): the path that got `less` is a follow-up ability's damage, and a monster's Reactive Strike already went through `applyFromCard`. The open item's sentence is corrected; nothing counted twice.
- 10 half verified: Anchoring Air's ChoiceSet values are "success" and "failure" (read from the compendium 2026-11-16); whether Curse of Recoil's critical-failure effect grants Off-Guard is checked in the live test.
- 11 verified true (rules): after a repentance the damage-triggered rows still struck back. Fixed: skipped when the monster repented, and no "takes 0" line (Volcanic Escape and Pyrefowl Rebuke stay: the player already acted).
- 12 verified true: Scintillating Safeguard could take enemies caught in an area and an arbitrary order. Fixed: the caster's side only, the caster first.
- Design point verified true: Shield of Reckoning ran Glimpse of Redemption for any champion. Fixed: asked only of a champion with Glimpse of Redemption (the party's champion's cause); noted in Open items.
