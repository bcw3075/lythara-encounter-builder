## Decisions made
<!-- Rhea's answers, each with the question it answered and the date. -->
- **Who decides a monster's reaction (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked who decides when a player's action gives a monster a Reactive Strike, she chose "1": a monster with Angel and Auto ticked takes it by itself at once; any other monster shows Do it / Skip on the Monster Turns card. Rejected: always the GM's click; the thinker deciding each time (seconds added to a player's turn and usage spent on a choice that is nearly always yes).
- **Which player actions trigger it (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked whether this release covers moves only, moves with ranged attacks and spells, or everything, she chose the third: "basically it should be anything that the rules state". So every trigger in the rule counts (a manipulate action, a move action, a ranged attack, leaving a square during a move action), not a subset. Rejected: moves only; moves, ranged attacks and spells only.
- **Spells (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** "It's not all spells, it's only spells that have the manipulate tag": a spell counts through its own traits (manipulate, or a ranged attack), never for being a spell.
- **Unsure triggers (Rhea, 2026-11-09):** asked whether an Auto monster should strike by itself when the module isn't sure a trigger happened (an item changing hands with no card, Prone clicked off, a one-square move that may be a Step), she chose "1": unsure cases always come to her card with the reason and Do it / Skip, even for Auto monsters; and "for cases where you are unsure, we can later build a button for the players to click to do these actions" (a later release; Open items). Rejected: Auto monsters striking on anything that looks like a trigger.

An independent agent read the diff, the record and the feature doc (2026-11-09 night; no Foundry: reasoned from the code, unit tests run). Each finding checked against the code before acting:
- 1 (bug) verified true: two Strikes at once share the one `pending` waiter (two monsters reacting to one move; a reaction during a turn's action). Fixed: `doReaction` queues reactions behind anything running, `doIt` waits for a reaction in flight.
- 2 (bug) verified true: the history rendered a non-Auto reaction as one "played by the GM" line, dropping its Strike, and marked it "(now)" on the monster's own turn. Fixed (`angel: true`, no combatant id) with a unit test.
- 3 (risk) verified true: two triggers in the same moment could write two offers for one monster. Fixed with an in-memory mark (`offering`).
- 4 (risk) plausible, not yet seen live: a consumable only shown in chat carries the same origin as one used. Fixed by reading the card's markup (`readCard().itemCard`); to be seen live.
- 5 (risk) verified true: Visioner alone answers "observed" for a pair with no record. Fixed: the monster's sight comes from `sense`, as on its turns.
- 6 (risk) verified true: a GM-hidden monster could react in the open. Fixed.
- 7 (risk) verified true: an item never seen got a guessed "before", and any `system.equipped` key (invested) counted. Fixed: only carry type or hands, never guessed; everyone's items noted at each turn change.
- 8 (risk) verified true in part: a check card's action is now read from its `action:` option as well as the title; the parsing moved to the pure `readCard` with unit tests on PF2e's real Interact card.
- 9 (risk) not verified either way (no live spell attack roll read yet): a spell attack now counts as ranged unless the spell's range is touch; to be seen live.
- 10 (risk) verified true: a Strike that never rolled still spent the reaction. Fixed.
- 11 (nit) true: Nimble Dodge isn't asked on a reaction's Strike, a hidden monster isn't brought out, Take Cover isn't ended, no amber. Recorded as Open items, not built.
- 12 (nit) true: a downed creature dragged past a monster offered a Strike: fixed. Keyboard moves and rerolls re-offering after a Skip: Open items.
- 13 (nit) true: a Tiny token's square was rounded, not floored. Fixed.
- 14 (nit) true: the doc's line on variants and on the history corrected; the front matter lists files a feature owns whole, so function-owned files stay out of it (false as a finding).
Not checkable without Foundry, so left for the live round: the real shape of PF2e's check and consume cards, whether `moveToken` comes once per drag, a melee roll at a target already out of reach.
