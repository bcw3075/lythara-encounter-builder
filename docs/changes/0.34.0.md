<!-- The independent reviewer's findings, each marked verified true or false and what was done.
     Required when code changed; otherwise write `waived by Rhea <date>` with her reason. -->
An independent agent read the diff, the decisions and the changed functions (no live run). 12 findings, each checked against the code:
- 1 (medium) verified true: a player's Steps answer and the GM's Do it in the same moment could strike and then mark the offer as Steps. Fixed: `stepsSettling` holds a Do it on that offer while the answer is written, and it does nothing if the offer was settled.
- 2 (medium) verified true: the window named monsters by their token's name setting only, so a monster the character doesn't perceive (Visioner hidden) could be named. Fixed: `seenBy` (Visioner's record from the mover, else seen); unperceived ones are "a creature".
- 3 (low) verified true: "Two Steps" was the default button, so a stray Enter or Space could answer it. Fixed: no default button.
- 4 (low) verified true: (a) a script's one-square move on its own turn became no trigger (before 0.34.0 Not certain); (b) a script's two-square move kept the Steps reason. Fixed: `script` in `moveTrigger`: a script's walk of up to three squares is Not certain with the script reason and asks nobody.
- 5 (low) verified true: with the timer at 0 the window stayed open until the turn changed, also after the GM answered every offer; "No answer: the GM decides" was wrong with no timer; a mover gone left "Asking" on the card. Fixed: a poll ends the question on the timer, when the GM has answered every offer, or when the fight is gone; the words read "If you don't answer, the GM decides."; the ask is cleared with the mover gone.
- 6 (low) verified true, partly changed: the module counts no party member's actions, so "three Steps" can be claimed with fewer left. The note now says how many actions the Steps took ("two of Test Hero's actions"); an Open item in the feature doc.
- 7 (low, uncertain) to check live: whether `system.movement.speeds.land.value` includes reductions (see the checks).
- 8 (low, uncertain) verified as a limit, not changed: difficult terrain is read from `modifyMovementCost` regions only, which is how PF2e 8.7.0 on Foundry 14 and Survey Sheet make it; an Open item.
- 9 (low) verified true: whose turn it was for Prone coming off was read 1.5 s later. Fixed: read at the moment it came off.
- 10 (low) verified true: "Strode" capitalised mid-sentence and "where a Step can't go in its reach"; duplicate names merged, "A creature" capitalised mid-list. Fixed: lower-case kinds ("strode into difficult terrain (a Step can't go there)"), names counted ("two Goblin Warriors", "a creature" lower case within the list). The 10 ft figure stays: it is what the Steps would be.
- 11 verified fine (the sender check is the same as the existing answers').
- 12 verified true: unit tests added for the question's names, the script case and the new words; the runner's question code is covered by the live checks r-37 and r-9.
